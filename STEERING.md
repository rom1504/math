## Leading sufficient lemma and convergence chain

Fix a compact window
$[p_0,p_1]\subset(1/\sqrt2,1)$, $m/n$ in that window, and
$c\in(0,1/4)$. For every relevant target pair, find an oriented cut $d$ with

~~~math
R_2(d)=O(n^{9/4-c}),\qquad
U_m\{S:\widehat\ell(S,d)\le O(n^{3/2-c})\}
\ge\exp\{-O(n^{3/4-c})\}.
~~~

This is (10.795). The inverse tail (10.1023) gives

~~~math
q_m\le(m/n)^{3/2}q_n+O(n^{3/2-c}).
~~~

Geometric-window summability and exact landing then force $q_n/n^{3/2}$,
and hence $M_n/n^{3/2}$, to converge.

The leading bare sufficient lemma remains

~~~math
\min_{d:U_m(\mathcal I_d)>0}
\left\{-\log U_m(\mathcal I_d)+n^{-3/2}R_2(d)\right\}
=O(n^{3/4-c}),\qquad
\mathcal I_d=\{S:\langle B_S,d\rangle\ge q_n\}.
~~~

A structured fixed-density implementation fixes noncentral switching ratio
$\kappa$, small $b_\kappa>0$, and a scalar optimizer at
$\lambda=b_\kappa n^{-3/2}$, and proves

~~~math
-\log U_m(\mathcal I_d)=O(H),\qquad
\Pr_{S\mid\mathcal I_d}
\{\theta_\kappa\Gamma_S>(1-\theta_\kappa)q_n\}
\ge e^{-O(H)},\quad H=n^{3/4-c}.
~~~

Then (10.1188)--(10.1191) give the row bound and hence (10.795). Both claims,
especially mass, remain open at fixed density.

## Evidence, obstructions, and falsification criteria

- Average complement incidence has the no-saving exponent
  $e^{-\Theta(n^{3/4})}$. Pair marginals, product tilts, priorities,
  conflict, decoding, and local switching do not locate the rare column.
- Geometric block Harnack is finitely false on every layer tested. Arithmetic
  retention tolerates empty orbit points but requires active slack of order
  $q_n$ and separately requires scalar-column mass.
- If $T=S^c$, every active slack obeys
  $\Gamma_S\le2Q(A[T])\le2|T|(|T|-1)$. Thus fixed-strength high slack is
  empty for $|T|=o(n^{3/4})$ and identically zero at $m=n-1$, even though
  nonempty columns there have polynomial mass.
- The fixed-density complement implementation is falsified by an unbounded
  exact-minimizer family for which every scalar optimizer with project mass
  has conditional high-slack fraction $e^{-\omega(H)}$. The bare route is
  falsified only if every eligible arbitrary cut fails the mass/row pair.
- Small slack cannot be turned into an improving signing by convexification.
  The exact integral replacement game is protected by adaptive extremal-state
  migration, while its fractional relaxation can have margin $q_n/4$.
- Hard extraction is
  $\max_z a_z\ge1-\epsilon_\ell/(1-\lambda_\ell)$. Uniform centers exceed
  the threshold on exact $A_8,A_9$ nearest-core examples. Powers, mixtures,
  generic mass, and law-free coarea are retired.
- Incidence size bias changes the exact hard target to
  $\mathbb E[a_zB_z]\le(1-\lambda)(1-\eta)\mathbb E[a_z^2]$.
  It repairs some finite failures but is not known to retain project-row mass.
  A project-row family with normalized ratio at least $1-o(e^{-CL_0})$
  falsifies this implementation.
- Tight decomposition has no counterexample among all 7,473 exact minimizers
  through order eight; one parent even works separately at every size.
  Nevertheless 840 order-seven and 2,520 order-eight representatives have no
  nested chain, and fixed-parent greedoid exchange fails from order five.
- The one-block fractional dual admits an abstract antipodal active law with
  no child ground, while every Boolean block game still holds. The exact
  conjecture can only be proved by joint constraints across overlapping
  completion profiles. A positive gap in (10.1203) is its exact falsifier.
- Strict pressure, high replicas, local harmonic Poincaré, monotone deletion,
  and constant shortfall without a square-root cavity reward remain retired.

## Most recent blank-slate abstraction audit

This Wave 45 audit began from the original problem before route comparison.
Its candidates and judgments are agent-authored hypotheses, not user
directives. This early refresh does not regenerate it; Wave 53 will.

1. **Finite-temperature zero-sum interpolation.** Balanced $o(n)$ additivity
   of the soft minimax free energy would imply convergence. Existing
   temperature-mismatch, oscillatory-pressure, and overlap-geometry walls
   reject it without a signing-specific bridge anti-alignment theorem.
2. **Summable cavity derivative.** An extension error
   $O(n^{1/2-\delta})$ would make normalized positive variation summable.
   This is the old cavity/constant-shortfall route; its square-root reward is
   open, so the reformulation is rejected.
3. **Second-order signed-kernel compactness.** Continuity of the Boolean
   quadratic supremum plus all-order recovery would prove convergence.
   Existing graphon, traffic, purification, and action-profile attempts miss
   planted Boolean directions or recovery. Keep dormant until a concrete
   recovery operation exists.

None supplies a stronger or more testable mechanism at the required scale.
