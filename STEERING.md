## Leading sufficient lemma and convergence chain

Fix a high-ratio window $m/n\in[\rho,1)$ and $c\in(0,1/4)$. For every
relevant target pair, choose an exact-minimizer parent signing and one oriented
parent cut $d$ such that

~~~math
R_2(d)=O(n^{9/4-c}),\qquad
U_m\{S:\widehat\ell(S,d)\le O(n^{3/2-c})\}
\ge\exp\{-O(n^{3/4-c})\}.
~~~

This is (10.795). The row-sensitive inverse tail (10.1023) gives

~~~math
q_m\le(m/n)^{3/2}q_n+O(n^{3/2-c}).
~~~

Geometric-window summability and exact landing then force $q_n/n^{3/2}$,
and hence $M_n/n^{3/2}$, to converge.

The leading bare sufficient lemma remains

~~~math
\min_{d:U_m(\mathcal I_d)>0}
\left\{-\log U_m(\mathcal I_d)+n^{-3/2}R_2(d)\right\}
=O(n^{3/4-c}),\qquad
\mathcal I_d=\{S:\langle B_S,d\rangle\ge q_n\}.
~~~

Every $S\in\mathcal I_d$ is favorable by (10.1045). A structured complement
lemma would fix noncentral $\kappa$, small $b_\kappa>0$, and a scalar
optimizer at $\lambda=b_\kappa n^{-3/2}$, and prove both

~~~math
-\log U_m(\mathcal I_d)=O(H),\qquad
\Pr_{S\mid\mathcal I_d}
\{\theta_\kappa\Gamma_S>(1-\theta_\kappa)q_n\}
\ge e^{-O(H)},\quad H=n^{3/4-c}.
~~~

Then (10.1188)--(10.1191) give the row bound and hence (10.795). The two
displayed claims, especially the mass claim, remain open.

## Evidence, obstructions, and falsification criteria

- Average complement incidence has the no-saving exponent
  $\exp\{-\Theta(n^{3/4})\}$, so a project-scale partition theorem is
  impossible. Pair marginals, product tilts, shared priorities, conflict,
  decoding, and local switching do not locate the rare column.
- Every nontrivial block layer of every stored $A_8,A_9$ scalar Pareto column
  contains an empty column. Thus logarithmic/geometric Harnack is retired.
  The arithmetic theorem tolerates empties, but its high-slack event is empty
  on those finite examples. No scalable obstruction is known.
- The central entropy wall extends to
  $|k-n/2|=o(n^{7/8})$; mean survival requires the much more noncentral
  $\theta>1/3$. A viable switching proof must genuinely exploit active slack.
- The complement implementation is falsified by an unbounded exact-minimizer
  family for which every scalar optimizer with project-scale mass has
  conditional high-slack fraction $e^{-\omega(H)}$. The bare route is
  falsified only if every eligible arbitrary cut fails the mass/row pair.
- For the hard route the exact extraction is
  $\max_z a_z\ge1-\epsilon_\ell/(1-\lambda_\ell)$.
  The target is $\epsilon_\ell\le(1-\lambda_\ell)(1-e^{-O(L_0)})$, or the
  integrated coarea inequality (10.1198). Powers and nonnegative mixtures
  cannot improve the normalized statistic.
- A fixed-coordinate slice cylinder has the desired $e^{-\Theta(L_0)}$ mass
  but negative near-core excess. Generic concentration, mass, and low
  description complexity are therefore insufficient. A scalable
  exact-minimizer family with the same normalized escape falsifies this hard
  implementation.
- Tight principal decomposition says a parent ground restricts to a ground
  child on some maximal-principal-norm selector. All 7,473 exact minimizers
  through order eight and the stored order-nine minimizer pass. The exact
  falsifier is a positive gap in (10.1203); minimality currently gives only
  the block game (10.1204), not the needed simultaneous separation.
- Even tight decomposition would only remove tropical interior facets.
  Posterior exclusion charges low crossing endpoints but not high-side
  reverse fan-in. Weighted overlap $D/\eta$, zero leading slope, restoring,
  orientation, and selector transport remain open.
- Strict positive pressure, high replicas, local harmonic Poincaré,
  monotone deletion, and constant-shortfall without a square-root cavity
  reward remain retired.
