F(15): what has been computed, 2026-08-06
==========================================

Notation.  T_n(t) = the set of order-n sign matrices with M <= t, one
representative per switching-and-permutation class, stored as a graph on n-1
vertices in graph6 (last row of the matrix switched to all +1).

Parity.  M = C(n,2) mod 2, so for a target of 21 the effective per-level
thresholds are 20 at orders 12 and 13 and 21 at order 14; for a target of 23
they are 22, 22, 23; for a target of 25 they are 24, 24, 25.

Method.  T_{n+1}(t) is exactly the set of one-vertex extensions of T_n(t): fix
x_{n+1} = +1, then E(x) = E_B(x) + <v,x> for the new sign row v, so one
(2^n x 2^n) block gives M for all 2^n rows at once.  v and -v give the same
class, so only half the rows are enumerated.  Extensions are canonically
labelled with nauty labelg and deduplicated.

Validation of the expansion step, against complete geng enumerations:
  expand(T_10(17)) deduped = T_11(17)   both 20 graphs
  expand(T_11(20)) deduped = T_12(20)   both 2220 graphs

Chain for the bound F(15) >= 23
-------------------------------
  |T_12(20)| = 2220        complete geng pass, 1018997864 graphs on 11 vertices
  |T_13(20)| = 1           the order-14 Paley conference matrix minus a vertex
  |T_14(21)| = 1           the order-14 Paley conference matrix
  |T_15(21)| = 0           no order-15 matrix has M <= 21
Parity at order 15 (C(15,2) = 105 odd) then gives F(15) >= 23.

Chain for the bound F(15) >= 25
-------------------------------
  |T_12(22)| = 3396332     complete geng pass, 1018997864 graphs on 11 vertices
  |T_13(22)| = 338737      from 4081150 raw extensions
  |T_14(23)| = 4           one with M = 21, three with M = 23
  |T_15(23)| = 0           no order-15 matrix has M <= 23
Parity then gives F(15) >= 25.

Upper bound
-----------
  F(15) <= 27.  Extend the order-14 Paley conference matrix by the best of its
  2^14 sign rows; M = 27, recomputed from scratch.  Stored in seed15.npy.
  Weaker candidates checked: order-18 Paley minus three vertices gives 29;
  the Seidel matrices of T(6), GQ(2,2) and the Clebsch two-graph give 35.
  Simulated annealing at order 15 does not get below 29, and the same annealer
  only reaches 25 at order 14 where the true value is 21, so local search is
  not a usable source of upper bounds at this size.

Conclusion so far:  F(15) is 25 or 27.

Sizing of the threshold-25 tower, which decides between them
------------------------------------------------------------
  |T_12(24)| = 82502142    complete geng pass, exact
  raw extensions per order-12 source at threshold 25 = 41.5
      (20000 uniform random sources out of the 82502142)
  so raw level 13 = 3.4e9 records, |T_13(24)| about 2.9e8
  raw extensions per order-13 source at threshold 25 = 0.064
      (29980 sources)
  so raw level 14 = 1.8e7 records, |T_14(25)| about 1.4e6
  level 15 is then 1.4e6 expansions, minutes

  Per-source cost of expand_prune under 16-way contention:
      order 12 source 0.86 ms, order 13 source 2.57 ms, order 14 source ~4 ms
  labelg canonical labelling of 12-vertex graphs: 1.3e6 per second per core

  Wall clock on 16 cores:
      level 12->13   1.2 h per pass, 8 passes to keep the sort inside the free
                     disk, so about 10 h
      level 13->14   about 13 h
      level 14->15   about 6 min
      total about 24 h, peak disk about 15 GB, final level-13 file 3.8 GB
