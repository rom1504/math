# A weaker balanced-residual route, superseded before promotion

2026-09-07. This records an independent first-principles route to showing
that one center's overlap-squared energy envelope cannot cover the whole
cube. Root reports a stronger constructive-track balanced WIDTH theorem;
that stronger proof is the live audit target. The argument here was not
used to claim a recurrence or a width constant.

For even n and Q(A)<=C n^(3/2), switch any chosen center to one and put
P=I-J/n, B=PAP. Cube-extension polarization gives beta(A)<=4Q(A), hence
||A1||^2<=4C n^(3/2)(n-1). It follows that

    ||B||F^2>=n(n-1)-8C n^(3/2),
    max_i ||B_i||2<=sqrt(n-1)+2sqrt(C)n^(1/4).

Thus sum_i||B_i||2>=(1-o(1))n^(3/2). A uniform balanced sign y can be
generated by a uniform perfect matching and independent opposite signs
on its pairs. For zero-sum b, E(b*y)^2=n||b||^2/(n-1); conditional matching
variance is at most 2||b||^2, and its Rademacher fourth moment is at most
three times its variance squared. Consequently E|b*y|>=||b||/sqrt(6).

For a zero-sum vector z, maximizing x*z over balanced Boolean x gives
min_t sum|z_i-t|, which is at least half sum|z_i|. Therefore balanced
bilinear norm is at least (1/(2sqrt(6))-o(1))n^(3/2).

If u is fractional balanced with coordinates in [-1,1], randomly round
two fractional coordinates at a time, keeping their sum and each mean.
For one step the expected change of H_A is -A_ij times the step variance;
the gain in squared Euclidean norm is twice that variance. Total expected
energy error is at most n/2. Thus fractional balanced energy is bounded
by balanced Boolean cap plus n/2. Polarization with (x+y)/2,(x-y)/2 then
gives balanced bilinear norm <=4 balanced_cap+2n.

This yields the weaker balanced cap lower bound
(1/(8sqrt(6))-o(1))n^(3/2), uniformly in the center. For odd n, deleting
one vertex and restoring it loses only O(n) and gives overlap magnitude
1/n. This does not establish the stronger width statement needed to
compare arbitrary child polarities; the constructive track's direct
greedy width argument is preferable and is being audited separately.
