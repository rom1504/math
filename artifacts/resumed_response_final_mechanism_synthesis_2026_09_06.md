## 3. Mixed-charge feedback is an actual new mechanism

The physical update u_next=sign(Bu+alpha u) feeds back the WHOLE previous
endpoint. Its finite-depth law is given by exact conditional Haar
query/response pairs: the unexplored residual produces a fresh Gaussian,
and the known span is acted on by a swap. See
`resumed_response_mixed_charge_involution_feedback_2026_09_06.md` and
the independent proof
`resumed_convergence_haar_adaptive_query_kernel_2026_09_06.md`.

Already at two updates, its charge-odd half D_2 is not measurable in the
complete old canonical Gaussian frame. At alpha_0=alpha_1=1/2, the exact

1/102400. The proof is a conditional Bernoulli variance and a simple
Gaussian rectangle; the independently byte-replayed exact certificate is
`computations/results/resumed_response_mixed_charge_distance_certificate_2026_09_06.json`.
The new information is a response fluctuation correlated with the
already observed field. Replacing it by an unrelated old even gate with
the same marginal distribution destroys precisely that correlation.

are NOT a certified asymptotic crossing of .45, and neither is a

## 4. What genuinely iterates, and why the increments need not stay large

After any nondegenerate Boolean query with limiting field k+sigma N,
the actual stability gap g=E|k+sigma N|-E[u k] is strictly positive.
The feasible damped endpoint (1-g/4)u+(g/4)sign(Bu) improves actual energy
by at least g^2/8 in the population limit; finite approximants retain a
strict margin. Every fixed finite inertial-sign iterate is nondegenerate.

More generally, every finite causal paired history has a compact moment
body for actual energy c^T Jc/2. At a maximizer ||c||<1: a largest-index
fresh-Gaussian argument rules out a bounded norm-one stationary vector.
Compactness gives a positive frame-dependent residual floor. One actual
new query plus feedback therefore strictly exceeds the best population
energy of that entire finite history. This theorem, including measurable
approximation margins and redundant-query deletion, is in
`resumed_response_paired_history_actual_energy_extension_2026_09_06.md`.
The director and bound-audit agent independently reconstructed it.

Consequently the supremum over all fixed finite equivariant GFOMs on
the flat-involution class is not attained by any finite algorithm. This
does not identify that supremum or the original-signing limit.

There cannot be a positive depth-uniform stability gap in this broad
feedback class: the independent auditor constructs fresh-gated damped
Boolean updates with arbitrarily small positive gaps, while their
energies remain below the fixed-GFOM ceiling and hence a fixed distance
below 1/2. See
`resumed_bound_audit_mixed_charge_feedback_2026_09_06.md`, Section 5.
Thus strict extension is real, but a uniform rate or sharp endpoint
requires additional structure not supplied by feasibility or local
stationarity alone.

## 5. Essential open comparison

The mixed-charge kernel and the actual-energy iteration theorems are
proved for Haar/flat involutions and explicit stable comparators. The
original minimization ranges over ALL hollow sign matrices. No argument
here proves that its minimizing sequences are flat, involutory, or
universality-comparable. That missing comparison, together with the
unidentified feedback optimum and possible growing-depth effects,
prevents these mechanism results from resolving the original normalized
convergence question.
