## Classification scale

## Candidate cards

### 1. Spectral action compactness

- **Imported theorem:** Backhausz--Szegedy compactness for `P`-operators under
  appropriate common norm bounds.
- **Problem translation:** for `T_A=A/sqrt(n)`,
  `Phi(T_A)=2Q(A)/n^(3/2)`.
- **New verified support:** if `||S||_(2->2),||T||_(2->2)<=C`, then

```math
|\Phi(S)-\Phi(T)|
\le5C\sqrt{2d_M(S,T)}+2d_M(S,T).
```

- **Exact target:** fixed-`C` spectral regularization `SR`, followed by a
  signed Gamma-limsup recovery sequence at every large index.
- **Strictness:** `SR` uses only cap and operator norm and is genuinely new.
  The recovery clause is not strict; it is exactly archived `AR`.
- **Falsifier:** a fixed positive cap gap between unrestricted minimizers and
  every fixed-`C` spectral class.
- **Decision:** retain as a structural B input; do not execute without a new
  substitute for `AR`.

### 2. Terminal coset drift

- **Translation:** `b_n(U)` is the number of edge flips increasing distance
  from `U` to the augmented cut code.  Exactly,

```math
b_n(U)=N_n-
\left|\bigcup_{g_v\le1}N_v\right|.
```

- **Strictness:** `(r,b)` provably does not recover the outer distribution.
- **Exact finite warning:** exhaustive orders `3`--`8` contain rapidly many
  nondeep dead ends; an order-10 root has `Q=15`, `b=0`, while `M_10=13`.
- **Scalable obstruction:** square-field Paley conferences have
  `Q=rn/2`, every edge flip has cap `Q+2`, and hence `b=0,z->1/2`.
- **Consequence:** a unique drift zero must be `1/2`; deepest roots then force
  `M_n/n^(3/2)->1/2`.
- **Decision:** withdraw as a reduction.  A second infinite terminal family
  with limiting `z!=1/2` is its exact falsifier, but searching for one is an
  upper-construction problem of independent difficulty.

### 3. Finite-temperature interpolation

- **Imported theorem:** Guerra--Toninelli size interpolation for quenched
  Gaussian disorder, plus the exact log-sum-exp zero-temperature sandwich.
- **Translation:** fixed-temperature pressure is a Laplace transform of the
  complete rooted coset weight distribution.
- **Exact target:** convergence of the pressure after minimizing over binary
  disorders, for every fixed temperature.
- **Why not weaker:** no theorem moves the outer minimum through the random
  interpolation; deterministic completion retains the full bridge/Gibbs
  response.  Strict-high-temperature stability does not continue to all
  temperatures.
- **Falsifier:** a nonvanishing fixed-temperature limsup/liminf gap, or a
  comparable-block family with a linear deterministic completion defect.
- **Decision:** class C; no further execution.

### 4. Composition, restriction, and growing representation

All three have correct conditional implications.  The generalized
subadditivity theorem is only a conclusion engine after a summable recurrence
exists.  Restriction needs the same scalar optimum comparison it was meant to
derive.  Growing harmonic degree can retain the required scale, but known
arbitrary-root implementations then carry the weighted coset histogram.
Consequently none removes an obligation.

### 5. Genuine nonconvergence

The even-extension Paley trace-fibre calculation is exact:

```math
|x^{\mathsf T}B_qx|=(1-p^{-2})q^{3/2}.
```

It is an `exists matrix, exists spin` high-energy witness, not an all-spin
upper construction or an all-signings lower theorem.  Arf phase is not
coset-uniform, and tensor parity leaves uncontrolled entangled spins.  No
candidate produces two rigorously separated subsequences of `M_n/n^(3/2)`.

## Three common bottlenecks

1. **Joint parent response.**  One bridge must answer exponentially many
   coupled child states.  A scalar cap or pressure discards the cancellation
   needed to select it; restoring that cancellation reconstructs the parent
   optimization.
2. **All-coset extreme tail.**  Packing, typical roots, fixed moments, and
   bounded local summaries do not see the deepest arbitrary root.  At the
   required accuracy, known growing states recover the near-complete rooted
   histogram.
3. **All-order fluctuation realization.**  Compactness and algebraic/tensor
   families give subsequences.  Convergence needs exact hollow sign matrices
   at every large order with `o(n^(3/2))` loss; known restriction and
   absorption mechanisms either pay a fixed loss or restate the scalar
   comparison.

## Director decision

The execution set is empty.  The panel found no architecture satisfying the
user's execution gates.  Starting a proof campaign on `SR` would be premature
because it does not imply convergence; starting one on terminal drift would
amount to attacking the stronger sharp-constant/no-metastability theorem.

No further autonomous work is currently justified inside these architectures.
A defensible restart needs a genuinely new input of one of these forms:

1. a lossless every-order signed recovery theorem that is strictly weaker
   than scalar optimum comparison;
2. a deterministic interpolation identity whose remainder is signed before
   the outer disorder minimum and has `o(n)` pressure defect;
3. an arbitrary-root algebraic hierarchy with a proved polynomial or
   subexponential closed state that does not determine the coset histogram; or
4. a selected all-spin low construction paired with a genuinely universal
   order-dependent lower obstruction for nonconvergence.
