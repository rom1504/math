# Critical-scale code geometry: verified statements and scope

Status: **Verified** by proof reconstruction and independent audit.
None of these statements proves convergence or nonconvergence of M_n.

## 1. Translation-invariant response metrics cannot be uniformly compact

Let C <= F_2^N be a binary linear code of dimension k. Give X=F_2^N/C
its quotient Hamming metric d, put r(x)=d(x,0), and let
Phi(x)=(N-2r(x))/a, where a>0. Then exactly

```math
\sup_{z\in X}|\Phi(x+z)-\Phi(y+z)|=\frac{2d(x,y)}a.          \tag{CG.1}
```

The triangle inequality proves <=, and z=x proves equality.

Suppose delta is a translation-invariant pseudometric on X and a common
modulus omega satisfies |Phi(x)-Phi(y)| <= omega(delta(x,y)), with omega
nondecreasing and omega(t) -> 0 as t -> 0. Applying this to all translations
in (CG.1), every delta-ball of radius eta is contained in a quotient
Hamming ball of radius floor(a omega(eta)/2). Therefore

```math
\mathcal N_\delta(X,\eta)\ge
\frac{2^{N-k}}{\displaystyle\sum_{j=0}^{\lfloor a\omega(\eta)/2\rfloor}
\binom Nj}.                                                \tag{CG.2}
```

If k=o(N), a=o(N), and eta is fixed, the denominator has logarithm o(N);
the covering number has logarithm (log 2+o(1))N. For the augmented cut
code, N=binom(n,2), k=n (n>=3), a=n^(3/2), this is
(log 2/2+o(1))n^2.

**Scope.** This requires a uniform continuity modulus on the entire
quotient and translation invariance, or explicit preservation of all
translated queries. It does not rule out states restricted to exact
minimizers, nontranslation metrics, physical continuation queries, or
Gamma convergence without these equicontinuity assumptions.

## 2. An isometric cube inside each leader support

If D is the support of a coset leader, then for every c in C,
|c intersect D| <= |c outside D|. For any E subset D, this implies
|c intersect E| <= |c outside E|, so E is itself a leader. Consequently

```math
F\longmapsto [D\setminus F]\quad(F\subseteq D)
```

is an isometric Hamming cube in the quotient: the distance of two images
is |F symmetric-difference F'|, and their distances to zero are
|D|-|F|. This also proves that all images are distinct cosets.

For a deepest augmented-cut leader, rho_n=(N-M_n)/2=(1/4+o(1))n^2.
Its t-deletion shell contains at least binom(rho_n,t) distinct cosets
with exact cap M_n+2t. At t=floor(n^(3/2)/log n),

```math
\log\binom{\rho_n}t=(1/2+o(1))n^{3/2}.                       \tag{CG.3}
```

Dividing by at most n! for vertex relabelings preserves this exponent.
Thus there are exponentially many o(1)-near-optimal orbit classes. Their
maximum raw quotient diameter is only 2t: normalized separation vanishes,
so this is not a fixed-resolution compactness obstruction.

The cap identity is archive-adjacent to the geodesic planted-face
construction. The isometry and shell count refine it. Puncturing does
not preserve leaderhood; this gives no cross-order recurrence.

For completeness, a fixed halo does obstruct compactness under the
global hypotheses of Section 1. Take t=floor(theta n^(3/2)) and greedily
pack the binom(rho_n,t) shell points at quotient-Hamming separation
alpha t, where 0<alpha<1. A quotient ball has at most

```math
V_N(\lfloor\alpha t\rfloor)
=\sum_{j\le\alpha t}\binom Nj
```

points. Its logarithm is (alpha theta/2+o(1)) n^(3/2) log n,
while the shell logarithm is (theta/2+o(1)) n^(3/2) log n.
The resulting packing therefore has logarithm at least
((1-alpha)theta/2+o(1))n^(3/2) log n. Equation (CG.1) converts this to
a fixed positive delta-separation by taking omega(eta)<2 alpha theta.
If delta is also invariant under vertex permutations, greedy removal of
the n! possible relabelings costs only O(n log n) in log packing size.
All points have Phi within 2theta+o(1) of its minimum. This does not
extend to shrinking halos at fixed accuracy.

## 3. Identical primal/dual enumerators miss the critical deficit

Let A6 and B6 be the length-six binary codes generated by decimal masks

```math
A_6=\langle3,5,57\rangle,\qquad B_6=\langle3,12,48\rangle.
```

Both have dimension 3, contain the all-one word, and have enumerator
1+3z^2+3z^4+z^6. Their leader enumerators are respectively
1+4z+3z^2 and 1+3z+3z^2+z^3, so their covering radii are 2 and 3.

These assertions can be checked by listing 8 codewords and 64 ambient
words. The published verifier performs those exact checks. Now set

```math
C_t=\operatorname{Rep}_{2t^4}\oplus A_6^{\oplus t^3},
\qquad D_t=\operatorname{Rep}_{2t^4}\oplus B_6^{\oplus t^3}.
```

Both have length N=2t^4+6t^3 and dimension 1+3t^3=o(N). Their primal
enumerators are identical by multiplication and their dual enumerators
are identical by the MacWilliams identity. Covering radius adds under
direct sum, giving radii t^4+2t^3 and t^4+3t^3. Therefore

```math
N-2\rho(C_t)=2t^3,\qquad N-2\rho(D_t)=0,
\qquad \frac{2t^3}{N^{3/4}}\longrightarrow 2^{1/4}.           \tag{CG.4}
```

This falsifies determining the N^(3/4) deficit from primal/dual weight
enumerators for general low-rate codes. Its dimension is Theta(N^(3/4)),
not the augmented cut code's Theta(sqrt(N)); it is not a counterexample
about that particular code or about M_n. Equal radial data does not mean
equal displacement-indexed or energy-conditioned overlap data.

## 4. Physical continuations have a smaller query language

The exact one-sided disjoint-continuation metric is already Theorem 21.29
of extremal_information/theorems.md: d_phys(A,A')=Q(A-A'). Its exposing
positive-clique construction has a Theta(n^2) calibration. It is not a
low-cap parent construction.

Here is a separation from arbitrary edgewise translations. Let S be a
graph with d=Theta(n^(3/2)) edges and maximum degree O(sqrt(n)), for
example a circulant graph of that degree. Let A be uniformly random
edge signs and A' differ by reversing exactly S. With positive probability,

```math
Q(A),Q(A')=O(n^{3/2}),\qquad
Q(A-A')=O(\sqrt{nd})=O(n^{5/4}).                            \tag{CG.5}
```

Proof: each fixed spin response is a sum of independent signs (or twice
d independent signs for A-A'); Hoeffding and a union bound over 2^(n-1)
spins prove all three bounds simultaneously, with sufficiently large
absolute constants.

Multiplication of both signings edgewise by A sends them to J-I and
J-I-2 1_S. The former has cap N; the latter has cap N-2d for large n.
To check this last assertion, a cut of size k(n-k), k<=n/2, decreases the
all-plus energy by 2k(n-k) and can undo at most 4 Delta(S)k negative-edge
penalty. Thus all-plus remains the positive maximum when Delta(S)<=n/4.
Every negative energy has magnitude at most n/2+2d, smaller than N-2d
for large n. The translated cap gap is exactly 2d=Theta(n^(3/2)).

Hence arbitrary translated-cap queries are not uniformly continuous in
d_phys/n^(3/2), even on a class with bounded normalized cap.
A fixed one-copy continuation, including minimization over a common
child-independent family of continuations, is 1-Lipschitz in d_phys.
A sum using q child copies is q-Lipschitz, so a uniform approximation
to this translated query with o(n^(3/2)) error would require
q=Omega(n^(1/4)) for these examples. The statement excludes rewriting
the old coefficients and does not concern exact minimizing children.

## 5. Exact primary-literature mapping

Let E_{n,2}={e_i+e_j:i<j}. The code

```math
P_{2,n}=RM(1,n)|_{E_{n,2}}
=\{(c+b_i+b_j)_{i<j}:c,b_i\in\mathbb F_2\}
```

is exactly the augmented cut code. For n>=3 its dimension is n: the sole
relation among the n+1 affine generators is the all-one linear form,
which vanishes on the weight-two slice. For a truth table f_ij and
a_ij=(-1)^(f_ij),

```math
\operatorname{NL}_{E_{n,2}}(f)=\frac{N-Q(A)}2,\qquad
\rho(P_{2,n})=\frac{N-M_n}2.                                \tag{CG.6}
```

The affine constant is essential for the absolute value. References:
[Carlet--Meaux--Rotella, restricted-input Boolean functions](https://doi.org/10.13154/tosc.v2017.i3.192-227),
[Dumer--Kapralova, spherically punctured Reed--Muller codes](https://doi.org/10.1109/TIT.2013.2250579),
and [the weightwise-nonlinearity study, Sections 2.1--2.2](https://eprint.iacr.org/2022/408.pdf).
These supply an exact literature translation, not a new reduction.
The inspected results do not improve the current asymptotic interval.
