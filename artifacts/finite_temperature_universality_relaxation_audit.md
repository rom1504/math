# Exact boundary of the available deterministic free-energy universality theorems

Status: **source-checked theorem audit with one usable relaxation and one explicit
blocker**.  The audit is restricted to
[Fan--Misiakiewicz--Wang--Wen, arXiv:2607.10102](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10102),
[Gorini--Jones--Kunisky--Pesenti, arXiv:2604.11729](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.11729),
and
[Wang--Zhong--Fan, arXiv:2206.13037v5](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.13037).

## 1. The one theorem-level relaxation

For a matrix `M`, let `Delta(M)` denote its diagonal part and let
`Delta<x>` be the diagonal monomials generated by multiplication by `x` and
nested applications of `Delta`.  Definition 2.6 and Theorem 2.8 of
arXiv:2206.13037 give AMP universality under the following conditions on a
bounded symmetric base matrix sequence `M_n`:

```math
{1\over n}\operatorname{Tr}p(M_n)\longrightarrow {\cal D}_{\rm diag}(p)
\quad\hbox{for every }p\in\Delta\langle x\rangle,                 \tag{1.1}
```

```math
\max_{i\ne j}|p(M_n)_{ij}|<n^{-1/2+\varepsilon}
\quad\hbox{eventually, for every fixed }p\in\Delta\langle x\rangle
\hbox{ and }\varepsilon>0,                                      \tag{1.2}
```

where the limiting diagonal distribution in (1.1) must equal that of an
orthogonally invariant ensemble.  The theorem is applied to

```math
W_n=\Pi_nM_n\Pi_n^{\mathsf T},                                  \tag{1.3}
```

with `Pi_n` a uniformly random signed permutation.

The proof of Corollary 2.10 in arXiv:2607.10102 says explicitly that AMP state
evolution and the operator-norm bound are the only disorder properties used in
its free-energy argument.  At zero external field, (1.3) does not change the
Ising partition function: signed permutation is a bijection of the Boolean
cube.  Combining the two papers therefore gives the following usable
corollary.

**Generalized-diagonal universality corollary.**  Suppose a deterministic
`M_n` satisfies (1.1)--(1.2), its diagonal distribution matches the
orthogonally invariant law with compact mean-zero spectral limit `mu`, and

```math
\limsup_n\|M_n\|_{\rm op}<1/2.                                  \tag{1.4}
```

Then the zero-field Ising free energy has the replica-symmetric limit from
Corollary 2.8 of arXiv:2607.10102.  In the notation used in the project,

```math
{1\over n}\log Z(M_n)\longrightarrow
\log 2+{1\over2}\int_0^1R_\mu(t)\,dt.                           \tag{1.5}
```

Applying the same statement to `-M_n` transfers the normalized `cosh`
pressure as well.

This genuinely removes the **uniform diagonal** part of Assumption 2.9:
diagonal quantities need only converge after normalized trace averaging.
It does **not** replace entrywise delocalization by an averaged condition.
Condition (1.2) is uniform over every off-diagonal entry and ranges over all
diagonal monomials, not merely powers.

Lemma B.1 and Proposition 2.7(b)(2) of arXiv:2206.13037 show that Assumption
2.9's power conditions imply (1.1)--(1.2).  They do not prove the converse and
do not derive (1.2) from Frobenius or traffic averages.

## 2. Exact mapping of the 2026 traffic theorem

Assumption 5.2 and Theorem 5.3 of arXiv:2604.11729 do not supply the missing
averaged-to-uniform step.  For `H_n` they require, with
`epsilon_n=n^{-1/2+o(1)}`,

```math
\max_{i\ne j}|W_\alpha(H_n)_{ij}|\le\varepsilon_n              \tag{2.1}
```

for **every open cactus** `alpha`, together with the centered `L^2` condition

```math
{1\over\sqrt n}\|P w_\sigma(H_n)\|_2\le\varepsilon_n           \tag{2.2}
```

for every rooted cactus `sigma`.  Here

```math
P=I-{1\over n}{\bf1}{\bf1}^{\mathsf T},\qquad A_n=PH_nP.        \tag{2.3}
```

The conclusion is equality of limiting traffic distributions for the
rank-one-projected matrix (2.3), followed by fixed-time polynomial-GFOM state
evolution.  Thus:

- (2.1) is still a maximum-entry hypothesis at the same scale as the missing
  condition;
- “puncturing” means projection away from the single all-ones direction, not
  deleting `o(n)` exceptional coordinates;
- the theorem proves polynomial-GFOM/traffic convergence, not an Ising
  free-energy formula for the original unpunctured matrix.

The remark following Corollary 2.10 in arXiv:2607.10102 compares a punctured
deterministic matrix with a similarly punctured orthogonally invariant one,
but explicitly says that analogues of the Gibbs/free-energy results for that
punctured random model are not derived there.  It therefore gives no
black-box pressure-preserving regularization of a signing.

## 3. Why Frobenius-near conference structure stops short

For a flat signing put

```math
X_n={\beta A_n\over\sqrt n},\qquad
c_n^2=\beta^2(1-1/n),\qquad E_n=X_n^2-c_n^2I.                    \tag{3.1}
```

Assume `||X_n||_op=O(1)` and

```math
{1\over\sqrt n}\|E_n\|_{\rm F}\longrightarrow0.               \tag{3.2}
```

There is a useful exact consequence of (3.2): the averaged diagonal
distribution in (1.1) is the Bernoulli/conference diagonal distribution.
Indeed, for every fixed `r`, telescoping powers gives

```math
{1\over\sqrt n}\|X_n^{2r}-c_n^{2r}I\|_{\rm F}=o(1),\qquad
{1\over\sqrt n}\|X_n^{2r+1}-c_n^{2r}X_n\|_{\rm F}=o(1).        \tag{3.3}
```

Since `Delta(X_n)=0`, induction over the construction of a diagonal monomial,
using that `Delta` contracts Frobenius norm, proves the corresponding
normalized-Frobenius approximation for every `p in Delta<x>`.  Taking traces
gives (1.1) and the conference diagonal law.

But (3.2) does not imply (1.2), already for `p(x)=x^2`.  This failure occurs
inside flat sign matrices.  Let `C_n` be a symmetric conference signing,
choose distinct `i,j`, and choose `r=floor(n^(3/4))` indices `k` for which
`C_(ik)C_(kj)=-1`.  Flip only the `r` edges `{i,k}` and call the resulting
signing `A_n`.  If `D_n=A_n-C_n`, then

```math
\|D_n\|_{\rm F}=2\sqrt{2r},\qquad \|D_n\|_{\rm op}=2\sqrt r,
```

and hence

```math
\|A_n^2-(n-1)I\|_{\rm F}=O(\sqrt{nr}+r).                       \tag{3.4}
```

Thus the scaled residual in (3.1) even tends to zero in unnormalized
Frobenius norm, and

```math
\|X_n\|_{\rm op}\le\beta+o(1).                                \tag{3.5}
```

Nevertheless the selected entry is

```math
(X_n^2)_{ij}={2\beta^2r\over n}=\Theta(n^{-1/4}),               \tag{3.6}
```

which violates (1.2) for every `epsilon<1/4`.  Random signed-permutation
conjugation cannot repair this: for every diagonal monomial,

```math
p(\Pi M\Pi^{\mathsf T})=\Pi p(M)\Pi^{\mathsf T},               \tag{3.7}
```

so the maximum absolute off-diagonal entry is unchanged.

## 4. Audited conclusion

The specified papers give a real but limited relaxation of Assumption 2.9:
uniform diagonal control may be replaced by convergence of the full averaged
diagonal distribution.  Frobenius-near conference structure verifies that
averaged part.  The remaining theorem-level obligation is still the uniform
off-diagonal condition (1.2), or some new universality theorem that replaces
it by an averaged/exceptional-coordinate hypothesis.

Neither arXiv:2604.11729 nor arXiv:2206.13037 proves such a replacement, and
neither proves that `o(n)` bad coordinates may be removed while preserving the
Ising pressure.  Consequently these papers do not presently close the
regularization lemma for deterministic pressure minimizers.  The exact new
frontier is narrower: **diagonal-distribution matching is automatic under
Frobenius-near conference structure; uniform off-diagonal diagonal-monomial
delocalization remains open.**
