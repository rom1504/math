## 1. Strongest actual-signing results

1. **All-order Paley saturation.** For every sequence of odd prime
   powers `q=1 mod 4`, both signed extrema of the Paley core approach
   `+/- q^(3/2)` in the full quadratic form. Thus its absolute half-cap,
   and that of the bordered conference matrix, approach coefficient
   `1/2`. This is all-q, not merely an even-extension or favorable-prime
   subsequence. See `resumed_convergence_paley_squarefree_audit_2026_09_06.md`.

2. **Uniform Fourier-reflection saturation.** Every real even Fourier
   multiplier taking values `+/-1` off zero, on any sequence of growing
   finite abelian groups, has Boolean absolute Rayleigh quotient tending
   to one. Witnesses can be asymptotically mean zero; only one absolute
   orientation is guaranteed in this generality. The nontrivial case is
   the centered reflection. The proof combines bounded squarefree
   Hermite profiles with finite Deuber/projective-vector Ramsey hosts.
   It does not apply to arbitrary non-Fourier flat involutions. See
   `resumed_convergence_fourier_involution_ramsey_saturation_2026_09_06.md`.

3. **Hamming/Frobenius robustness around spectrally flat Cayley families.**
   The bound-audit extension proves that any hollow symmetric `B_q`
   within Frobenius `o(q)` of a real symmetric convolution `A_q` whose
   lower spectral bulk is at least `(1-o(1))sqrt(q)` has
   `liminf Q(B_q)/q^(3/2)>=1/2`. No operator bound is needed. In
   particular, `o(q^2)` coefficient flips around an asymptotically
   spectral-optimal Cayley signing cannot produce a sub-half family.
   This extension was independently reconstructed here. See
   `resumed_bound_audit_cayley_hamming_stability_2026_09_06.md`.

4. **Exact radial Paley protection.** At each admissible q, if a signing
   `B` differs from the Paley core `A` on d edges,

       Q(B)>=Q(A)|1-4d/[q(q-1)]|.

   Affine symmetries average exact oriented ground states to a covariance
   proportional to `A`. In particular, fewer than
   `q(q-1)/(2Q(A))=(1-o(1))sqrt(q)` coefficient flips cannot lower its
   cap, using energy parity. This is local protection, not global
   optimality. See `resumed_convergence_paley_radial_hamming_law_2026_09_06.md`.

5. **A genuinely nonflat characteristic-three theorem.** Every sequence
   of hollow symmetric additive-Cayley signings on `F_(3^r)` satisfies

       liminf Q(A)/q^(3/2)>=2080/(9 sqrt(269441))
                              approximately .4452346798594428.

   The homogeneous weighted extension and translation projection also

6. **Spectral tests and the remaining Cayley regime.** Every real
   symmetric convolution matrix satisfies `Q(A)>=n||A||op/3`, by exact

## 2. Original-minimizer geometry

The gap-two geometry audit and extension show square-root-scale
separation, not just the earlier fourth-root scale. With an oriented
ground switched to all ones, a same-oriented gap-two state flipping at
most r coordinates can only flip rows with ground field at most `2r-1`.
The coefficient-local-minimality argument then forces a gap-two state
at distance approximately `(c/2)sqrt(n)` when `Q~c n^(3/2)`.

The orientation dichotomy is more informative than a raw count:
oppositely oriented near states already have distance at least
`(Q-1)/n`; if every near state has positive orientation, identical
vertex-code classes are negative cliques. Random class switches imply
`sum class_size^2<=2Q+n`, hence at least `n^2/(2Q+n)` code classes and
approximately `(1/2)log_2 n` near states at a half-cap. This does not
force a large unavoidable bridge field: even code-class sizes permit
within-class balancing. See
`resumed_convergence_gap_two_orientation_geometry_2026_09_06.md`.

## 4. Seed-algebra work: genuine mechanisms, but no landing theorem

The track also constructed exact/limiting signed Boolean modules from
three-cell simplex quotients, resonant Gauss-phase carriers, programmable
projective phases, vectorial Maiorana--McFarland powers, affine quadratic
index inversion, arbitrary binary phases, and controlled translations.
These are genuine finite-profile bilinear witnesses, but they do not
automatically compose on overlapping coordinates.

The enlarged arbitrary Fourier-permutation model realizes the Boolean
covariance majorant T through Gaussian-cloud matching. Actual quadratic
index families cannot implement that independent-cloud matching, even
with arbitrary frequency signs; this is an information-family bound,
not a prohibition on correlated witnesses. The final arbitrary controlled
translation module is physically block diagonal in its selector and
does not increase the optimized seed norm beyond the binary-phase
family alone. No `R=T` theorem was proved.

More importantly, even `R=T` would not itself settle the original
convergence question. The outstanding upper-bound obligation is an
upper-preserving all-order landing from near-minimizers of the ORIGINAL
Boolean quadratic cap. The new lower witnesses and structured-family
rigidity results do not discharge that obligation.

The last boundary package makes this gap especially explicit. Every
transitive real seed has `T(B)>=sqrt(k)||B||_F/2`, with equality exactly
at a flat spectrum. Yet the sparse weighted family `Paley9 tensor I`
has actual ratio `Q/(sqrt(q)||B||_F)=sqrt(2)/3<1/2`. This falsifies
unrestricted homogeneous weighted realization, but NOT a statement
also imposing bounded entries and near-full Frobenius mass. See
`resumed_convergence_transitive_majorant_and_weighted_barrier_2026_09_06.md`.
