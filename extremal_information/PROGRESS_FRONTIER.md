## Objective and guardrail

The eventual objective is convergence of `M_n/n^(3/2)`.  This campaign first
asks which collective response properties are forced by genuine
`epsilon`-near-minimizers

```math
\mathcal N_n(epsilon)=
\{A:Q(A)\le M_n+epsilon n^{3/2}\}.
```

Structured exact-sign families are Level 4 evidence.  They become Level 5
only when a theorem and a serious falsifier both concern certified low-cap or
near-minimizing dense signings.  No current result authorizes a direct attack
on convergence.

   scale, because this remains a vanishing `Theta(n^(-1/2))` fraction of all

   or force one fixed-ambient-gap pair; more literal common-intersection
   lemmas are unnecessary.  Two consecutive fixed-scale strikes freeze this
