## 1. Multi-selector verdict

The physical collision is growing information, not one bit.

- In the original PC.3 sparse-flip architecture, Theorem MS.3 constructs a
  one-hot family

  ```math
  N^{1/(10000\log16)}\le k_N\le N^{1/2}
  ```

  with one common balanced-compiler query bank and a fixed
  `Theta(N^(3/2))` response gap.  The upper bound counts native projective
  poles; neither bound is sharp.
- This is a packing, not a `2^k` independently togglable edit cube.  The
  literal cube/multiplexed-parent problem remains open; disjoint edge layers
  and canonical rank-one superposition have only architecture-specific
  ceilings.
- The campaign then obtained the stronger Level-5 operational statement.
  Every complete signing with `Q(A)=O(n^(3/2))` has a switching suborbit of
  size `exp(Omega(n))` separated by all-spins-free exact-sign, bounded-cap
  scalar contexts.  One public base fill generates all queries by switching.
  Thus its fixed-scale public response rate satisfies

  ```math
  (\gamma/\log2)n\le R_n\le n-1.
  ```

The last lower bound is exact in order, but its language matters: optimized
switching-covariant bridges erase the entire child switch label.  It is an
extensive fixed-reference response theorem, not an optimized-bridge
incompressibility theorem.

## 3. The two selected near-minimality lemmas

### `L_tail` — proved, then retired

For each fixed `C`, there are `d_C,kappa_C>0` such that every complete
signing with `Q(A)<=Cn^(3/2)` obeys, at both one-sided extrema,

```math
#\{x:P_\sigma(A)-\sigma H_A(x)<d_Cn^{3/2}\}
\le e^{(\log2-\kappa_C)n}.
```

This is stronger than the selected exact-minimizer statement.  Its proof
combines Pietsch principal-core localization, the one-sided discrepancy
product on the linear complement, block superadditivity, and conditional
Rademacher/Hanson--Wright concentration.  It has assumption-distance score
4 and plugs into the switching/matched-roof/boundary-fill chain, yielding
the `Theta(n)` fixed-reference response theorem.

It does **not** produce near-minimal parents at the doubled order, a
cross-order recurrence, or pressure convergence.  Theorem 37.3 and Theorem
37.4 show that its scalar thermal consequences are insufficient.

### `L_projective` — held and frozen, not recommended for execution

The clean remaining statement is: for some fixed `gamma>0`, every exact
minimizer has an `o(n^(3/2))` positive shell containing a growing, ideally
`exp(Omega(n))`, projective packing at ambient edge distance `gamma n^2`.
AO.2 would compile such a packing into bounded-cap physical response.

Evidence is finite only.  The strongest counterpressure is exact:
arbitrary vanishing near-minimizers can have a planted one-cap shell, while
generic deep-hole, finite-flip, affine, and fractional-reservoir arguments
stop at `Theta(M_n)=Theta(n^(3/2))` edge distance, a vanishing
`Theta(n^(-1/2))` ambient fraction.  The assumption-distance remains 3 only
because exact optimality is essential.  Two consecutive scale-transfer
strikes freeze this lemma; no more shell variants are justified without a
new ambient-scale amplifier.

No second open near-minimality lemma survived severe selection.  In
particular, exact active isotropy, spectral flatness, bounded operator norm,
and scalar tail-to-pressure closure are now rejected.

## 7. Best current route toward `M_n`

Only one chain is defensible enough to recommend:

```text
exact entropy-regularized bridge identity
+
L_basin: for every fixed beta and comparable split, take the two child
minimizers at beta*sqrt(m/N) and beta*sqrt(n/N) used in the exact bridge
identity; an exp(-O(N)) family of exact-sign bridges has parent pressure at
most P_m(beta)+P_n(beta)+O(N^(1-delta))

+
convergence of P_n(beta)/n for every fixed beta
+
the uniform soft/ground sandwich as beta -> infinity
=> convergence of M_n/n^(3/2).
```

`L_basin` is weaker than full parent optimization: it asks only for the mass
of a pressure sublevel set and an aggregate threshold, neither an optimal
bridge nor the full spin/coset landscape.  It is also directly falsifiable by
a quadratic-speed lower-deviation theorem.  It must be proved for the exact
contracted-temperature child minimizers; conference examples alone do not
suffice.  The conference analysis now shows exactly where such a positive
law could hide: beyond an `r^(3/2)` edit collar of the operator-regular set,
outside the power-regular pressure-to-quartic reduction, and without any
fixed leading improvement of the typical Gram/quartic coordinates.  It must
also spread `O(r)` conditional information over `Theta(r^2)` coordinates;
sparse/affine support, weak product templates, and adaptive gauges are
rigorously insufficient.

The old `L_replace` chain is not recommended until it names a concrete
gauge-invariant state.  In its current form it remains too close to an
all-order restatement of the desired transfer.
