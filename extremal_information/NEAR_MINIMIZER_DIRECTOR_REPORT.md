## 1. Multi-selector verdict

The physical collision is growing information, not one bit.

- In the original PC.3 sparse-flip architecture, Theorem MS.3 constructs a
  one-hot family

  ```math
  N^{1/(10000\log16)}\le k_N\le N^{1/2}
  ```

  with one common balanced-compiler query bank and a fixed
  `Theta(N^(3/2))` response gap.  The upper bound counts native projective
  poles; neither bound is sharp.
- This is a packing, not a `2^k` independently togglable edit cube.  The
  literal cube/multiplexed-parent problem remains open; disjoint edge layers
  and canonical rank-one superposition have only architecture-specific
  ceilings.
- The campaign then obtained the stronger Level-5 operational statement.
  Every complete signing with `Q(A)=O(n^(3/2))` has a switching suborbit of
  size `exp(Omega(n))` separated by all-spins-free exact-sign, bounded-cap
  scalar contexts.  One public base fill generates all queries by switching.
  Thus its fixed-scale public response rate satisfies

  ```math
  (\gamma/\log2)n\le R_n\le n-1.
  ```

The last lower bound is exact in order, but its language matters: optimized
switching-covariant bridges erase the entire child switch label.  It is an
extensive fixed-reference response theorem, not an optimized-bridge
incompressibility theorem.

## 3. The two selected near-minimality lemmas

### `L_tail` — proved, then retired

For each fixed `C`, there are `d_C,kappa_C>0` such that every complete
signing with `Q(A)<=Cn^(3/2)` obeys, at both one-sided extrema,

```math
#\{x:P_\sigma(A)-\sigma H_A(x)<d_Cn^{3/2}\}
\le e^{(\log2-\kappa_C)n}.
```

This is stronger than the selected exact-minimizer statement.  Its proof
combines Pietsch principal-core localization, the one-sided discrepancy
product on the linear complement, block superadditivity, and conditional
Rademacher/Hanson--Wright concentration.  It has assumption-distance score
4 and plugs into the switching/matched-roof/boundary-fill chain, yielding
the `Theta(n)` fixed-reference response theorem.

It does **not** produce near-minimal parents at the doubled order, a
cross-order recurrence, or pressure convergence.  Theorem 37.3 and Theorem
37.4 show that its scalar thermal consequences are insufficient.

### `L_projective` — held and frozen, not recommended for execution

The clean remaining statement is: for some fixed `gamma>0`, every exact
minimizer has an `o(n^(3/2))` positive shell containing a growing, ideally
`exp(Omega(n))`, projective packing at ambient edge distance `gamma n^2`.
AO.2 would compile such a packing into bounded-cap physical response.

Evidence is finite only.  The strongest counterpressure is exact:
arbitrary vanishing near-minimizers can have a planted one-cap shell, while
generic deep-hole, finite-flip, affine, and fractional-reservoir arguments
stop at `Theta(M_n)=Theta(n^(3/2))` edge distance, a vanishing
`Theta(n^(-1/2))` ambient fraction.  The assumption-distance remains 3 only
because exact optimality is essential.  Two consecutive scale-transfer
strikes freeze this lemma; no more shell variants are justified without a
new ambient-scale amplifier.

No second open near-minimality lemma survived severe selection.  In
particular, exact active isotropy, spectral flatness, bounded operator norm,
and scalar tail-to-pressure closure are now rejected.

## 5. Quantitative progress since the prompt

- The campaign crossed from Level 4.x to an audited Level-5 theorem: the
  selected `L_tail` is now uniform over every bounded-cap complete signing.
- Physical multi-selector capacity moved from one bit to a polynomial PC.3
  packing and then to `exp(Omega(n))` response states, with a matching
  `Theta(n)`-bit upper rate on each switching orbit.
- Query variation fell from a possible quadratic per-query description to
  an `n-1`-bit pole label after one public equivariant base fill.
- Universal affine charts reached dimension `Theta(n/log^2 n)` at
  `O(n^(3/2)/log^2 n)` shell defect; near-minimizer atlases achieved
  `O(n^(4/3))` within-chart response error.  Neither improved fixed-ratio
  cross-order loss.
- Deficit-scale coding geometry improved from coordinate coverage to
  growing `Theta(M_n)` projective packings, but the normalized ambient scale
  stayed `Theta(n^(-1/2))`; this was explicitly scored as no global frontier
  change.
- Rigid spectral, exact-active, hereditary-exact, and cheap-peeling routes
  were eliminated by genuine exact/near-minimizer witnesses or scalable
  vanishing-near-minimizer constructions.
- Finally, two apparent consequences were removed: labelled switching
  information is pure gauge for optimized covariant bridges, and spin-space
  thinness does not imply favorable bridge-disorder mass.  These are scope
  corrections, not convergence-side gains.
- On the orthogonal conference benchmark, the bridge lower-tail problem was
  reduced strictly.  Every fixed pressure deviation in the overwhelming
  operator-regular sector costs `exp(-Omega(r^2))`, this remains true through
  a `Theta(r^(3/2))` edit collar, and every positive disorder tilt `o(r)` is
  annealed there.  A scalar pressure sublevel cannot supply the missing
  regularity: an exact planted-block family forces
  `Omega(r^(1/4))` Frobenius response inside one strict sublevel.
- The exact gauge-invariant fourth coefficient is
  `J=||BB^T||_F^2+||AB+epsilon BA||_F^2`, with support edge `r^3` exactly at
  conference completion.  Its near edge, and indeed every fixed leading
  improvement from its typical value `4r^3+o(r^3)`, has probability
  `exp(-Omega(r^2))`.  Any tilted bridge law producing such an improvement
  pays `Omega(r^2)` relative entropy.  Therefore an `O(r)`-entropy favorable
  basin must use higher-order cancellation in the deeply irregular sector.
- Exact optimized small-query computation also found a gauge-invariant
  difference between the two order-eight minimizer classes at query order
  three (`[12,15,17,17]` versus `[12,15,19,19]`), independently reproduced
  by a symmetry-free MILP.  Its gap is two and carries no asymptotic claim.
- The remaining bridge phase was narrowed information-theoretically.  Any
  entropy-`O(r)` law gaining `Theta(r)` pressure must have quadratic
  effective conditional-information support `s_*=Theta(r^2)`.  This applies
  to the actual Gibbs bridge tilt, not only designed candidate laws.
  Codimension-`O(r)` affine fibres, independent weak templates and exact

  transport plus convex restoration then excludes *every* such row-product

  `o(1)+K_kappa beta sqrt(tau_r/r)`.  Thus a fixed favorable phase needs

## 6. Theory portfolio decision

**Contextual response has reached its relevance ceiling as the dominant
positive route.**  Preserve it as a diagnostic theory: it proved exactly how
much fixed-reference response survives and identifies which information a
candidate state discards.  Do not spend more time on shell, selector, tail,
or switch-label refinements.

Presented rare-event states stay warm but unpromoted.  Adversarial
statistical mechanics remains the sole focused active branch, but only for
one final conditional-singularity test.  Its conference benchmark now has an exact
phase criterion, quadratic regular-sector localization, a quartic entropy
barrier, a conditional-information support law, a complete bounded-density
row-product closure, a quantitative total-correlation barrier, and a no-gain
theorem for every uniformly regular latent-product decomposition.  A finite
phase must be conditionally diffuse, carry `Omega(r)` dependence between
rows, put nonvanishing mass on increasingly singular conditional row laws
or retain irreducible dependence after conditioning, and remain
**irreducibly extensive after every cheap response peel**.  It cannot be
affine, independently biased, localized, gauge-selected, sublinearly
correlated, or a regular de Finetti-type mixture.  This is a Level-4 reset,
not promotion to the original problem; nothing has yet tied the surviving
exceptional sector to pressure-minimizing children.

## 7. Best current route toward `M_n`

Only one chain is defensible enough to recommend:

```text
exact entropy-regularized bridge identity
+
L_basin: for every fixed beta and comparable split, take the two child
minimizers at beta*sqrt(m/N) and beta*sqrt(n/N) used in the exact bridge
identity; an exp(-O(N)) family of exact-sign bridges has parent pressure at
most P_m(beta)+P_n(beta)+O(N^(1-delta))
+
the audited basin criterion / balanced-tree almost-subadditivity
+
convergence of P_n(beta)/n for every fixed beta
+
the uniform soft/ground sandwich as beta -> infinity
=> convergence of M_n/n^(3/2).
```

`L_basin` is weaker than full parent optimization: it asks only for the mass
of a pressure sublevel set and an aggregate threshold, neither an optimal
bridge nor the full spin/coset landscape.  It is also directly falsifiable by
a quadratic-speed lower-deviation theorem.  It must be proved for the exact
contracted-temperature child minimizers; conference examples alone do not
suffice.  The conference analysis now shows exactly where such a positive
law could hide: beyond an `r^(3/2)` edit collar of the operator-regular set,
outside the power-regular pressure-to-quartic reduction, and without any
fixed leading improvement of the typical Gram/quartic coordinates.  It must
also spread `O(r)` conditional information over `Theta(r^2)` coordinates;
sparse/affine support, weak product templates, and adaptive gauges are
rigorously insufficient.  In addition, its irregularity cannot be confined
to any `o(r)`-rank response subspace whose orthogonal bulk admits a regular
low-cost coupling.  Every bounded-Renyi-two iid-row law has such a peel, and
even arbitrary dependence within `o(r)` total correlation of that product
law is harmless.  Hence a surviving candidate must spend `Omega(r)` genuine
cross-row information while retaining the quadratic entrywise support.  If
that dependence is a latent mixture of product laws, uniformly tight
component Renyi-two complexity is also harmless; nonvanishing conditional
singularity is necessary.  The conference benchmark therefore names a
sharper necessary geometry, but no theorem forces or excludes that geometry
for the actual child minimizers.

The old `L_replace` chain is not recommended until it names a concrete
gauge-invariant state.  In its current form it remains too close to an
all-order restatement of the desired transfer.
