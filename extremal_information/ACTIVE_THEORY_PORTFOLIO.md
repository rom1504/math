### Live exact-sign boundary

The certificate dichotomy is resolved negatively.  A periodic PC.3 selector
is `O(j^(-1/2))`-correlated with all `4^j` active top poles.  Targeted sparse
exact-sign flips give, after hollowing,

```math
\max_S D_(SS)=o(1),\qquad Delta_(Maj)=\kappa+o(1),
\qquad ||A_N||=(1+o(1))\sqrt N.
```

This certificate obstruction now upgrades to a contextual-response
collision.  For a fixed labelled field of sufficiently small constant
strength, a spherical upper bound shows that **no** Boolean child spin can
repair the sparse-flip loss.  At `kappa=1/2,lambda=1/10`, the normalized
response gap is rigorously greater than `0.0146`.

The proposed leading-scale query is feasible without a hidden size cost:
the periodic field obeys
`||h_j||_1=sqrt(7/(2pi))N_jsqrt(j)+O(N_j)`.  Repeating every port
`Theta(sqrt(N_j/j))` times uses only `O(sqrt(N_jj))` new vertices, whose
entire internal exact-sign block is subleading.

Closed subroutes:

- exact signs already permit a fixed-arity `5/16` completed-roof coherent
  excess, but the marginal deficit is fixed and full trust optima are not
  separated;
- every diagonal switching of the PC.3 tower has selector deficit at most
  twice its mean product deficit, by a depth-one gapped twirl;
- every monomial tensor/direct-mixture amplification of the fixed bad seed
  reproducing its prescribed selector blockwise retains an odd marginal
  deficit at least `3/32`;
- scalar switching visibility is sufficient but not necessary: PC.3 has
  `nu=N^(-1/4)` while collective twirl coercivity remains constant.

The direct free-shore realization is also closed: its all-positive endpoint
has field `l_1` norm `Omega(N_jj)` and hence super-target
`Omega(N_j^(3/2)sqrt j)` cap.

**Next theorem:** construct a balanced-endpoint exact-sign compiler whose
entire free endpoint language stays at the target scale and retains the
labelled separation, or prove that every sublinear-shore compiler exposing
this field necessarily admits a super-target biased endpoint.  Do not reopen
the closed child-spin or naive free-shore constructions.

## 2. Renormalized rare-event state — keep warm, B+

- **Core object:** extremal hypographs plus the second-speed/tangent mass
  visible to the declared future.
- **Strongest theorem:** Gaussian tangent data
  `(height,mean,covariance,log-power,mass)` form a finite exact convolution
  semigroup with integer all-order recovery.  Product-orbit witness
  complexity is exactly the Cramer rate.
- **Benchmark success:** branching extremes compress to derivative mass for
  unmarked queries; Hamming/code orbit queries recover classical covering
  rates.
- **Falsifier:** genealogy-marked futures require a mass measure, and
  non-Gaussian saddles generate new tangent shapes.
- **Next theorem:** a finite non-Gaussian extremal semigroup with a natural
  model, or a classification no-go.
- **Promotion:** keep warm; it attacks rare extrema orthogonally but has no
  dense-sign realization theorem.

## 3. Adversarial statistical mechanics — keep warm, B

- **Core object:** lower spectral radius/projective cavity for disorder chosen
  adversarially before the spin optimization.
- **Strongest theorem:** finite-state, finite-width discrete disorder has both
  pressure and ground-state-density limits; uniform positivity gives a
  finite approximate cavity state.
- **Benchmark success:** adversarial chains without convexifying the sign
  alphabet.
- **Falsifier:** dense sign interfaces have exponential transfer width and a
  leading contraction loss.
- **Next theorem:** a nonlocal antinorm or interpolation state whose dimension
  remains sub-landscape across growing interfaces.
- **Promotion:** keep warm, below rare-event state.

## 4. Coarse replacement / cut norm — archived, C+

- **Core object:** all-future labelled cut-norm replacement.
- **Strongest theorem:** dense-scale (`n^2`) futures are continuous under cut
  replacement and admit finite block approximants.
- **Falsifier:** at `n^(3/2)` accuracy the required weak-regularity scale can
  have exponentially many blocks; pinned rectangle witnesses are sharp.
- **Promotion test:** a structured exact-sign replacement theorem with
  `o(n^(3/2))` loss and sublinear response state.
