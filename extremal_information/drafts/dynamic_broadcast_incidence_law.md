# A dynamic broadcast-incidence law

Status: rigorous task-local synthesis draft.  This combines the bounded-atom
incidence law with the contracting-fibre/cocycle decomposition.  Its content
is a sharp separation: hidden information placed in contracting centred
fibres is paid once at the forgetting scale, whereas information placed in
visible recurrent cohomology can be broadcast at positive response rate.

## 1. Hidden families on one certified carrier

Fix a strongly connected directed graph `G=(Q,E)`, probability fibres and
Markov kernels satisfying the common transported-law contraction hypotheses
of Theorem 33.1, with coefficient `rho<1`.  Let `z in {0,1}^h` index terminal
dictionaries `u_q^z` and edge-reward dictionaries `a_e^z`.  For a bit `i`
write `z^i=z+e_i` and decompose the differences into

```math
Delta u_q^i=Delta bar u_q^i1+Delta v_q^i,
\qquad
Delta a_e^i=Delta m_e^i1+Delta b_e^i.                \tag{DB.1}
```

Define

```math
U_i=max_q|Delta bar u_q^i|,
R_i=max_q||Delta v_q^i||_2,
B_i=max_e||Delta b_e^i||_2,
M_i=max_e|Delta m_e^i|,
C_i=chi_G(Delta m^i).                                \tag{DB.2}
```

Let `d_L(z,z^i)` be the supremum, over all visible paths of length `L`, of
the `L^2` difference between the two expected accumulated responses.

### Theorem DB.1 (finite-depth and rate decomposition)

For every `z,i,L`,

```math
d_L(z,z^i)
<=U_i+rho^LR_i+{1-rho^L\over1-rho}B_i
  +LC_i+(|Q|-1)M_i.                                  \tag{DB.3}
```

Moreover the asymptotic response-rate pseudometric is exactly

```math
limsup_(L->infinity){d_L(z,z^i)\over L}=C_i.         \tag{DB.4}
```

Thus no amount of centred hidden-state incidence produces a persistent rate
on a fixed strictly contracting carrier.  Positive rate is equivalent to a
hidden change in visible recurrent cohomology.

#### Proof

Apply Theorem 33.1 to the difference of the two dictionaries.  Its centred
identity gives the first three terms.  Loop erasure bounds the scalar path
sum by `LC_i+(|Q|-1)M_i`, proving (DB.3).  Repeating a cycle attaining
`C_i`, and using orthogonality of the constant and centred channels, proves
(DB.4). `square`

## 2. Charging the three channels to atom incidence

Suppose each dictionary is generated by bounded public atoms,

```math
u_q^z=\sum_alpha c_alpha(z)phi_(alpha,q),\qquad
a_e^z=\sum_beta d_beta(z)psi_(beta,e),                \tag{DB.5a}
```

where terminal atoms `alpha` and global edge-dictionary atoms `beta` have
coefficient oscillations `omega_alpha,omega_beta`, dependency sets
`I_alpha,I_beta subseteq[h]`, and maximum component `L^2` norm at most one.
Split every edge atom into its scalar mean and centred part; charge its
coefficient oscillation once to each of those two projections.  Put

```math
J_U=\sum_alpha|I_alpha|omega_alpha,
\qquad J_A=\sum_beta|I_beta|omega_beta.               \tag{DB.5}
```

The deliberately coarse double charge keeps the law representation-free.

### Corollary DB.2 (dynamic incidence budget)

For every `z,L`,

```math
\sum_(i=1)^h d_L(z,z^i)
<=2J_U+(1+{1-rho^L\over1-rho}+L+|Q|-1)J_A.          \tag{DB.6}
```

At the rate level,

```math
\sum_(i=1)^h
 limsup_(L->infinity){d_L(z,z^i)\over L}
<=J_A.                                               \tag{DB.7}
```

Consequently, if every hidden neighbour has persistent response rate at
least `epsilon`, then

```math
h epsilon<=J_A.                                      \tag{DB.8}
```

If there are `E_0` scalar unit atoms, each reading at most `t` hidden bits
and having oscillation at most `2B`, this specializes to

```math
h<={2BtE_0\over epsilon}.                            \tag{DB.9}
```

#### Proof

For each bit, orthogonal projection and the triangle inequality bound
`U_i,R_i` by the sum of terminal-atom oscillations reading that bit, and
`B_i,M_i` by the corresponding edge-dictionary sum.  Since
`C_i=chi_G(Delta m^i)<=M_i`, the same charge controls the rate coordinate.
Summing over bits counts atom `gamma` exactly `|I_gamma|` times.  Substitution
in (DB.3) proves (DB.6)--(DB.7). `square`

The constants in (DB.6) are not advertised as optimal because terminal mean
and centred projection can share one atom.  The scale separation and (DB.7)
are exact.  Singleton fibres on visible cycles attain the rate law (with the
declared zero convention when there is no recurrent cycle), while two-state
eigenmodes attain the terminal and geometric fresh-reward scales.  Cartesian
products make the resources coexist.

## 3. What the law predicts

1. **Weighted automata/control.**  A small static residual shell is reusable
   only after the scalar reward cochain is retained; hidden reward bits in
   that cochain obey an ordinary incidence budget.
2. **Semantic phase refresh.**  Strict mixing can erase a phase label only
   when its observable excess has not reappeared as a visible scalar toll.
3. **Dense compatibility broadcast.**  The Gram construction escapes the
   static local ceiling by dense incidence.  To survive repeated contracting
   composition, the same information must either enter a persistent cycle
   class or be freshly rebroadcast; centred storage alone is geometrically
   forgotten.

This is not a necessary-and-sufficient theorem for discovering a carrier.
The common transported laws and contraction are certified input.  Its new
conclusion is a resource conversion rule once that certificate exists:
static hidden incidence has finite forgetting-scale value, while dynamic
rate has to be explicitly represented in recurrent cohomology.
