# Independent audit: power-orbit and bounded-repair laws

**Scope.** This audit reconstructs BOQ.1--BOQ.2 in
`phase3_blur_orbit_quotient_law.md`, including extended pseudometrics, the
program-power statement, the tropical and matroid applications, and the
convex Hausdorff obstruction.  The argument is short enough that a new
finite verifier would test implementations rather than the theorem, so none
was added.

## Verdict

The two theorems and all three specializations are correct after the
sharpness hypothesis in BOQ.13 was amended to require a common saturated
representative.  BOQ.1 is an exact metric-monoid identity.  BOQ.2 is a valid
geometric sufficient condition for a low-oscillation congruence, with the
claimed no-accumulation decoder.  The tropical and matroid examples are
genuinely different applications; the convex example is a sharp negative
control.

The abstraction is more informative than merely reapplying CSC.1, but in a
precise limited sense.  CSC.1 characterizes every exact closed scalar
summary; BOQ.2 supplies a checkable certificate for one special class of
such summaries.  BOQ.1 additionally treats non-idempotent blurs, which are
outside CSC.1's exact-homomorphism hypothesis.  Neither theorem by itself
proves that the image state is smaller than the original state space.

## 1. BOQ.1 in an extended pseudometric

Associativity and commutativity give, without using the metric,

```math
\prod_{i=1}^m P_bx_i
=\left(\prod_{i=1}^m x_i\right)b^{\star m}.
```

Translation contraction therefore gives the upper bound
`d(b^(star m),b)`.  Setting every `x_i=1` gives the reverse inequality as
an attained value.  This remains valid when the distance is `+infinity` and
when distinct points have pseudodistance zero; no subtraction or separation
axiom is used.

In a composition expression, every occurrence of `P_b` introduces exactly
one central factor `b`.  Thus an expression with `k` occurrences returns
the exact unblurred product times `b^(star k)`, independent of tree shape or
placement of the blurs.  Comparing with one final blur has exact worst-case
cost `d(b^(star k),b)`, again attained at identity inputs.  Taking the
supremum over `k` proves the stated power-orbit radius.

The equivalences are also exact:

- `b star b=b` makes `P_b` both idempotent and multiplicative;
- idempotence at `1` gives `b star b=b`;
- multiplicativity at `(1,1)` gives the same identity.

When this holds, `P_b(M)` is closed, has identity `b`, and `P_b` restricts
to the identity on its image.  Its equality fibers form a congruence because
`P_b(x star y)=P_bx star P_by`.  Calling `P_b` “multiplicative” rather than
a unital endomorphism of the original monoid is important: it generally
sends the original identity `1` to the image identity `b`.

## 2. BOQ.2 and sharpness

For `s=P_bx`, the hypotheses give exactly

```math
0\le F(x)-F(s)\le Ld(x,s)\le LD.
```

Hence every fiber lies in one interval of length `LD`, and midpoint
decoding costs at most `LD/2`.  If `x` and `y` share a summary, then after
joining any common future context their products still share a summary;
closure of `C` lets the same one-shot interval be applied at the root.
There is therefore no hidden per-composition payment.

The revised BOQ.13 is the correct sharpness condition: two admissible points
in one fiber with values `F(s)` and `F(s)+LD` force decoder error at least
`LD/2`.  Without the newly explicit equality
`P_bx_-=P_bx_+=s`, the original displayed endpoint conditions would not
have implied sharpness.

The theorem is still meaningful if `C` is only composition-closed and has no
identity: the image is then an associative summary semigroup.  The identity
is used only in BOQ.1's worst-case test over the ambient monoid.

## 3. Tropical specialization

The extended sup metric

```math
d_\infty(f,g)=\inf\{a:f\le g+a,\ g\le f+a\}
```

is an extended pseudometric, and common min-plus convolution is
nonexpansive: each of the two pointwise inequalities survives taking the
convolution infimum.  The subgroup indicator satisfies
`iota_H star iota_H=iota_H`, and convolution by it is exactly minimization
on `H`-cosets.

For a word profile whose support contains the fixed coordinate basis,
Hamming one-Lipschitzness gives

```math
0\le f(x)-\min_{h\in H}f(x-h)\le d
```

when `H` is generated by `d` coordinate vectors.  These profiles are finite,
so their maxima are one-Lipschitz for `d_infinity`; BOQ.2 yields the claimed
`d/2` decoder.  The class is convolution-closed because support union is the
corresponding word-profile convolution.

For finite nonnegative kernels on a finite group, powers stabilize at the
shortest-path closure `b_*`, so BOQ.1 gives
`sup_k ||b^(star k)-b||_infinity=||b_*-b||_infinity`.  This is exactly the
earlier tropical defect law.  “Subgroup indicators are precisely the exact
idempotent charts” is correct only within the indicator-kernel subclass;
finite-valued subadditive kernels are additional idempotents.

## 4. Matroid specialization

For flats, put

```math
\delta(X,Y)=r(X\vee Y)-r(X),\qquad
d_\vee(X,Y)=\max\{\delta(X,Y),\delta(Y,X)\}.
```

Submodularity proves both required inequalities:

```math
\delta(X\vee U,Y\vee U)\le\delta(X,Y),
\qquad
\delta(X,Z)\le\delta(X,Y)+\delta(Y,Z).
```

Applying the first inequality in both orientations proves common-join
contraction; applying the second in both orientations proves the triangle
inequality for `d_vee`.  Zero distance forces equality because a proper
inclusion of flats strictly increases rank.  Thus this is a genuine metric,
not a response metric inserted by assumption.

Residual rank is one-Lipschitz, adjoining a rank-`d` flat moves any state by
at most `d`, and it can only lower residual rank.  BOQ.2 therefore recovers
the fixed-flat contraction quotient with error `d/2`.  The bottom flat and
`W` share saturation `W` and differ in residual rank by exactly `d`, so the
constant is sharp, including for matroids with loops.

## 5. Convex Hausdorff specialization

For compact convex bodies, support functions give

```math
d_H(mK,K)
=\sup_{\|u\|_*\le1}|(m-1)h_K(u)|.
```

Because `0 in K`, `h_K` is nonnegative, and duality gives
`sup h_K=max_(x in K)||x||`.  Hence the exact value is `(m-1)R(K)` in an
arbitrary finite-dimensional normed space.  Taking all exact input bodies
to be `{0}` attains this defect, while common Minkowski addition supplies
the upper bound.  The orbit is bounded only for `K={0}`.

## 6. Scope and novelty judgment

BOQ.2 does not improve the universal optimal-index formula in CSC.1.  Its
contribution is constructive: a central idempotent, an external repair
metric, and a one-sided Lipschitz order prove all future fiber-width bounds
without enumerating the future-response table.  BOQ.1 contributes the
separate exact law explaining when repeated approximate use saturates and
when it accumulates.

The claimed two-model scope is fair.  The tropical chart uses min-plus
profiles and word-metric coset diameter; the matroid chart uses flat join and
rank-increment geometry.  They share the newly isolated algebraic mechanism
rather than merely two names for the same object.  Still, the result should
be advertised as a reusable sufficient mechanism, not a classification of
all useful summaries: triggered contractions and zero-separating matroid
congruences already lie outside the principal-blur class.

One wording change remains advisable.  CRL.2 proves only that
monotonicity/idempotence alone cannot force compression.  It does not prove
that a condition *as strong as* bounded-repair common saturation is
necessary.  The safe conclusion is that additional structure is necessary
and that bounded-repair saturation is one sufficient form.
