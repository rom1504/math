## What was actually predicted

The framework did not predict a universal small state. It predicted which
resource each benchmark must pay:

```math
exact reuse = response-exposing quotient + forward congruence,
```

while approximate reuse replaces the second term by summable suffix gain.
The Ising exposure gadget proves the first term cannot shrink under arbitrary
strip futures. Restricted alphabets shrink the second term by residual
minimization. The Cantor system proves that a small static image cannot stand
in for either.

## What remains classical

The transfer update, weighted partition refinement, and contraction-based
symbolic abstraction are classical. The campaign's additional conclusions
are quantitative and comparative:

- the exact strip contextual metric;
- the strict width-three full-dimensional response certificate;
- the exact finite-horizon defect slope after quotient failure;
- the contraction-weighted response-tree entropy transform and its exact
  affine extremizer;
- the finite predictive compactness theorem; and
- the Helly-sharp obstruction to local tie-path assembly.
