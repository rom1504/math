## Director judgment

The contextual-response program should **continue as the leading branch of
a portfolio**, not monopolize the project and not yet be applied directly to
the minima `M_n`.  It passed the benchmark ladder, produced quantitative
multi-model laws, found extensive information barriers for generic dense
bridges, and—most importantly—constructed a growing dense exact-sign
interface with a strict sub-landscape state.  Robustness now survives both
fixed-label tensoring under `Delta_tau` and growing Cartesian composition
under a factored relative certificate `D_i<=delta_iG_i`.

The ceiling is equally precise.  Pairwise geometry, individual product
deficits, and even the whole currently exposed defect table can miss joint
coherence that future composition reveals.  The successful structured
family is an exceptional affine pole algebra on an unbalanced
`Theta(sqrt n)` shore; it neither transfers a liminf object to all orders nor
selects such an algebra for near-minimizers.  Its robust theorem is relative
to the spectral roof `rn/2`; unless that roof is itself within
`o(n^(3/2))` of the Boolean cap, it does not approximate a near-minimizer's
true response baseline.

The final exact-sign experiment resolves a contextual dichotomy rather than
the original minimization problem.  Sparse selector-targeted flips preserve all
`4^j` active PC.3 product poles to `o(1)` and the actual `sqrt(N)` operator
scale while inflicting constant loss on their joint selector.  A sharp field-
size law shows that this damaged channel can be queried at leading scale with
only `o(N)` new vertices.  A two-channel spherical relaxation proves a fixed
gap for the **full Boolean trust response** under that labelled field.  The
naive free-shore lift fails because an all-positive endpoint creates
`Omega(N^(3/2)sqrt(log N))` energy, but a rowwise microcanonical compiler
balances every endpoint.  Its uniform affine law closes both endpoint and
child-spin repair and yields two all-spins-free exact-sign parents separated
at the leading scale.  Neither parent is known to minimize its order.

## 3. New mathematics

Only the campaign's principal theorem-level results are listed here.

1. **Dynamic response law.**  On a common contracting carrier, terminal
   error decays geometrically, fresh centred reward costs a geometric shell,
   and persistent scalar reward is exactly recurrent graph cohomology.  The
   corresponding covering exponents are sharp.  Bounded-delay residuals
   separately give an exact support/potential carrier and an empty-core
   counterexample.
2. **Bridge information transition.**  Fixed-rank bridges have an exact
   associative roof with exponential-in-rank but system-size-independent
   state.  A matching, a generic dense sign bridge, and complete quadratic
   children force extensive response information at the target scale.
3. **Bounded-cap compilation and holonomy.**  A `sqrt(n)`-width regular-
   Hadamard anti-pin embeds an exponential contextual code into exact
   bounded-cap parents.  Common-factor orientation networks have an exact
   one-bit-per-edge quotient; joining pieces creates precisely relative
   orientation and cycle-flux bits.
   The universal-pin boundary is sharp at the architecture level: a linear-
   order exact pin is possible only after a quadratic calibration, low-cap
   universal locking fails, while a query-dependent bounded-cap anti-pin
   succeeds.
4. **Scale/phase laws.**  Regular-Hadamard response sets admit phasewise
   limits and summable-recovery theorems, but one explicit exact-sign
   hierarchy retains a nonconstant mantissa profile.  A Doeblin-style phase
   refresh is sufficient and quantitatively obstructed in the bad family.
5. **Boolean port Fourier algebra.**  The exact labelled linear response is
   invertible convolution of the projective row histogram.  Its exact
   feature dimension is `2^(p-1)`, while fixed-error response entropy is
   sharply `Theta_eta(p)` bits.  Mergeable union reservoirs, independent
   tensor occurrences, and uniform-component forgetting give three distinct
   reuse laws.
6. **Scalable information collision.**  Equal full Gram--Rayleigh states in
   one regular-Hadamard family have Boolean trust responses separated by
   `rn/8` before completion and by `rn/8-O(n)` after a common exact-sign
   completion.  Thus the obstruction is not merely an integrality gap of one
   decoder.
7. **Exact and robust product synchronization.**  Odd Fourier-product
   closure makes one majority selector pay the child and every port jointly.
   It yields a logarithmic-arity dense family with `sqrt(n)` histogram bins.
   Approximate closure is controlled by the intrinsic joint defect
   `Delta_tau`, which is tensor-subadditive; growing Cartesian pole algebras
   instead use `D<=delta G`, with survival `prod(1-delta_i)`.
8. **Dynamic coherence falsifier.**  Even the complete one-step endpoint-
   defect table is not a tensor congruence (`48/64` versus `47/64` after one
   composition).  Approximate error algebra can therefore be strictly
   smaller than an exact contextual quotient.
9. **Marginal-defect blindness and sign-specific repair.**  At
   `p=(log_2N)/2`, two generated Walsh pole systems agree on their complete
   generator block and every individual `O(1/p)` product deficit, yet their
   majority selector losses are `1-2/pi+o(1)` and `O(1/p)`.  Twirling proves
   the positive converse `D<=(d+eta)G`, where `eta` is an approximate-
   equivariance/commutator defect.  Exact signs do not remove the phenomenon
   at fixed arity: at the completed Hadamard roof, an order-16 five-port seed
   has every marginal deficit `3/16`, majority defect `1/2`, and a tensor-
   preserved `5/16` excess.  Hollow diagonal deletion preserves the Boolean
   energies; at a valid hollow contraction roof the excess tends to `5/16`.
   This is a selector certificate, not separated full trust optima, and its
   marginal error does not vanish.  Conversely, switching visibility proves
   the sign-specific bound
   `D<=(d/nu+sum_i sqrt(2k_i)/r)G`, sharply on hollow PC.3 and exactly on a
   non-Hadamard Cayley signing.  PC.3 tensor visibility is exactly
   `nu_j=N_j^(-1/4)`, so persistent symmetry alone does not keep the
   certificate dimension-free.
10. **Collective-twirl and seed-amplification no-gos.**  Every diagonal
    switching of the full PC.3 tensor tower obeys a depth-independent bound:
    selector deficit is at most twice the mean active-product deficit.  The
    mechanism is a pole-conjugation twirl with exact spectrum `{-1,0,1}`;
    it succeeds even though scalar visibility decays.  Separately, arbitrary
    monomial/direct-mixture amplification of the fixed five-port bad seed
    that reproduces its prescribed selector blockwise leaves some odd channel
    with deficit at least `3/32`; in the common-
    factor subclass, `d_full>=(3/8)d_sel-(11/8)eta`.  Thus neither diagonal
    switching nor natural dilution of that seed can produce the desired
    vanishing-marginal exact-sign falsifier.
11. **Sparse-flip exact-sign obstruction.**  An explicit periodic endpoint
    in the PC.3 tower is uniformly `O(j^(-1/2))`-correlated with all `4^j`
    active product poles.  Flipping a `kappa/sqrt(N)` fraction of the
    off-diagonal edges positive for that endpoint produces hollow exact
    signings with actual operator norm `(1+o(1))sqrt(N)`, every declared
    product-pole deficit `o(1)`, and endpoint-selector deficit
    `kappa+o(1)`.  This escapes both the diagonal-switching twirl and the
    fixed-seed monomial-dilution no-go.  It is a scalable **certificate**
    obstruction.  The associated periodic
    field has the sharp scale
    `||h_j||_1=sqrt(7/(2pi))N_jsqrt(j)+O(N_j)`.  Repeating each port
    `Theta(sqrt(N_j/j))` times therefore exposes this channel at
    `Theta(N_j^(3/2))` cost using only `O(sqrt(N_jj))` new vertices; their
    entire internal completion is subleading.  Finally, a spherical trust
    bound handles both quadratic signs and upgrades the example to a full
    labelled-response collision; for `kappa=1/2,lambda=1/10` its normalized
    gap is greater than `0.0146`.  Free endpoint switching in an ordinary
    appended parent remains outside this theorem.  The naive repeated shore
    blows up by `sqrt j`, but a rowwise microcanonical sign compiler realizes
    the target field exactly with global cross cap only `o(N^(3/2))` above
    its norm.  Its stronger uniform affine endpoint law proves endpoint
    stability and yields two unconstrained exact-sign parents whose caps
    differ by `(delta+o(1))N^(3/2)`.  This is a physical response collision,
    not a comparison of minimizing signings.
12. **Factorized phase law.**  For independent pole factors, the maximum of
    the entire endpoint histogram is within `sqrt(sum q_t^2)/p` of the
    scalar first-moment phase.  Together with relative synchronization this
    proves a restricted exact-sign thermodynamic limit when the mass and
    first-moment phases converge and the relative defect vanishes.  Repeated
    PC.3 gives `3/4`, while alternating mean-`1/2` and mean-zero exact factors
    give a zero-defect signing sequence with cap subsequences `1` and `1/2`.
13. **Orthogonal control cases.**  Finite-width adversarial discrete disorder
    has pressure and ground-state limits via a lower spectral radius and
    projective cavity.  Separately, Gaussian tangent data form a finite exact
    convolution algebra with integer recovery; quartic/non-Gaussian saddles
    prove that this closure is special rather than universal.

## 4. Near-original bridge hierarchy

- **Fixed rank:** solved at the response level by a finite-dimensional upper
  roof; fixed-error state is exponential only in rank.
- **Slowly growing rank:** generic spectral truncation reaches a linear-rank
  ceiling.  The positive exception is algebraic: `p=(log_2 n)/2+1` generated
  ports close through `sqrt n` affine poles and retain a sublinear exact
  state.
- **Sparse bridges:** sparsity alone is not compression.  A degree-one
  matching can carry exponentially many macroscopic responses; a genuinely
  small live separator is the useful parameter.  A vertex-cover derivation
  gives a candidate `2^k` bridge table, but its independent audit remains
  pending.
- **Structured dense bridges:** common-factor orientation and affine pole
  algebras give exact associative quotients.  Robust relative synchronization
  survives vanishing accumulated factor perturbations without a false
  dimension loss.  For factorized affine-pole products with
  `sqrt(sum_t q_t^2)/p -> 0`, convergent mass and first-moment phases, and
  vanishing relative defect, that phase computes the max response.  Exact
  closure without phase convergence still permits macroscopic oscillation.
  A separate fixed permutation-orbit class closes through a magnetization
  quotient, showing that pole algebras are not the only positive mechanism.
  The new switching-visibility theorem supplies a second exact-sign positive
  mechanism: a projectively closed multiplicity-free action closes relative
  coherence at cost `1/nu` plus a signed edge-orbit error.  The five-port
  tensor seed shows that sign integrality without such visibility still
  permits a leading prescribed-selector gap.
  Two broad amplification routes are now closed.  A uniform gapped
  conjugation twirl forces coherence for every diagonal switching of PC.3,
  while its positive-roof monomial quotient prevents every tensor/direct-
  mixture dilution reproducing the prescribed seed selector from making all
  marginals vanish.  Neither no-go is universal: the sparse-flip construction
  gives exact signings at the correct operator scale with all active-product
  deficits `o(1)` and a constant prescribed-selector deficit.  A spherical
  trust bound and balanced compiler further show that neither child-spin nor
  free-endpoint optimization repairs one such channel.  It therefore rules
  out deriving semantic NMSS from individual product deficits alone; the
  remaining distinction is whether this one physical bit amplifies and
  whether any analogue is forced on near-minimizers.
- **Random/arbitrary dense bridges:** response packing is extensive at the
  `n^(3/2)` scale.  Cut-norm replacement only helps at the coarser `n^2`
  scale.

The results indicate that the transition is not rank or sparsity in
isolation, but whether future-visible channel coherence is generated by a
small algebra that remains a congruence—or at least carries a composable
error budget.  This is an organizing law, not a proved if-and-only-if
classification.

## 7. Two candidate eventual programs for the signing problem

These are the best scoped reconnection candidates found, not two lemmas
already known to be mathematically easier than the original optimization.
Program A is formally query-narrower; Program B is an architecture
falsifier.  The campaign found no second route meeting the stronger
demonstrably-easier standard.

### Program A: generated selector synchronization

Known product-Fourier inversion, fixed-scale histogram coresets, and
exact-sign completion, plus the following semantic lemma:

```math
\boxed{\text{semantic NMSS lemma}}
```

For each `n`, predeclare a family `F_n` of at most `exp(o(n))` generated
`p_n=(log_2n)/2+O(1)` port systems, independent of the input signing.  Along
a near-liminf sequence, one member, named by an `o(n)`-bit index and chosen
without a maximizing-spin table, has selector witnesses satisfying

```math
\max_\epsilon\{Q(A_n)-|H_(A_n)(x_\epsilon)|\}=o(n^{3/2}),
```

while its repeated-shore field table has fixed positive `n^(3/2)`
oscillation.

This would give an `o(n)`-bit plus `O(sqrt(n)log n)`-bit leading-scale
response carrier for actual liminf objects.  The lemma's conclusion is
strictly query-narrower than the original optimization: it controls only
`Theta(sqrt n)` declared selector words and one logarithmic-rank continuation
language, and asserts no cross-order recurrence.  It is **not yet proved
mathematically easier**.  The robust spectral theorem supplies one possible
proof mechanism only in classes whose spectral roof is already tight; it is
not silently assumed here.  The sparse-flip theorem additionally rules out
  proving this semantic lemma from near-optimality of the individual generated
  product poles, even under exact signs and the correct operator scale.  The
  one-channel compiler also shows that full-response repair can fail, but it
  does not select that channel for near-minimizers; a successful proof still
  needs a near-minimizer-specific collective state.

### Program B: a decisive coherence lower bound

Known generic dense-bridge packing, the equal-`(G,R)` collision, and the
endpoint-table noncongruence, plus the following architecture falsifier.
For a fixed sequence `eta_n -> 0`, let

```math
\mathcal C_n(\eta_n)=
\{A:Q(A)\le M_n+\eta_n n^{3/2}\}.
```

Declare in advance the two-layer generated-port continuation language
`L_n^(2)` of total shore `O(sqrt n)`, and define its normalized contextual
pseudometric

```math
d_n^{(2)}(A,A')=
{1\over n^{3/2}}\sup_{C\in L_n^{(2)}}
|\operatorname {OPT}(A\circ C)-\operatorname {OPT}(A'\circ C)|.
```

The precise candidate statement is:

```math
\boxed{\text{near-minimizer coherence dichotomy}}
```
For some fixed `epsilon,c>0` and infinitely many `n`,

```math
\operatorname {Pack}
 (\mathcal C_n(\eta_n),d_n^{(2)},\epsilon)\ge \exp(cn). \tag{S.1}
```

Consequently any deterministic reusable carrier with response distortion
below `epsilon/2` has at least `exp(cn)` states.  This would not decide
`M_n`; it would rigorously exclude **sublinear carriers for that declared
architecture**, not all compositional proofs, and redirect the project
toward a different state or the rare-event branch.  It may require proving
the existence of exponentially many inequivalent near-minimizers and is not
known easier than the original problem.

Thus Program A is one candidate reconnection lemma and Program B is one
architecture falsifier; no second route yet meets the full
demonstrably-easier standard.
