# Final director audit: actual-child bridge-law campaign

## Verdict

The campaign made genuine Level-5 structural progress on the bridge law
induced by the actual contracted-temperature children.  It did **not**
produce a Level-6 pressure recurrence.  The correct endpoint is a
three-way classification of individually tight rows, not a proof that the
remaining obstruction is purely irreducible dependence.

## 1. Strongest theorem about the actual-child law

The strongest result is the combination of Theorems 37.27 and 37.32.
For every pair of exact minimizing children one can choose the row direction
and bias-canceling orientation so that the canonical row factor has the
optimizer-specific bound

```math
D_\infty(r_i\Vert U)\le
\lambda\{\delta_n(t)+\log2\}
\le\lambda(\beta^2/2+\log2).
```

In the same physical scaling, every hybrid conditional row and every factor
of the globally optimal row-product shadow is within `O(1)` Renyi two of
the canonical factor.  Moreover, if the canonical mismatch `J` is linear,
a positive density of these individually regular rows must carry scaled
marginal drift or row-versus-rest mutual information; if the best product
repairs a linear part of `J`, a positive density of its regular factors must
retune.  Therefore non-tight conditional components and sparse exceptional
rows are rigorously excluded.  This is optimizer-specific only in the
balanced `D_infinity` envelope; the conditional `D_2` and localization
inequalities hold more generally but apply exactly to the actual law.

The sector--Gram carrier in Theorem 37.31 is the best strict compression:
four real coordinates per child determine the first nonzero `u^4`
interaction tangent.  It is not uniform at physical scale and is not a
reusable composition state.

## 2. Sharpest falsifier

The sharpest falsifier is the certified pair of order-eight exact thermal
minimizer classes paired with the same order-two minimizing child.  The two
experiments have the same complete pressure/entropy and radial data and the
same complete canonical one-row quotient `(s,c)`, but their joint tangent
coefficients are `20` and `12` (and their low-channel canonical coefficients
are `7\lambda^2` and `5\lambda^2`).  Thus even exact scalar and one-row data
of actual minimizing children do not determine the joint bridge response.
This is a fixed-order/small-channel falsifier, not a contracted-order
asymptotic obstruction.  The triangle/edge sector-preference reversal is a
separate exact warning that neutral deletion optimality is not contextual
optimality.

## 3. Audit of `L_balanced-joint`

`L_balanced-joint` is narrower in **descriptive scope** than the campaign's
starting request: component support, sector-bias escape, and sparse-row
explanations have been removed.  It is not yet a demonstrated strict
lower-information reduction.  The quantities

```math
J=D(r^{\otimes m}\Vert q),
\qquad
\mathcal I^{\leftarrow}
=\inf_{P\ \text{ row product}}D(P\Vert q)
```

can retain the full joint bridge likelihood and the full row-product
variational problem.  Requiring target relevance adds another comparison
with the optimized parent.  The phrase “from an observable strictly smaller
than the full bridge/external-field table” is a necessary admissibility
condition, not an already supplied observable or reduction theorem.

There is also a material trichotomy issue.  `J=\Omega(N)` need not imply
the irreducible branch (ES.42): it may be repaired by a best product through
positive-density coherent retuning.  Accordingly the SML must retain all
three outcomes:

1. `J=o(N)` with a summable rate;
2. `\mathcal I^{\leftarrow}=\Omega(N)` certified by a named compressed
   actual-child observable; or
3. `J-\mathcal I^{\leftarrow}=\Omega(N)` with the coherent direction of
   the positive-density retuning certified without solving the full product
   variational problem.

Calling the present SML “purely joint” or saying the only residual is
“irreducible joint compatibility” overstates Theorem 37.27.  The accurate
phrase is “collective joint dependence and/or coherent regular-factor
retuning.”

## 4. Level judgment and next campaign

No credible Level-5-to-6 route has yet been established.  The tangent
sector--Gram state is a credible clue, but the missing order-uniform tail
theorem could fail through higher parity structure, and target relevance is
independent.  The one justified next campaign is therefore a bounded audit
of the balanced joint phase: seek one named compressed actual-child
observable that decides the three-way alternative above at physical scale
and simultaneously prices the balanced orientation's target loss.  Another
generic concentration bound, one-row quotient, or fixed-order moment
expansion is a strike.  Two such strikes should freeze adversarial
statistical mechanics and promote rare-event/renormalization, exactly as the
frontier stopping rule requires.
