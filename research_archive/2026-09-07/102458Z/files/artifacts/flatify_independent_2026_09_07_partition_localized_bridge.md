# An actual sign bridge localized on arbitrary coarse partitions

Date: 2026-09-07. Proved finite operation and all-order asymptotic
consequence. This generalizes the elementary dephased-Hadamard
fixed-center construction. It does not supply a partition capturing all
high-energy states of actual optimizing children.

## 1. Finite construction, including unequal final block sizes

Partition the left and right sets of n physical vertices into arbitrary
atoms, with at most R atoms on each side. Choose a Hadamard order k.
Split each atom into chunks of size at most k. If their numbers are
s_L,s_R, choose a Hadamard order L>=max(s_L,s_R). Pad every nonempty
chunk to size k and add empty chunks until both sides have L chunks.
Put N=Lk and D=N-n, the common number of padding coordinates per side.

Let H_L and H_k be dephased Hadamards, so their first rows and columns
are positive and H_k 1=k e_1. In the padded chunk coordinates use

    C=H_L tensor H_k,

and restrict to the original n rows and n columns, obtaining a full
rectangular sign matrix B. The row and column chunks can be associated
with their physical vertices independently. Exactly

    ||C||op=sqrt(N),       ||B||op<=sqrt(N).              (1)

For a physical spin x, write

    A_x=sum_{nonempty left chunks J} (sum_{i in J}x_i)^2/|J|,

and define A_y using the right chunks. These are the squared norms of
the orthogonal projections onto the ORIGINAL chunk-constant spaces;
0<=A_x,A_y<=n.

Then the construction satisfies the following simultaneous bound for
EVERY pair of physical spins:

    |x^T B y|
      <=sqrt(N) sqrt(n-A_x+D) sqrt(n-A_y+D)+3N sqrt(L).   (2)

The square roots may be replaced by sqrt(min(n,n-A_x+D)) and the analogous
right term, but the looser displayed form is convenient.

## 2. Proof of the finite bound

Extend x,y by zero on padding coordinates, giving X,Y in [-1,1]^N with
norm sqrt(n). Let P project onto vectors constant on each padded chunk
of size k. Both PX and PY remain in the cube. Dephasing the inner
Hadamard gives

    C(PY) supported on L coordinates,
    C^T(PX) supported on L coordinates.

Their Euclidean norms are at most sqrt(Nn), by (1). Thus every term
containing a block-constant factor obeys

    |X^T C(PY)|, |(PX)^T C Y|, |(PX)^T C(PY)|
       <=sqrt(LNn)<=N sqrt(L).                         (3)

Expanding X=(I-P)X+PX and Y=(I-P)Y+PY and using (3) leaves

    |X^T C Y|<=sqrt(N)||(I-P)X||||(I-P)Y||+3N sqrt(L).  (4)

For an original chunk J of size s<=k,

    (sum_J x)^2/s-(sum_J x)^2/k
       <=s(1-s/k)<=k-s.

Summing over chunks bounds the projection-energy loss caused by padding
by D. Therefore ||(I-P)X||^2<=n-A_x+D and similarly for Y. Substitute
in (4) to obtain (2). Empty padded chunks contribute zero throughout.

## 3. All-order asymptotic version

Suppose a sequence of original partitions has at most R_n atoms on
either side. If one can choose Hadamard orders k_n such that

    k_n->infinity,       R_n k_n=o(n),

then s_L,s_R=n/k_n+O(R_n). Choose outer Hadamard orders L_n just above
max(s_L,s_R), with L_n/max(s_L,s_R)->1. Such choices use only the already
established multiplicative density of Hadamard orders generated by 2
and 12; they do not use convergence of M_n. The construction gives

    N/n->1,      D/n->0,      L_n/n->0.

Consequently (2), uniformly in all physical spins, becomes

    |x^T B_n y|/n^(3/2)
       <=sqrt[(1-A_x/n)(1-A_y/n)]+o(1).                 (5)

Uniformity at zero residual follows, for example, from
sqrt(u+e)sqrt(v+e)<=sqrt(uv)+2sqrt(e)+e for 0<=u,v<=1.
The explicit additive term 3N sqrt(L)/n^(3/2) tends to zero because
k_n=N/L_n tends to infinity.

This is an actual full-sign construction at every sufficiently large
original order n, not a statement only on the padded subsequence.

## 4. Simultaneous localization of prescribed Boolean centers

Given r_n prescribed Boolean centers on the left, partition the vertices
by their joint sign patterns; do the same independently on the right.
There are at most 2^(r_n) atoms on each side. Every center is constant
on every resulting chunk. Hence A_center=n in (2), and every prescribed
center obeys

    sup_y |center_left^T B_n y|=o(n^(3/2)),
    sup_x |x^T B_n center_right|=o(n^(3/2)).             (6)

This handles any fixed number of centers. More generally, for every
fixed epsilon>0 it handles

    r_n<=(1-epsilon)log_2 n,

by choosing k_n a power of two of order n^(epsilon/2). Then
2^(r_n)k_n<=O(n^(1-epsilon/2))=o(n). The outer order still tends to
infinity, so its relative padding cost vanishes.

No orthogonality or independence among the prescribed centers is required.
The sign-pattern partition, not a fictitious simultaneous orthogonal
rotation of the Boolean cube, realizes their localization.

## 5. What would imply an original-value composition

For actual children A,D, the parent cap is exactly

    max_{x,y} [|H_A(x)+H_D(y)|+|x^T B_n y|].

Thus a sufficient original-value input is a pair of partitions above
for which EVERY child energy is controlled by its block-constant
projection, for example

    |H_A(x)|/n^(3/2)<=c A_x/n+o(1),
    |H_D(y)|/n^(3/2)<=c A_y/n+o(1),                    (7)

uniformly in spins. Combining (5) with (7), and using
sqrt((1-u)(1-v))<=1-(u+v)/2, bounds the parent by

    [1+(c-1/2)(u+v)+o(1)]n^(3/2).

For c<=1/2 this is at most (1+o(1))n^(3/2). In fact (7) is IMPOSSIBLE
for a growing sequence of full-sign children with affordable partitions.
Apply the construction to the same child twice. Its conclusion would
give M_(2n)/(2n)^(3/2)<=1/(2sqrt(2))+o(1), contrary to the banked
universal lower bound c_lower=0.4333221116640807. More quantitatively,
if a uniform additive per-child defect d is allowed in (7), this argument
forces

    d>=sqrt(2)*c_lower-1/2 > 0.1127.

This obstruction was noticed independently by the adversary auditor;
it applies to growing affordable partitions, not merely one center.
More general exact energy/projection profiles can be substituted without
replacing the original absolute-cap objective by width.

The elementary weak-regularity energy argument at discrepancy scale
n^(3/2) may require order n cuts, not the O(log n) centers or affordable
atom count above. No such structural input has been proved here. The
operation is positive and all-order, but does not establish convergence.
