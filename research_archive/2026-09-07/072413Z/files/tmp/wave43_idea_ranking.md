# Wave 43 idea ranking

Evidence basis: README, STEERING cutoff Wave 39, and ledger Updated frontier
through Wave 42.  This is scratch planning, not a ledger claim.

Ranking criteria: a result must connect to an exact sufficient target, survive
the recorded finite obstructions, be falsifiable, and operate at the project
scale rather than merely rename an existing obligation.

1. **Strict mixed-pressure inverse theorem.**  At
   `theta=Theta(n^(-3/4-c))`, prove the one-input bound (10.1133) for some
   exact-minimizer, low-row center by exploiting the maximization/minimization
   over centers.  Equivalently, show that failure for every eligible center
   forces a contradiction with optimality or with the rare-coverage converse.
   This is the shortest route from a genuinely nonlinear new lemma to the bare
   tail.
2. **Weighted colored-codegree minimax package.**  Optimize the exact global-map
   dual (10.1141), with full deterministic code length and fiber entropy, and
   seek a theorem or a scalable obstruction that treats row, code, and colored
   exclusive-fiber disagreement simultaneously.  This is the sharpest current
   implementation of the leading complement kernel.
3. **Integrated context-mass harmonic theorem.**  Use the interpolation Gibbs
   mass, rather than pointwise score bounds, to charge reveal, affinity, and
   cross-level costs from (10.1144)--(10.1147).  Seek an exact integral
   inequality for signed migration; the `A_9` wall is a required test case.
4. **Strict pressure by multi-center aggregation.**  Average or soft-max the
   mixed pressures of all low-row centers and use exact-minimizer exchange to
   turn aggregate positivity into one good center.  This is distinct from a
   fixed-center moment bound but less direct than item 1.
5. **Colored-codegree falsifier construction.**  Build scalable actual
   minimizers whose affordable incidence fibers force either excessive
   code-length or colored disagreement.  A valid family would decisively prune
   the leading structured implementation while leaving the bare target open.
6. **Tropical-envelope control of harmonic migration.**  Prove that large
   endpoint score slopes can contribute only near crossings of affine ground
   envelopes and bound the total crossing mass by endpoint free-energy or
   entropy.  Promising, but translating finite tropical gaps uniformly to the
   project temperature is delicate.
7. **Favorable-distance / min-plus scaffold.**  Construct a metric or directed
   distance between exact minimizers and restricted targets whose triangle
   inequality yields approximate composition at `o(n^(3/2))`.  This is the
   most independent live fallback, but presently lacks an exact bridge to the
   bare favorable-mass target.
8. **Bare-tail contrapositive from rare coverage.**  Assume every low-row cut
   has too little favorable mass and combine covering multiplicity with
   minimizer incidence counts to contradict the known abundance of low-row
   orientations.  This avoids a structured selector but needs a new
   dependence-sensitive counting inequality.
9. **Nonlinear cavity reward / constant shortfall.**  Couple two or more
   insertions so cancellation produces a square-root reward.  Retained as a
   lower-ranked independent route because all linear puncture identities
   collapse to the original decrement.
10. **Compactness of normalized extremizers.**  Embed signings/cut profiles in
    a graphon-like limit and prove continuity of the normalized optimum under
    blow-up.  This might explain convergence but currently has no topology
    controlling the `n^(3/2)` discrepancy, so it is not assigned.

Selected independent assignments: items 1 (including item 4 as a fallback),
2 (including item 5 as a falsification branch), and 3 (including item 6 as its
mechanism).  Item 7 remains the first replacement if an assigned route closes.
