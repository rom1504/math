# Center-conditioned sign bridges: arbitrary center profiles

Date: 2026-09-07. Proved extension of
`flatify_independent_2026_09_07_joint_gaussian_bridge_sector.md`.
The center need not have Gaussian coefficients. This controls a dominant
noisy-cloud sector for every actual center, but not every spin in the cloud.

Use exactly the bipartite rank-two full-sign ensemble, n=mk, k=2m,
C/sqrt(n)=UKV^T, and alpha=4/5 from that artifact. Set s=3/5.

## 1. The appropriate reference law retains the actual center

For a physical center row x0_i, write z_l=(F^T x0_i)_l/sqrt(k), and let
rho_i be the symmetrized empirical law of its k coefficients. In particular
E_rho Z^2=1. Assign to a physical pair (x0_i,x_i) the reference quotient law

    law of [(Z,alpha Z+sG)],   Z~rho_i, G~N(0,1) independent,              (1)

on R^2/{+1,-1}. Define the pair's squared profile error as quotient W_2^2
between its actual k-vector word and (1); average this over fibres on each
side, as in the preceding Gaussian-center theorem.

The reference row is generated by taking a uniform common signed
permutation of the EXACT center word, then adding independent Gaussian
noise in the second coordinate. Its center profile is not approximated
or discarded. This row law is invariant under simultaneous signed
coordinate permutations, so the same quotient matching and group
symmetrization couple it to the exact physical construction law.

## 2. Uniform empirical transport approximation, with paid tails

For any k-word of center magnitudes c_l>=0 with k^(-1)sum c_l^2=1,
consider the random empirical law of (c_l,G_l), with independent standard
Gaussians G_l, and the reference law empirical(c_l) tensor N(0,1).
Uniformly over all such words,

    E W_2^2 <= C k^(-1/3) log(k+1).                       (2)

Here is a direct proof. Separate c_l>R; their fraction is at most R^(-2).
On this part couple each center coordinate identically, and couple its
single observed Gaussian to an independent Gaussian. The expected cost
is at most 2/R^2, with NO error in the center coordinate. On [0,R], bin
the centers into J<=1+R/h intervals of width h. In each bin couple the
observed Gaussian empirical law to N(0,1), and move center coordinates
only within that bin. The expected cost is at most

    C[h^2+R^(-2)+log(k+1)sqrt((1+R/h)/k)].                (3)

For completeness, k_j Gaussian observations have expected W_2^2 at most
C log(k_j+1)/sqrt(k_j): truncate the Gaussian at sqrt(2log(k_j+1)),
integrate the empirical-CDF variance to bound W_1, use W_2^2<=diameter W_1
on the truncated interval, and bound both Gaussian second-moment tails.
Weighting this by k_j/k and using sum sqrt(k_j)<=sqrt(Jk) proves (3).
Take R=k^(1/6), h=k^(-1/6) to obtain (2).

The linear map (c,g)->(c,alpha c+sg) has operator norm at most sqrt(2).
Passing to the antipodal quotient cannot increase transport cost. Hence
Markov gives an empirical-reference event of probability at least 1/2
at epsilon_k=O(k^(-1/6)sqrt(log(k+1))), uniformly over every center word.
Conditioning independently in the 2m fibres still costs at most 2^(2m).
The finite coupling errors (3) in the preceding artifact are unchanged.

## 4. The enlarged sector is typical for every actual noisy center

This typicality statement is separate from, and is NOT needed for, the
uniform sector construction theorem. Fix ANY center row and ANY Hadamard
basis. Let x_l=x0_l eta_l with independent P(eta_l=-1)=1/10. Then

    (F^T x)_j/sqrt(k)=alpha z_j+s V_j,
    V_j=(s sqrt(k))^(-1)sum_l F_lj x0_l(eta_l-alpha).

Each V_j has mean zero and variance one. For distinct j,j', orthogonality
gives Cov(V_j,V_j')=0. A smooth-test Lindeberg replacement gives convergence
of V_j to a standard Gaussian and of (V_j,V_j') to independent Gaussians,
uniformly in the indices, basis, and center: each coefficient is bounded
by a constant times k^(-1/2), so the third-order replacement remainder
is O(k^(-1/2)) for each fixed smooth test. Bounded continuous tests follow
by smoothing and truncation. All fourth moments are uniformly bounded.

To turn this into empirical transport, bin the deterministic bounded
center coefficients as in Section 2. For a fixed bounded noise test f,
the variance of k^(-1)sum_{j in a bin}f(V_j) tends to zero uniformly:
diagonal terms cost O(1/k), and distinct-coordinate covariances tend to
zero uniformly by the bivariate replacement. Finitely many bins therefore
have the correct limiting Gaussian noise laws. Truncate noise at T; its
expected omitted squared mass is at most C/T^2 by the fourth-moment bound.
For |z_j|>R again match the CENTER identically and pay only noise cost;
the expected contribution is at most C/R^2. Take k to infinity first,
then T,R to infinity and bin width to zero. This proves uniformly

    E W_2(actual pair quotient law, reference (1))^2 ->0.  (5)

The same conclusion holds after averaging arbitrarily many fibres,
uniformly over all actual global centers. Thus some deterministic
delta_k->0 has probability 1-o(1) of putting an iid-noisy pair in the
sector. No operator norm property of the center's child is involved.

## 5. Hamming windows and the exact remaining gap

Iid noise has global overlaps alpha+o(1), rather than exactly alpha. For
a Hamming window |<x0,x>/n-alpha|,|<y0,y>/n-alpha|<=omega_n with omega_n->0,
the physical marginal-Gram discrepancies have Frobenius norm at most
sqrt(2)n omega_n. The finite exponent bound gains only the error
sqrt(2)||L||F omega_n per n. The window's pair entropy is
2h(1/10)+o(1). Hence the same strict .08 margin proves existence of actual
sign bridges controlling ALL window pairs in the enlarged sector by
(37/50)n^(3/2), with the center bridge o(n^(3/2)).

For actual low-cap children, the previously proved noisy-extremum energy
concentration and (5) hold simultaneously with probability 1-o(1).
The controlled sector thus contains exp[2n h(.1)-o(n)] actual pairs from
the very intermediate-energy family that defeats the uniform one-state
Chernoff-plus-union estimate. This is a genuine overlap-sensitive escape
for that dominant family, with arbitrary actual centers.

It remains essential that probability 1-o(1) does NOT make the atypical
set subexponential in cardinality. Those exceptional physical pairs,
other energy windows, and a paid covering by centers remain uncontrolled.
No whole-cloud, whole-parent, all-order, or original-convergence theorem
follows by omitting them.
