## Preliminary implication alignment: composition and pressure

### `L_IC`

The extremal report requires, for every comparable pair of low-cap children,
one bridge whose exact joint parent cap has a summable defect after the
`2/3` transform.  Ledger 10.140.1 requires the same joint bridge at the same
scale (with a different but equivalent homogeneous display) and rejects it
because the exact block identity leaves the full state-dependent bridge
maximization.  The report strengthens the child quantifier from selected
minimizers to every low-cap pair; it does not remove an archived obligation.

### `L_advGT`

The discrepancy report requires near-superadditivity of the *minimized*
fixed-temperature antipodal pressure.  The implication through Hammersley
and the uniform soft-max sandwich is correct.  Ledger 10.113.2, 10.135, and
10.140.2 already isolate convergence of this same pressure for every fixed
temperature as the sufficient condition.  `L_advGT` is a desired scalar
inequality, not an imported interpolation mechanism: the cited SK theorem is
quenched/averaged and its min/expectation order has the wrong direction.

### `L_Lap`

The spin report instead requires a deterministic binary cross-completion for
*every* prescribed child pair at each fixed scaled temperature.  It keeps the
two antipodal channels jointly, and one fixed-temperature Laplace statistic
does not determine a coset histogram.  Nevertheless the exact bridge witness
is the archived pressure-composition state, now with a stronger universal
child quantifier.  Random bridge averaging has a linear pressure defect and
temperature dilution; the archive's quenched reveal and entropy-tilt formulas
show that removing it requires the full Gibbs response unless a new closure
theorem is supplied.  Whether this deserves `B` rather than `C` turns on an
independent verifier's assessment of the temperature-dependent existential
quantifier; its very low specialist confidence is evidence, not a class.
