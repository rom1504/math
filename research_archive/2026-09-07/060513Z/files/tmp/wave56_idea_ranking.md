# Wave 56 independent idea ranking

Evidence basis: README.md, the Wave 55 Updated frontier in ledger.md, and the
Wave 55 STEERING.md refresh, all reread after commit bf9d481. Scores are
promise/testability/independence on a 1--5 scale. These are agent-authored
hypotheses, not user directives.

1. **Aggregate excess-block migration duality (5/5/5).** Use the many choices
   of child-positive excess sets in (10.1324), rather than one deterministic
   set. Double-count which `o(T_n)`-deficit parent states can escape them.
   Prove a dichotomy: exponentially/saved many near-ground witnesses, or one
   witness anti-correlated with a quantitatively large fraction of every
   high-field star. Map either conclusion to a low-row child fibre or state an
   exact remaining obstruction.
2. **Active-face convex separation (5/4/5).** Generalize the attenuated-block
   theorem to many simultaneous edge blocks. If the signed edge-pattern
   vectors of the parent near-ground face do not approximately balance the
   child excess directions, use separation plus discrepancy rounding to
   construct an integral signing with smaller cap. Seek an explicit convex-
   hull radius and deficit scale, not only an existence slogan.
3. **Mesoscopic full-histogram phase diagram (5/5/5).** Analyze common-core
   kernels at `ell=n^gamma`, `3/4-c<gamma<1`, which are non-direct by
   (10.1270) but are outside Wave 55's fixed-linear audit. Derive exact
   asymptotics for eigenvalues, transition intersection, typical width,
   partner capacity, and the full-histogram versus one-threshold gap. Identify
   a scale whose required partner count could be generated by signing
   migration, or prove a new feasibility wall.
4. **Selected near-ground cut law (5/4/5).** Construct a nonuniform law on
   global cuts using parent deficit and project row, then derive an exact
   selector-tail inequality. The target is one deterministic cut satisfying
   (10.795); explicitly avoid reverting to the uniform local-state/completion
   product law retired by (10.1317).
5. **Migration-to-row conversion (4/5/4).** Starting from the escaping parent
   state in (10.1326), compare its row square with the child completion
   polynomial (10.1287). Seek an exact identity showing that reversal of a
   field-excess block supplies negative `d` or `H_T` correction after a
   compatible selector exchange.
6. **Full-histogram shell derivative (4/4/5).** At controlled project-row
   caps, write the signed increment of the unnormalized complete histogram as
   the cap crosses a row shell. Use exact minimizer symmetries or edge
   replacement pressure to force a positive integrated shell budget without
   invoking monotonicity, which is already false pointwise.
7. **Common-center all-seed aggregation (4/4/4).** Strengthen (10.1310) by
   retaining the actual signed deficit and center migration map. Bound its
   preimages using row geometry and determine whether iterating all seeds can
   create a mesoscopic-core full histogram at one controlled center.
8. **Near-ground face entropy versus project row (4/5/5).** Prove that a large
   family of `o(T_n)`-deficit parent states contains a cut with
   `R_2=O(n^(9/4-c))`, or construct an exact/scalable obstruction. Use the
   Walsh identity for average row square rather than generic state count.
9. **Nonlinear core optimization (3/5/4).** Optimize `ell` all the way from
   `omega(H)` through `o(n)` and toward `m-o(n)`, tracking the extraction
   denominator and diagonal. Check whether near-identity kernels offer a
   narrow-transition mechanism not covered by compact linear-core no-go.
10. **Finite migration/histogram atlas (3/5/3).** Exhaust stored exact
    minimizers: enumerate excess-edge choices, escaping near-ground witnesses,
    their row squares, favorable child fibres, and complete intersection
    histograms. Use this to falsify overly strong aggregate bridges before an
    asymptotic proof attempt.

Selected parallel attacks: combine 1--2 as aggregate migration/active-face
duality; 3 as the mesoscopic histogram phase diagram; and 4 as selected-cut
near-ground construction. The main agent will independently attack ideas 5
and 8 and audit all outputs.
