# Wave 40 boundary: post-generation ledger comparison

This comparison was written only after the three candidates in
`blank_slate_wave40_stage1.md` had been fixed.  It is scratch support for the
compact audit recorded in `STEERING.md`.

## Candidate A: finite-pressure thermodynamic limit

The mapping and theorem are valid, but this is not a new route.  Ledger §6.4
already defines the same fixed-temperature optimized spin pressure (without
the harmless explicit `cosh` symmetrization) and gives the same
`log(2)/b` zero-temperature squeeze.  Sections 3.3 and 3.9 record two precise
walls: comparable block splitting changes the effective diagonal temperature,
and even centered subadditivity, convexity, analyticity, correct variance, and
correct zero-temperature slope admit oscillatory scalar countermodels.
Section 10.48 finds an exact Gibbs--Shearer inequality for a related canonical
pressure, but its characteristic again changes temperature; the ground-gauge
down-set is neither matroidal nor hereditary and block gluing restores a
leading rectangular term.  A revival would need a genuinely signing-specific,
coefficient-level overlap identity, not the scalar limit statement itself.

**Judgment:** reject as a new leading mechanism.  The exact theorem remains a
logically sufficient independent fallback, but no new way to prove it was
generated.

## Candidate B: augmented-cut-code deep-hole product

Equation (B2) is precisely the uniform-amplification sufficient lemma in §6.1.
The suggested balanced orthogonal blow-up encounters the recorded mechanism
walls: universal Hadamard lifts relax scalar Boolean states to vector states
(§3.4), literal tensor submultiplicativity is false (§3.8), weight-two
characters tensor to weight four and the Segre restriction misses arbitrary
Boolean block states (§10.50), and exact compressed lifts are monotone in
centered width with strict finite equality obstructions (§10.31--10.32).
The code language therefore does not remove the all-cuts requirement.

**Judgment:** reject as a vocabulary-level reformulation unless a nonlocal
decoration is supplied that controls every Boolean block state without the
known vector-relaxation loss.  No such mechanism arose in the blank-slate
generation.

## Candidate C: discrete homogeneity exchange

The pointwise bound (C2) is a stronger version of sharp one-vertex insertion
(§6.2).  Its signing-level deletion/insertion interpretation substantially
overlaps the finite-pressure constant-shortfall path (10.617), while the
dyadic cumulative form overlaps the weaker cumulative optimized-restriction
criteria.  The puncture cocycle (10.653)--(10.658) proves that linear
deletion/insertion compatibility collapses to the original decrement and is
one square-root scale too weak.  Pressure-minimizer stability also points in
the wrong direction.  Thus (C2)/(C3) state a sufficient regularity outcome but
do not provide the missing exchange mechanism.

**Judgment:** reject as a new route.  Constant shortfall remains a valid
fallback, but it still lacks the square-root cavity-reward theorem and is not
stronger than the current restriction or harmonic targets.

## Resulting strategy judgment

The audit found no candidate with a genuinely stronger or newly testable
mechanism at the project scale.  It therefore does not displace adaptive
optimized principal restriction.  Wave 39's information-to-Gram transport
makes the affordable low-information, low-parent-row, low-conflict kernel
(10.1089) the strongest structured implementation of the bare tail (10.795).
The harmonic scalar-curvature target (10.1096) remains the strongest
independent analytic alternative.  Exceptional-center degree, correlated
complement congestion, terminal soft potentials, and constant shortfall stay
ranked fallbacks.  Finite pressure or amplification should be revived only
with an explicit mechanism that evades the particular ledger walls above.
