# Wave 55 independent idea ranking

Evidence basis: README.md, the Wave 54 Updated frontier in ledger.md, and
the Wave 54 STEERING.md refresh, all reread after commit dff5875. Scores are
promise/testability/independence on a 1--5 scale. These are agent-authored
hypotheses, not user directives.

1. **Canonical-head selector exchange (5/5/5).** On a both-orientations-far,
   K-bad state, use the canonical clipped head `I subset S^c`, `|I|<H`, rather
   than an arbitrary exceptional set. Exchange some or all of `I` into the
   selector and track the exact changes in `h`, `g`, `u`, and row square.
   Prove a charging/termination lemma or exhibit a scalable exact obstruction.
2. **Joint local-energy/cross-Gram conditioning (5/4/4).** For uniform local
   spins, analyze jointly `e=y^T A[S]y` and
   `G=y^T A[S,T]A[T,S]y`. Seek a dependence-safe dichotomy showing that the
   selector-driven low-`|e|` branch has saved cross energy and a nonconcentrated
   canonical profile. Avoid separate moment bounds already known to fail.
3. **Exact-minimizer localized-curvature exclusion (5/4/5).** Starting from
   (10.1298)--(10.1303), turn a hidden frozen mode
   `I >> n^(9/4-c), X,d,H_T small` into a concrete internal block replacement
   or simultaneous edge perturbation that lowers the exact parent cap. The
   target must use more than generic trace/operator structure.
4. **Multilevel weighted-intersection kernel (5/5/5).** Work directly with
   (10.1292), using a nonnegative generating polynomial in intersection size
   or a mixture of common-core kernels. Optimize its spectrum and maximum
   possible partner load; prove a saved weighted excess theorem or a sharp
   feasibility wall without collapsing to the impossible one-threshold lemma.
5. **Tuned Johnson-ball inverse theorem (4/4/5).** At
   `alpha=(beta-p^2)/(1+beta-2p)`, characterize near-equality in the entropy
   comparison. Determine whether the required almost-full ball structure is
   incompatible with project-row favorable families of an exact minimizer.
6. **Common-center star percolation with cancellation (4/4/4).** Iterate the
   arbitrary-seed positive-threshold star while tracking actual signed deficit
   increments and row changes. The goal is a linear-radius cluster at one
   center; stop if center migration or `Theta(rn)` drift is provably sharp.
7. **Aggregate signed-`d` visibility (4/5/4).** Average the identity
   `sum_i r_i a_i=(||d||^2+||BPy||^2-||Cu||^2)/2` over a carefully chosen
   selector/child-ground incidence law. Either force a saved signed-`d` gain
   or isolate a rigid paired-field symmetry for exact minimizers.
8. **Near-parent-ground to favorable-restriction transfer (3/4/5).** Combine
   the star-flip migration/entropy dichotomy with random principal restriction.
   Ask whether exponentially many `O(H)`-deficit parent states force one
   low-row center with multiple favorable restrictions, without normalizing by
   the whole `2^n` state space.
9. **Delsarte/Johnson literature import (3/5/5).** Search primary sources for
   weighted intersection, complete-monotonicity, or stability inequalities
   whose exact normalization matches (10.1292). Import only a theorem that
   beats the full-ball feasibility boundary without assuming family density.
10. **Finite canonical-head atlas (3/5/3).** Exhaust the stored exact
    minimizers, recording canonical heads, selector excess, exchange response,
    and signed-`d` curvature. Use the atlas to falsify overly strong exchange
    statements before an asymptotic proof attempt.
