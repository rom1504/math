# Director selection rubric

The panel is a retrieval and selection experiment, not a vote.  Score only
after archive verification and revision.

## Hard gates

A proposal is ineligible for execution if any answer is yes:

- Does its missing lemma quantify over, or permit recovery of, every parent
  maximizing spin or the complete coset-weight histogram?
- Does it bound multiple scalar channels before their cancellation?
- Does its recurrence have a leading coefficient worse than the target by a
  fixed positive amount?
- Is its state finite-dimensional only before accuracy is set, but exponential
  at the required `n^(-1/2)` or `o(n)` scale with no algebraic closure theorem?
- Does it prove only a random/typical-disorder statement while the required
  quantifier is uniform over deterministic minimization?
- Does it transfer examples only on a sparse subsequence without an all-order
  realization theorem?
- Is its claimed nonconvergence merely a route counterexample or finite residue
  effect?

## Positive gates

At least one must be established, not asserted:

1. imports a theorem/mechanism absent from the archive with hypotheses that
   plausibly match;
2. defines a state from which the full optimization provably cannot be
   recovered, while still controlling the required extreme;
3. gives an exact falsifier and survives it;
4. yields a cross-order inequality with no fixed leading loss and a summable
   accumulated defect;
5. gives a scalable construction plus universal theorem capable of strict
   subsequence separation.

## Comparative score (0--3 each)

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Novel imported leverage | archived | cosmetic variant | genuinely different but distant | exact theorem matches most hypotheses |
| Strictness of reduction | equivalent | unclear | information loss argued | non-recoverability proved |
| Scale | fixed loss | wrong/unknown | correct leading scale conjecturally | exact correct-scale identity |
| Falsifiability | none | vague | finite test | decisive structural or scalable test |
| All-order/extreme control | typical/subsequence only | missing bridge | explicit bridge lemma | theorem already supplies it |
| Tractability | original problem | several new obligations | one isolated lemma | imported theorem nearly proves lemma |

Confidence estimates from specialists and verifiers are recorded but do not
enter mechanically.  The director must explain disagreements.

## Execution rule

Select no more than two.  Before any large computation, write the exact
minimal theorem and assign one proof attempt and one disproof attempt.  A
counterexample ends the route unless it reveals a qualitatively different
statement.  Two substantive checkpoints without primary progress trigger a
new director review, not an adjacent variant.
