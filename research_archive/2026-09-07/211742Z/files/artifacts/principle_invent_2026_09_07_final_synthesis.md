## Why the preceding natural relaxation was insufficient

The marked-column recursion itself is a proved positive operation:
`principle_invent_2026_09_07_marked_column_bellman.md`.
Exact dephasing leaves one marked child and independent ordinary siblings.
The original envelope E=g(E Var)-I closes by extinction of the single
marked spine; no asymmetric controlled CLT is needed.

But its unstratified scalar certificate has an exact all-temperature
near-constant variance-ratio limit 1/(2sqrt(p))>1/2. The joint packet node
is what bypasses that proved relaxation failure. Replacing E by another
scalar descriptor without retaining the packet relation would not explain
the improvement.

Two important audits narrowed the safe construction. A raw DC law cannot
be globally reversed and averaged without checking its envelope change.
Also output-row permutations may mix different child laws and lower E.
Sylvester translations avoid both problems: the raw DC coordinate is
fixed exactly, and every other output changes only sign. The H24 scalar
certificate and exact automorphism data are preserved, but their attempted
same-law inheritance is deliberately NOT promoted.
