# Wave 33 ranked ideas

Evidence base: README, Wave 32 Updated frontier (§10.85.5), and STEERING.md
at commit 455e898.

1. **Global block-signing variation.** Derive exact necessary conditions for
   an optimal complete signing under one-edge and whole-block replacements;
   test whether they quantitatively forbid the planted `n^(3/4)` quadratic
   mode from (10.936)--(10.937).
2. **Soft center-star / Renyi reduction.** Replace hard distances by
   `exp(-lambda a_z(S))`, derive an exact Laplace and minimax dual for the
   total-cost moment (10.926), and identify a cross-selector condition weaker
   than normalized degrees.
3. **Edge-symmetrized screened response.** Combine the two orientations of a
   parent coordinate edge in (10.939)--(10.940), retain drift--KL
   cancellation, and seek an exact endpoint-specific comparison avoiding a
   generic parent Poincare bound.
4. **Near-ground active-cut certificate.** Use discrete optimality under every
   edge flip to prove that near-active parent cuts balance every edge block;
   translate the required multiplicity/entropy into conditional fibers.
5. **Johnson transport of centers.** Couple favorable labels for adjacent
   selectors, charge their projective disagreement to exchanged vertices,
   and build low-cost stars or trees along a random Johnson spanning tree.
6. **Orientation synchronization on a random selector tree.** Treat node
   orientations as an Ising synchronization problem and bound the exact
   coordinate terminal min-cut by overlap disagreements plus a controlled
   hidden-conflict term.
7. **Conductance-weighted small-beta expansion.** Expand the screened residual
   through its first nonzero cumulants under the actual edge conductances and
   test whether the minimizer row-square bound controls the remainder.
8. **Shearer entropy for favorable fibers.** Exploit that all principal child
   problems come from one signing to seek a high-order overlap inequality
   that excludes independent-label dispersion.
9. **Quadratic boundary susceptibility from global replacements.** Average
   (10.914) over carefully chosen shores and use exact signing optimality to
   control the squared boundary vector rather than its signed total.
10. **Primary-literature transfer.** Search discrepancy stability,
    hereditary-discrepancy minimizers, and robust matrix-game certificates
    for a theorem that can supply either block replacement or high-order
    incidence correlation with verified hypotheses.
