# First architecture tests and scoped failures

2026-09-07. Exploratory reconstruction, not a list of solved obligations.

1. Small global perturbations looked like a possible source of a new
   near-ground variational principle. Reading the actual preceding proofs
   showed this is already achieved by target-contraction surgery, including
   a full signed covariance law. Explicit suboptimal Hadamard matrices have
   stronger stationarity. No new progress is claimed for this rediscovery.

2. An unrestricted row-square-regular weighted relaxation is too broad.
   The five-cycle Seidel matrix has zero row sums and quadratic cap 4:
   a one-spin flip has energy zero; a two-spin flip has energy +/-4; larger
   flips are complementary. For N divisible by5, take N/5 copies scaled
   by sqrt((N-1)/4). Each row has squared mass N-1, while cap is exactly
   (2/5)N sqrt(N-1). This is below the preserved full-sign lower bound.
   Its entries grow as sqrt(N), so it does NOT refute bounded-amplitude
   profiles, or the two-block target with comparable growing minimizers.
   The latter distinction motivates the invention agent's present test.

3. Balanced incidence factorization alone does not transfer a favorable
   seed through a Hadamard weave: free incidence gauges can cancel the seed
   exactly. Conversely it represents essentially ALL edge signings and
   yields a uniform atom-scale ensemble comparison; that positive theorem
   is recorded separately. Its nested Boolean maximum remains unresolved.

4. Deterministic first-order-method literature supplies a precise possible
   connection, not an automatic theorem on minimizers. Root reread the
   primary Gorini--Jones--Kunisky--Pesenti paper (arXiv:2604.11729), especially
   Assumption5.2/Theorem5.3, and the archive's exact universality audit.
   Open-cactus maximum-entry bounds and diagonal-distribution hypotheses
   are not implied by the available fourth-moment bound. Puncturing is a
   rank-one projection, not deletion of bad coordinates. The associated
   Fan--Misiakiewicz--Wang--Wen pressure theorem (arXiv:2607.10102) remains
   a high-temperature theorem; no zero-temperature import is made.

5. Sparse-degree approximations and independent clique-design assembly
   have the familiar variance-scale issue: sampling to mean degree d gives
   a full-cube error of order N^2/sqrt(d), not o(N^(3/2)) when d=o(N).
   This calculation rejects that particular concentration proof, not all
   correlated sparse-to-dense constructions. No new impossibility theorem
   is claimed for the whole architecture.

Current constructive tests: globally selected skew/cyclic lifts; bounded
regular variance-profile replacement; using entropy-rich global compatibility
without enlarging the actual row-code feasible set. None has yet produced a
cross-order bound. The campaign continues until approximately21:24 UTC.
