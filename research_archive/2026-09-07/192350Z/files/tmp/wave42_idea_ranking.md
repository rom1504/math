# Wave 42 ten-idea ranking

Evidence basis: README, Wave 41 Updated frontier, and the Wave 40 boundary
STEERING assessment. These are main-agent research hypotheses, not user
directives.

1. **Mixed principal-norm pressure via a tilted selector law.** For the exact
   criterion (10.1125), study the law proportional to
   `exp{-theta Q(A[S])+theta sigma C_S}`. Derive exchange gradients of
   `Q(A[S])`, a self-bounding/Efron--Stein estimate, or an exact-minimality
   inequality that controls both positive pressure and the replica gap at
   `theta=Theta(n^(-3/4))`. This attacks the newly isolated obstruction rather
   than another moment of `C_S`.
2. **Two-replica common-competitor bound.** Rewrite
   `K(2theta)-2K(theta)` using two selectors and couple their optimizing child
   cuts through the overlap. A shared extension or exchange competitor might
   cancel the two principal norms and leave a Hanson--Wright-controlled signed
   term. Seek an exact inequality or a finite/scalable wall.
3. **Soft principal-cap interpolation.** Replace `Q(A[S])` temporarily by a
   log-sum-exp over child cuts and differentiate in its inverse temperature.
   Test whether the negative soft-cap pressure has useful convexity before
   taking the hard limit. This is lower ranked because the recorded generic
   smoothing entropy error is at the wrong scale unless the replica
   normalization cancels it.
4. **Incidence-preserving consensus convex program.** Optimize a joint law on
   exact complement incidences with simultaneous information, row, and
   anchored-conflict prices. Derive its finite minimax dual and a sharp
   trilemma/separation certificate. The goal is a new joint mechanism, not
   another deterministic canonical cycle sum.
5. **Common random perturbation/MinHash selection.** Give every global cut one
   shared random priority and choose the highest-priority incidence witness
   for each selector, possibly after a row threshold. Expected disagreement
   becomes an overlap/Jaccard statistic of incidence fibers. Audit finite
   minimizers and identify the exact codegree theorem that would reach project
   scale.
6. **Outside-spin reveal martingale for harmonic anti-resonance.** Reveal a
   fixed-density completion one spin at a time using (10.916), decompose score
   jumps into one-deletion log-cosh increments, and seek a square-function or
   entropy bound that prevents linear accumulation of resonance.
7. **Selector-posterior influence tradeoff.** Use
   `chi_i=log(r_i(d)/r_i(d^i))`, fixed-size `sum_i r_i=n-m`, and adjacent-
   selector Hellinger energy to show that many large active score jumps force
   excessive posterior/Johnson cost. This directly tests whether the omitted
   harmonic input excludes the Wave 40 erasure wall at project scale.
8. **Direct signed migration as a mixed completion derivative.** Differentiate
   the actual completion partition jointly in interpolation time and outside
   coupling strength, retaining the cancellation in (10.1083). Seek a
   boundary Bregman term rather than the falsified isotonic majorant.
9. **Favorable-distance scaffold under minimizer exchange.** Combine the exact
   soft terminal identity (10.1114) with a minimizer-specific exchange
   scaffold and try to bound (10.1117) directly. This remains independent but
   has no new source of the missing `O(k_0)` estimate.
10. **Nonlinear cavity reward for constant shortfall.** Search only for a
    genuinely multi-coordinate square-root reward that evades the puncture
    cocycle wall. It remains lower ranked because no current construction
    supplies such a mechanism.

Selected assignments: ideas 1--2 as one mixed-pressure task; ideas 4--5 as
one incidence-consensus task; ideas 6--8 as one fixed-density harmonic task.
The three attacks have different failure modes and directly follow the Wave
41 frontier.
