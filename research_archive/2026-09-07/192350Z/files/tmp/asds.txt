Abstract. We introduce almost supplementary diﬀerence sets (ASDS). For odd
m, certain ASDS in Zm that have amicable incidence matrices are equivalent to
quaternary sequences of odd length m with optimal autocorrelation. As one con-
sequence, if 2 m − 1 is a prime power, or m ≡ 1 mod 4 is prime, then ASDS of
this kind exist. We also explore connections to optimal bina ry sequences and group
cohomology.

2 Quasi-orthogonal cocycles and optimal sequences
Let G and U be ﬁnite groups, with U abelian. A map ψ :G ×G →U such that

denotedZ 2(G,U ). Given any map φ :G →U , the coboundary∂φ ∈Z 2(G,U ) is deﬁned by

ψ is orthogonal precisely when RE(Mψ ) is least, i.e., RE(Mψ ) = 0.

Proposition 1 ([4, Proposition 1]). Let |G| = 4t + 2 > 2. If ψ ∈ Z 2(G, Z2) then
RE(Mψ ) ≥ 4t, whereas RE(Mψ ) ≥ 8t + 2 if ψ ∈B2(G, Z2).

to the situation for orthogonal cocycles, we do not know of any gr oup over which they do
not exist.
