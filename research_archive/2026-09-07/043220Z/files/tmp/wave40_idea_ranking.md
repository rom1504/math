# Wave 40 ten-idea ranking

Evidence basis: README, Wave 39 Updated frontier, and the early Wave 40
STEERING refresh.  Scores are qualitative combinations of promise,
falsifiability, scale fit, and independence.  None is a user directive.

1. **Pairwise exchange for anchored complement certificates.**  For two
   selectors sharing the anchor, combine their complement-flip optimality
   inequalities under a common switching gauge and seek an exact four-corner
   bound on corrected label disagreement.  Success would supply the remaining
   conflict clause in (10.1089); a finite exact counterexample would sharply
   delimit what extra cost is necessary.
2. **Harmonic curvature regression / integrated isotonic defect.**  Differentiate
   `m_s(g)=E[V_s|g]`, or rewrite its antitone component as a two-replica
   covariance, to prove the time-integrated estimate (10.1096).  Test literal
   monotonicity first and retain an integrated bound if pointwise monotonicity
   fails.
3. **Entropy-regularized common complement kernel.**  Minimize conditional
   row cost plus anchored disagreement over complement incidences, derive its
   exact dual, and test whether parent minimality bounds the dual without a
   full-slice fractional cover.  This attacks all clauses of (10.1089) jointly
   and must avoid treating common row price as collision control.
4. **Exceptional-center nonlinear moment.**  Use (10.1087) to remove row/Gram
   concerns and seek a direct high moment or dependent-random-choice theorem
   producing (10.967) from complement incidences.  It is promising but shares
   much of the first route's overlap bottleneck.
5. **Terminal log-partition soft potential.**  Replace hard favorable fibers by
   dynamic-programming/log-sum-exp potentials on a completion tree, with
   edge-conditional normalization intended to make the laminar increments in
   (10.1099) telescope once per terminal.  Prove the exact Euler/telescoping
   statement or exhibit a concrete obstruction.
6. **Two-replica harmonic transport without isotonic projection.**  Integrate
   (10.1091) before applying signs and express the adverse term as a Dirichlet
   form along a monotone coupling of the scalar score.  Seek cancellation over
   time that is invisible to pointwise regression bounds.
7. **Correlated rounding of a fractional complement cover.**  Couple local
   certificate draws through shared randomness/optimal transport so their
   marginals retain low information and row cost while disagreement is bounded
   by a transport entropy.  A theorem must operate on an affordable anchored
   family, not merely use independent product noise.
8. **Selector-transposition Hellinger/restoring package.**  Prove the harmonic
   adjacent-selector input directly by a swap coupling and exact likelihood
   ratios, then determine whether restoring follows from the same scalar
   Dirichlet form.  Useful but leaves (10.1096) open.
9. **Nonlinear two-coordinate cavity exchange.**  Seek a plaquette/cycle lower
   bound for some deletion cavity reward, strong enough for (10.617), while
   explicitly avoiding the linear puncture cocycle wall.  Lower ranked because
   no square-root reward mechanism is visible.
10. **Signing-specific finite-pressure coefficient interpolation.**  Derive a
    one-vertex or edge-replacement identity for the optimized pressure that
    uses higher cumulants/overlap profiles and test whether it evades §§3.3,
    3.9, and 10.48.  The blank-slate audit found the target but no new
    mechanism, so this remains dormant rather than selected.

Selected independent assignments: idea 1 (leading kernel conflict), idea 2
(harmonic scalar defect), and idea 5 (terminal soft-potential fallback).  Idea
3 is retained for the main-agent synthesis if the pairwise exchange exposes a
usable variational cost.
