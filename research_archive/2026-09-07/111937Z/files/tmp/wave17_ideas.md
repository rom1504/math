# Wave 17 candidate routes

The starting point is Ledger (10.600): seek a critical-scale partition with
leading captured excess and a subleading correlated active-state defect, then
still prove a temporal compatibility theorem.  Any route that merely charges
response to root residual, Cartesianizes the active states, or subtracts
captured excess as a scalar is already falsified.

Ranking uses promise / immediate falsifiability / independence, each on a
five-point scale.

1. **Near-active cross-Gram bridge (5/5/5).**  Polarize two actual correlated
   near-cross states, rather than two exact parent grounds, and derive the
   exact endpoint-shore/resource identity for `z^T A w`.  Determine whether a
   large deficit-corrected response forces either a compatible opposite state
   with large cross-Gram or a quantitatively large active-set deficit.  Audit
   the sharp constants exhaustively on A8 and all forty A9 completions.

2. **Selected-child active-defect potential (5/5/5).**  Strengthen (10.531)
   with a state variable built from terminal excess and `Gamma` (or a
   near-ground margin), derive the exact one-deletion drift, and search for a
   vertex-selection rule making it nonpositive.  First test every small
   minimizer and specifically the flat A9 -> A8 -> A7 obstruction.

3. **Mixed A9 x growing conference/Hadamard audit (4/5/5).**  Classify the
   natural mixed Kronecker/fibre constructions not covered by (10.582)--
   (10.585).  Either exhibit a tensor-compatible wall at growing scale or
   prove a general Boolean witness makes the construction nonminimal.  Keep
   pure sign/zero-diagonal constraints explicit.

4. **Ground-signature partition (4/4/4).**  Select `r ~ (1/2) log_2 n`
   correlated near-grounds and partition vertices by their joint sign
   signatures, giving blocks of size about `sqrt(n)`.  Express captured
   internal energy and `Gamma` in the same empirical correlations.  Prove a
   dichotomy: either signature collisions yield (10.600), or the active
   family has enough metric entropy to force a separate large-Q witness.

5. **Causal Hall dual as a forbidden replacement (4/3/5).**  Dualize the
   laminar temporal flow exactly.  From a violated proper Hall cut, construct
   a weighted local replacement or correlated active-state mixture whose
   response contradicts global minimality.  This directly targets timing and
   avoids assuming that response has a pre-existing endpoint location.

6. **Active-set complexity dichotomy (4/4/4).**  If the near-cross active set
   has low metric entropy, simultaneously round the convex response LP on it;
   if it has high entropy, use its Gram matrix/Sudakov separation to produce a
   large quadratic witness.  Quantify at tolerance `t=o(n^(3/2))` and avoid a
   full Cartesian product of block extrema.

7. **Correlated Gibbs response (3/4/4).**  Replace the maximum over cross
   states by a low-temperature Gibbs law proportional to
   `exp(e_C/tau)`.  Use the Gibbs variational principle to retain correlations
   while distributing block response, and optimize the entropy penalty
   (`<= n log 2`) against the critical scale.  Test whether the resulting
   average gives any proper temporal cut rather than only a scalar total.

8. **Response-LP rounding by active dimension (3/5/3).**  Prove a discrepancy
   bound for rounding independently convexified block minimizers to a pure
   tuple, with error governed by the rank or Gaussian width of the actual
   near-active profile matrix rather than by all `2^n` states.  A subleading
   gap would transfer mixed certificates to pure correlated ones.

9. **Sparse random-restriction cocycle (3/4/4).**  Use a geometrically sparse
   sequence of order windows and signed martingale differences of restriction
   excess, seeking square-summable rather than absolutely summable boundary
   error.  Any proposal must survive the perfectly flat A9 two-level wall and
   land at deterministic terminal order.

10. **Noise/interpolation differential inequality (3/3/5).**  Embed uniform
    restrictions in Bernoulli vertex noise, differentiate the expected
    support function, and use quadratic-chaos hypercontractivity or influences
    to bound the integrated normalized excess.  The target is an adaptive
    tail comparison, not a second copy of the exact cocycle (10.525).

Selected independent routes: 1, 2, and 3.  Route 1 attacks spatial
compatibility, route 2 attacks temporal compatibility, and route 3 attempts
to amplify or kill the strongest finite obstruction.
