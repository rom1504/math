# A local-stability-filtered selector: exact formula and a row-count bound

Status: director derivation, September19. This is an exact uniform selection
certificate, not a proved asymptotic bound. The conditional distribution,
local fields and permanent identities passed independent reconstruction.
An integer checker verifies46 cases,2,256 sector permutations and120,672
permutation/matching pairs. No external novelty claim is made.

The previous energy-only first moment overcounts parent states that cannot
be maxima. Here all spin directions must be locally stable simultaneously.
The permutation average has an exact sector-permanent formula. A classical
permanent inequality removes that permanent at the cost of a computable
bound involving only row-degree pairs. Whether it is strong enough at the
desired n^(3/2) scale remains OPEN.

## 1. Conditional distribution of a random twist

Fix a hollow full signing A of order n. Choose a uniform permutation p,
independent uniform switches s, and independent uniform matching signs d.
Write G e_i=s_i e_(p(i)), B=G^T A G, and D=[[A,B+diag d],[B+diag d,-A]].
Fix parent spins x,y. Set

```math
t_i=x_i y_i,\quad z=Gx,\quad w=Gy,\quad
I_\sigma=\{i:t_i=\sigma\},\quad
J_\sigma=\{j:z_jw_j=\sigma\},\quad k=|I_+|.
```

The pair (z,w) is generated by choosing z uniformly, choosing J_+ uniformly
among the k-subsets, and setting w_j=z_j on J_+ and -z_j elsewhere.
Conditional on z,w, p is uniform among the k!(n-k)! sector-preserving
bijections. Its switches are forced by s_i=z_(p(i)) x_i. This follows by
counting the original2^n n! equally likely pairs (s,p); it is not an
independence assumption on B's edges.

Define the four actual child row-field arrays

```math
\alpha_i=x_i(Ax)_i,\quad \gamma_i=y_i(Ay)_i,\qquad
b_j=z_j(Aw)_j,\quad c_j=w_j(Az)_j,
```

and u_i=d_i t_i. The two parent spin-aligned local fields at position i are

```math
\alpha_i+b_{p(i)}+u_i,\qquad -\gamma_i+c_{p(i)}+u_i. \tag{1}
```

Also its energy is

```math
E_0+\sum_i u_i,\qquad
E_0=H_A(x)-H_A(y)+z^TAw.                            \tag{2}
```

The base energy is independent of the remaining sector bijection. Since
the full parent has even order, its aligned fields are odd integers: no
single-spin tie occurs. Positive one-spin local maximality is equivalent
to both fields in(1) being strictly positive for every i.

## 2. Exact generating permanent

For matching-sector indices put

```math
m_{ij}=\min\{\alpha_i+b_j,-\gamma_i+c_j\},\qquad
K_{ij}(t)=\tfrac12\bigl(t\,1_{m_{ij}\ge0}
                         +t^{-1}1_{m_{ij}\ge2}\bigr).
```

Both arguments of the minimum are even. Thus u_i=+1 is allowed exactly
when m_ij>=0, while u_i=-1 is allowed exactly when m_ij>=2.
Let K_+,K_- denote the two square sector matrices, and use permanent1
for an empty sector. Conditional on x,y,z,w, the exact Laurent generating
function is

```math
\mathbb E_{p,d}\left[1_{\mathrm{stable}}t^{\sum_i u_i}
  \mid x,y,z,w\right]
=\frac{\operatorname{per}K_+(t)\operatorname{per}K_-(t)}
       {k!(n-k)!}.                                  \tag{3}
```

Consequently the probability of stability AND energy>L is exactly the sum
of the coefficients of t^v on the right for v>L-E0. No maximization over
parent states or permutations is concealed inside this conditional formula.
It still must be averaged over child spin pairs; that difficulty is real.

## 3. Removing the permanent by row counts

For lambda>=0 put p_lambda=e^lambda/(2cosh(lambda)). In each sector define

```math
a_i=\#\{j:m_{ij}\ge2\},\qquad
h_i=\#\{j:m_{ij}=0\},\qquad
\rho_\lambda(a,h)=\mathbb E\,g(a+\operatorname{Bin}(h,p_\lambda)),
```

where g(0)=0 and g(r)=(r!)^(1/r) for integers r>=1. Then

```math
\operatorname{per}K_+(e^\lambda)
\operatorname{per}K_-(e^\lambda)
\le(\cosh\lambda)^n\prod_{i=1}^n\rho_\lambda(a_i,h_i). \tag{4}
```

Proof: K/cosh(lambda) has entries0, p_lambda, or1. Independently replace
each p_lambda entry by a Bernoulli(p_lambda) variable, obtaining a0-1
matrix U. Multilinearity gives E per(U)=per(K/cosh(lambda)). Brégman's
inequality is per(U)<=product_i g(rowdegree_i). The randomized row degrees
are independent, which factors the expectation and proves(4).

For completeness, reconstruct Brégman's entropy proof. If U has any perfect
matching, choose one uniformly and reveal its matched columns in an
independent uniform row order. Conditional entropy for a row is at most
the log of the number of its allowed columns not used earlier. For each
fixed complete matching, the rows matched to a given row's r allowed
columns form an r-set containing that row. Its uniform rank makes that
remaining-column count uniform on1,...,r. The expected log is log(r!)/r.
The entropy chain rule and summation give log per(U)<=sum_i log(r_i!)/r_i.
If a row is zero or there are no perfect matchings, the inequality is
immediate. This is the classical mechanism of
[Radhakrishnan,1997](https://doi.org/10.1006/jcta.1996.2727), not a new
permanent theorem.

Combining(2)--(4), for any lambda>=0,

```math
\Pr\{\mathrm{stable},H_D(x,y)>L\mid x,y,z,w\}
\le e^{\lambda(E_0-L)}
 \frac{(\cosh\lambda)^n\prod_i\rho_\lambda(a_i,h_i)}{k!(n-k)!}. \tag{5}
```

One may also cap this by1. The conditional lambda can be optimized
separately for each child pair: every such bound holds before averaging.
The coefficient formula(3), when evaluated exactly, is stronger than(5).

### A simpler coefficientwise bound preserving the matching-energy tail

There is also a bound that requires no Chernoff parameter or binomial sums.
Write d_i^0=a_i+h_i and d_i^2=a_i, within the relevant sector. Then,
coefficient by coefficient as Laurent polynomials with nonnegative real
coefficients,

```math
 \operatorname{per}K_+(t)\operatorname{per}K_-(t)
 \preceq\prod_{i=1}^n
 \frac{g(d_i^0)t+g(d_i^2)t^{-1}}2.                 \tag{6}
```

To prove it, expand each permanent by the matching signs u_i. For each fixed
u, the remaining matrix is0-1 with row degree d_i^0 or d_i^2 according to
u_i. Apply Brégman to those two sector matrices and then collect equal
powers t^(sum u). This directly proves every coefficient inequality.
It also explains a valid correlated auxiliary-row construction: reveal all
weak edges of a row together rather than independently. Averaging a permanent
only needs independent rows, because a monomial uses exactly one entry in
each row. Neither formulation assumes independent edges of the actual bridge.

Thus the exact conditional probability in(3) is bounded by the appropriate
coefficient tail of the product in(6), divided by k!(n-k)!. Its coefficients
can be computed by an O(n²)-operation convolution after the row counts are
known. The irrational factorial roots require rigorous interval arithmetic
if this bound is used as a numerical certificate. It can be stronger than
a Chernoff relaxation; no unproved comparison with the edge-Bernoulli bound
(4) is needed. The exact identity checker does not rely on these irrational
upper bounds.

## 4. A valid existence criterion and the unresolved estimate

Sum(5), or its exact counterpart, over projective parent spins with x_0=+1
and all y, and average over the conditional-uniform z,J_+ distribution.
Call the resulting upper bound U_A(L). If U_A(L)<1, some allowed parent
has Q(D)<=L. Indeed chirality makes the positive and negative extrema equal;
any cap>L has a positive global maximum, hence at least one positive stable
projective state above L. With the full rather than projective count, the
correct cutoff is2. The energy-only bad-pair cutoff8 must NOT be imported.

The row-count bound uses a simultaneous dominance test on two field arrays,
not two independent probabilities or separately paid energy channels.
It removes the permutation factorial from a conditional probability,
but does not bound the remaining child-pair average uniformly. No theorem
currently establishes U_A(2sqrt(2)Q(A)+o(n^(3/2)))<1 for selectable
near-minimizers, or U_A(4beta(A)/3+o(n^(3/2)))<1 on another useful class.
The child row-field distributions may still contain extensive information.
Thus this is a concrete mechanism to test, not a renamed completed recurrence.
