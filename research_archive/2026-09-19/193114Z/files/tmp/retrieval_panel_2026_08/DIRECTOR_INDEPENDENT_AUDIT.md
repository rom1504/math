# Director independent audit

## Verdict

No frozen architecture qualifies for execution under the director's gates. There is no overlooked class-A route. The only final B is graph-limit **spectral regularization (SR)**, and it is a supporting compactness lemma rather than a convergence architecture: without all-order action realization (AR), SR says only that action-cluster values and scalar-cluster values agree. AR is itself the archived all-order realization obligation and is class C. Thus standalone SR cannot count as an executable route.

Classifications below use the final, post-critique version of each claim. “Collision” names the exact theorem-shaped obligation, not merely a thematic resemblance. `N=n+m`; `Q(A)=max_x|H_A(x)|`; `F_n` is minimized finite-temperature pressure.

## Shared information-theoretic bottlenecks

1. **Joint parent/Gibbs response.** A bridge must work simultaneously against exponentially many parent configurations. Separate channel estimates or scalar cap/pressure data discard the correlation needed to select that bridge; restoring it is exactly the B2/E1/D1/SG/C1/S2/S3 archive obligation.
2. **All-coset extreme tail.** Fixed moments, typical-root sampling, packing, and low-dimensional drift summaries do not see the deepest root or top two states uniformly over every coset. Growing degree or exact closure restores essentially the full exponential histogram; this blocks C1--C3 and the universal high halves of N1--N3.
3. **All-order fluctuation realization.** Compactness, algebraic families, tensor powers, and subsequences do not construct exact signed matrices at every large order with the required `n^(3/2)` control. Far-down restriction then collapses to scalar optimum comparison. This is the common obstruction in B1/E2/G-AR/G-A/G-B and the sparse-order nonconvergence routes.

## Gate decision

The verified action-continuity modulus, exact Paley trace-fibre identity, and Paley terminal trap are useful supporting facts or falsifiers, not convergence/nonconvergence architectures. Final execution set: **empty**. Hold SR as a class-B input only; do not promote it unless paired with a genuinely new, non-archived substitute for AR. No class-A theorem was proved or hidden by the revisions.
