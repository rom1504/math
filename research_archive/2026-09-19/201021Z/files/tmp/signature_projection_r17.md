# Subgroup-signature projection (Wave 17 root audit)

Let `G <= {+-1}^V` be a finite group under coordinatewise multiplication,
and let `A^g = diag(g) A diag(g)`.  Partition the coordinates according to
their characters on `G`: `i ~ j` iff `g_i=g_j` for every `g in G`.  If `D`
is the block-diagonal part of `A` on these character classes and `C=A-D`,
character orthogonality gives the exact Reynolds identities

```math
D=|G|^{-1}\sum_{g\in G}A^g,
\qquad
z^{\mathsf T}Dz=|G|^{-1}\sum_{g\in G}(z\odot g)^{\mathsf T}A(z\odot g),
```

and therefore

```math
z^{\mathsf T}Cz
=z^{\mathsf T}Az-|G|^{-1}\sum_{g\in G}(z\odot g)^{\mathsf T}A(z\odot g).
```

Consequences:

1. `Q(D) <= Q(A)`, since `D` is a convex average of switchings of `A`.
2. On every orbit `zG`, the `D`-energy is the constant orbit mean of the
   `A`-energy, and the `C`-energies are the centered orbit energies.  In
   particular their sum on an orbit is zero.
3. If the character cells have maximum order `s`, then
   `X=sum_chi Q(D_chi)-sum_chi q_|V_chi|`.  Thus any leading lower bound on
   `sum_chi Q(D_chi)` (or already on `Q(D)`) produces leading captured excess
   once the standard `sum q_|V_chi| = O(n sqrt(s))` bound is subleading.

For rank one, `G={1,g}`, the partition has two blocks and

```math
D=(A+A^g)/2,
\qquad C=(A-A^g)/2,
\qquad D^g=D,
\qquad C^g=-C.
```

If `z` is any absolute ground of `C`, then `z` and `z odot g` have opposite
`C`-energy and the same `D`-energy.  The two oriented active states therefore
have opposite `D`-responses.  Hence, exactly,

```math
\mathcal R_{C,0}(D)
=\max_{z:\ |z^T C z|=Q(C)}|z^T D z|\ge0,
\qquad
\Gamma_{C,0}(D)=0.
```

Moreover `max{|d+c|,|d-c|}=|d|+|c|` gives `Q(C)<=Q(A)`.  Thus the exact
active defect is automatically zero for every bipartition; the hard content
of (10.600) begins only when many blocks must be handled simultaneously.

There is a quantitative many-block substitute.  Choose an oriented exact
cross ground `(sigma,z)`, so
`sigma z^T C z=Q(C)`, and put

```math
\delta_G(\sigma,z)
=Q(C)+\min_{g\in G}\sigma(z\odot g)^T C(z\odot g).
```

The orbit-average identity makes this number lie in `[0,Q(C)]`.  At a
minimizing `g`, the oppositely oriented state `(-sigma,z odot g)` has cross
deficit `delta_G`.  Its `D`-response is the negative of the original
ground's `D`-response.  Therefore

```math
\mathcal R_{C,\delta_G}(D)
\ge \max\{a,-a-\delta_G\}
\ge-\delta_G/2,
\qquad
\Gamma_{C,\delta_G}(D)\le\delta_G/2.
```

Thus (10.600)'s active-defect requirement follows from a single nearly
antipodal pair in one actual switching orbit; no Cartesian completion of
block states is used.

This has an equivalent variance sufficient condition.  For
`Y_g=sigma (z odot g)^T C(z odot g)`, write
`v_G(z)=|G|^{-1} sum_g Y_g^2`.  It has mean zero, maximum `Q(C)`, and minimum
`-(Q(C)-delta_G)`.  The elementary Bhatia--Davis inequality
`E Y^2 <= (max Y)(-min Y)` gives

```math
\delta_G\le Q(C)-\frac{v_G(z)}{Q(C)}.
```

Consequently, with
`t=Q(C)-v_G(z)/Q(C)`, monotonicity of the active family yields
`Gamma_{C,t}(D)<=t/2`.  This is useful only if a cross ground has orbit
variance `Q(C)^2-o(n^3)`; average-state Parseval supplies only order `n^2`,
so such a high-variance ground would be genuine new structure rather than a
generic second-moment fact.

The checker `audit_signature_partitions_r17.py` enumerates every rank-at-most
three subgroup generated by projective grounds on the finite walls and
verifies the averaging and antipodal-defect identities for every state:

- A8: 79 distinct subgroups.  A rank-one `(4,4)` partition has `X=8`,
  `Q(C)=16`, and `Gamma_{C,t}(D)=0` for `t=0,4,8`; this recovers the known
  response partition.
- A9: 1,917 distinct subgroups.  A rank-one `(4,5)` partition has `X=12`,
  `Q(C)=20`, and zero defect.  A genuinely rank-two `(1,2,6)` partition has
  `X=12`, `Q(C)=24`, orbit mean zero, and zero defect for the same three
  tolerances.  The latter displays the desired nonrectangular mechanism:
  the subgroup orbit sees zero total internal energy even though the block
  norms have leading finite excess.

This is a verified structural identity and finite observation, not an
asymptotic construction.  To advance (10.600), one still needs a subgroup of
about `sqrt(n)` characters with controlled cell multiplicities and leading
incompatible block norms, or an approximate version.  The identity supplies
no temporal Hall compatibility by itself.
