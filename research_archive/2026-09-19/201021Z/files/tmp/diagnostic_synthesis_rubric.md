# Diagnostic synthesis rubric

This rubric is fixed before reading the three final reports.

## Evidence tests

1. **Correct object:** the claim concerns the actual optimum `M_n`, or a
   rigorously sufficient recurrence for every required exact minimizer.
2. **Asymptotic scale:** all estimates are uniform on the needed order or
   density classes and have the required `o(n^(3/2))` or fixed-separation
   scale.
3. **Logical novelty:** the claimed bridge removes an obligation; it does not
   rename bare favourable mass, project row, collision, or recurrence.
4. **No circularity:** no hypothesis already implies the target recurrence by
   a stored concentration, localization, or extraction theorem.
5. **Scalability:** finite examples and abstract non-minimizer models are
   evidence only unless a proved construction persists on an infinite exact
   order class.
6. **Complete chain:** every additional bridge between the proposed result
   and convergence/nonconvergence is named.

## Recommendation rule

- **Resume with one named target** only if a report supplies a genuinely
  weaker, testable lemma with a complete implication chain and no stored wall.
- **Change research architecture** only if a report supplies a concrete new
  mechanism whose bottleneck differs materially from the selected-prior tail.
- **Seek external mathematical review** if the audit finds likely hidden
  equivalence/stagnation and neither blank-slate direction yields an internal
  mechanism, but the problem remains plausibly tractable with specialist
  insight.
- **Stop** if the reports establish a decisive impossibility or show no
  credible next target even for external review.

The final decision must identify contrary evidence and must not average three
weak suggestions into one apparently strong route.
