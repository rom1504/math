# Wave 58 idea ranking

Read against README.md, the Wave 57 Updated frontier, and STEERING.md.  These
are main-agent research hypotheses, not user directives.

1. **Exact row-change migration calculus.**  If two oriented cuts differ by
   a relative cut/uncut `D`, express `R(omega)-R(d)` through the signed cross
   fields of `d`.  Combine this with a randomized or dyadic field-excess
   block so that reversing a constant fraction forces descent of the LP
   scalar `h+lambda R`, not just Hamming separation.  Prove the descent or
   give an exact minimizer counterexample respecting the migration premise.

2. **Johnson reuse from one fixed migrated state.**  The common-law Fubini
   step gives one near-ground parent state on a constant selector fraction.
   Analyze the selector family on which this same state has sector
   cancellation/favorability and ask whether exact child-ground exchange
   forces the size-biased `s_*`-overlap retention in (10.1364).  Test against
   A9/A10 and against balanced-partition-like labelings.

3. **Construct the optimal two-column selected prior.**  Use the exact convex
   hull/LP in (10.1368)--(10.1369).  Along orientation reversal, one-spin
   paths, switching orbits, or near-ground migration, seek two columns on
   opposite sides of a supporting row price whose mixture has information
   `O(H)` and captured row `O(n^(9/4-c))`.  Identify a KKT descent theorem or
   a finite wall.

4. **Row-controlled fibre transfer.**  For cuts `d,omega`, derive the exact
   centered slice polynomial `hat ell(S,omega)-hat ell(S,d)` and its row
   parameter.  Quantify precisely when a saved fibre of `d` transfers to
   `omega` after `O(T_n)` threshold enlargement.  Couple it to item 1; do not
   assume the response row is controlled.

5. **Random legal excess blocks.**  Choose each vertex's `r_i` positive
   edges uniformly.  Translate the active-face reversal probability into a
   statement about the whole relative signed cut `P_D-N_D`, or prove a
   cancellation counterexample uniform over this randomness.

6. **High-field visibility from exact minimality.**  The hub-avoidance wall is
   abstract.  Prove that in an exact minimizer, a favorable fibre of entropy
   `O(H)` cannot avoid every vertex carrying a correct-sector row excess, or
   construct a scalable signing showing which extra minimizer property is
   required.

7. **Online migration potential.**  Iterate exact-minimality responses while
   choosing the next block from the current high-row fields.  Use a no-regret
   potential combining deficit, row, and fibre surprise to force termination
   at a low-row favorable state; audit whether cycles destroy monotonicity.

8. **Full-histogram reuse from escape.**  Replace the one-threshold `Pi_s`
   by the exact weighted overlap codegree (10.1363's full-histogram analog).
   Determine whether the constant-density common escape family has enough
   multi-shell energy even when every single threshold misses its baseline.

9. **Orientation-pair convexification.**  Compute the two points `(h,R)` for
   both orientations of every physical cut.  Seek an exact inequality on the
   harmonic mean of their fibre sizes using retained parent deficit, capable
   of excluding the A8 wall at positive `T_n` tolerance.

10. **Exposed-face exchange graph.**  Build the graph of parent near-grounds
    joined by migrated relative cuts and study conductance/codegrees under
    the selector labels they escape.  A spectral gap or bounded congestion
    could simultaneously yield row descent and Johnson-correlated witness
    reuse; finite exact enumeration should test whether this is realistic.

Selected independent assignments: 1 together with 4 and 7; 2 together with
8 and 10; and 3 together with 6 and 9.  The main agent will independently
audit randomized excess blocks and the row-change identity.
