# Wave 41 ten-idea ranking

Evidence basis: README, Wave 40 Updated frontier, and the Wave 40 boundary
STEERING assessment.  These are main-agent research hypotheses, not user
directives.

1. **Johnson-cycle curvature for canonical complement responses.**  Sum the
   cyclic monotonicity (10.1103)--(10.1104) over adjacent swaps and elementary
   four-cycles of the anchored slice.  Pairwise flat examples need not defeat
   a global selector-graph modulus.  Derive an exact Johnson Dirichlet/cross-
   overlap identity and either lower-bound corrected conflict or build an
   actual-minimizer/canonical-response wall.
2. **Quadratic-completion constraint on the harmonic erasure components.**
   Express the matched omitted-coordinate likelihoods as the actual external
   Ising partition functions from (10.899).  Derive a determinant, moment,
   Fourier, Hessian, or replica identity that arbitrary positive erasure
   components do not satisfy; test it on the Wave 40 OR wall and finite
   minimizers.  A scale-correct constraint could bound migration or exclude
   the wall.
3. **Direct signed harmonic migration through mixed partition derivatives.**
   Avoid the lossy isotonic majorant and rewrite (10.1083) as a mixed
   time/deletion derivative of the actual completion log partition.  Seek
   cancellation or a Bregman endpoint bound using the quadratic signing
   representation.  This is close to idea 2 but attacks the logically weaker
   quantity.
4. **Consensus-penalized complement response game.**  Jointly optimize
   complement energy minus row price and anchored disagreement over all
   selectors.  Derive the exact finite dual/critical-price curve and ask for a
   project-scale interval on which incidence, row, and conflict all survive.
   The Wave 40 A5 wall forbids a zero-slack pairwise proof, not joint selection.
5. **Exceptional-center positive-part Laplace theorem.**  Attack (10.967)
   through the necessary nonlinear selector functional (10.969), with a row
   penalty built into the center choice.  Seek a minimizer-specific high
   moment/Paley--Zygmund or inverse theorem; do not use the circular uniform
   mean (10.970) or generic one-bit row pricing.
6. **Dependent random choice in the complement-incidence hypergraph.**  Use
   exact pointwise coverage and codegrees of row-good incidences to extract an
   affordable anchored family with coherent labels.  The input must be a new
   average codegree/cycle estimate; first moments and independent noise are
   already stopped.
7. **Multi-marginal transport kernel for complement fibers.**  Couple local
   certificate distributions through a common latent cut and bound corrected
   conflict by a transport entropy, retaining (10.1089)'s row/information
   budgets.  This is testable via an exact finite LP but risks merely
   restating exceptional centers.
8. **Signing-specific higher Walsh spectrum of external completion.**  Bound
   the high-degree spectrum of `K_(beta,S)` using its quadratic exponential-
   sum representation and relate it to the OR wall or scalar regression.
   Lower ranked than idea 2 because the prescribed temperature may make
   generic hypercontractive bounds too costly.
9. **Adjacent-selector Hellinger from a swap coupling.**  Close the harmonic
   Hellinger input using exact likelihood ratios and selector transpositions.
   Useful but leaves signed migration and endpoint orientation unresolved.
10. **Nonlinear cavity-cycle reward.**  Revisit constant shortfall only via a
    genuinely nonlinear multi-coordinate cavity identity that evades the
    puncture cocycle wall.  No current square-root mechanism makes it
    competitive with the restriction/harmonic targets.

Selected independent assignments: idea 1 (global complement-cycle modulus),
ideas 2--3 combined into one signing-specific harmonic task, and idea 5
(direct exceptional-center tail).  Idea 4 is retained for main-agent synthesis
if the Johnson-cycle calculation identifies the correct conflict price.
