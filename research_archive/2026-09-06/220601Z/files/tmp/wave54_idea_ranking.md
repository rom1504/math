# Wave 54 independent idea ranking

Evidence basis: README, the Wave 53 Updated frontier in ledger §10.106.4,
and STEERING.md (evidence cutoff Wave 52), all reread after commit 48f1926.
Scores are promise/testability/independence on a 1--5 scale. These are
agent-authored hypotheses, not user directives.

1. **Joint far-margin/truncated-profile abundance (5/5/5).** Use the exact
   clipped K dual (10.1280)--(10.1282) to split local states by exceptional
   cross coordinates, then seek a conditional counting or entropy theorem
   correlating a far negative margin with a paid head or diffuse tail on
   `exp{-O(H)}` mass. This attacks (10.1285) and must not revert to raw moments.
2. **A-squared cancellation against child-field spikes (5/5/5).** Start from
   the exact completion polynomial (10.1287). Prove that low cross energy plus
   `r_max` above the box scale forces either a large linear coefficient `d` or
   a negative direction of `H_T`, enough to cancel the internal Gram; otherwise
   build a scoped exact finite obstruction.
3. **All-seed global cluster amplification (5/4/5).** Aggregate exchange stars
   over all maximizing or low-row child-ground incidences and double count
   collisions at reached centers. Determine whether the number of seeds can
   supply the exponential partner count in (10.1294), without assuming saved
   degree or charging a one-seed sublinear ball.
4. **Discrete edge-minimality versus field spikes (4/4/5).** Compare an exact
   minimizer with carefully chosen single- and multi-edge sign flips to derive
   a higher-order certificate involving child local fields. The target is
   (10.1290); old parent-ground first-order subgradients are insufficient.
5. **Exceptional-coordinate exchange plus K tail (4/5/4).** Use the clipped
   head in (10.1282) as a concrete set of outside vertices. Either its l1 mass
   pays the far margin, or exchange those vertices into the selector and use
   the resulting deficit/row identities. This must respect that far local
   states are not child grounds.
6. **Nonlinear lower tail of the box completion polynomial (4/4/4).** Apply a
   one-sided degree-two small-ball or conditional block-reveal argument to the
   centered polynomial in (10.1287), exploiting its specific Gram structure
   rather than generic Bonami estimates already retired for the other tail.
7. **Cap-inflated all-center pair-load theorem (3/5/4).** At level two, double
   count distinct exact-ground pairs across all centers after an `O(n^2)` cap
   enlargement. Any proof of `P_2>=lambda_2` solves the degree problem directly;
   finite self-loops cannot be counted as evidence.
8. **Linear-radius cancellation percolation (3/4/4).** Track actual row-square
   increments along selector/center exchanges and seek a martingale or energy
   cancellation allowing `Theta(n)` radius. The required endpoint is the
   exponential partner multiplicity, not merely reachability.
9. **Weighted-core generating function (3/5/3).** Sum the exact distinct-partner
   identity (10.1292) over linear cores with nonnegative weights to find whether
   one global intersection statistic can force a saved positive mode. Reject it
   if its direct collision coefficient already assumes saved degree.
10. **High-singular-subspace completion (2/4/5).** Split `A` at singular value
    `n^(5/8-c/2)` and use the outside coordinates to cancel the low-rank high
    subspace, paying the residual by its operator norm. This advances the box
    route only if the fixed child ground can be completed with bounded signs;
    generic zonotope membership is known to fail finitely.
