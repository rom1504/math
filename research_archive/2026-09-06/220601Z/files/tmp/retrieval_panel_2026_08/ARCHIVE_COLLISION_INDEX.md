# Archive collision index for verifiers

This index is not independent-research context.  It is supplied only after a
proposal has been frozen.  Every collision must be checked in the cited source;
shared terminology is not enough.

## A. Composition and finite-fibre interfaces

1. **Exact block-state dependence.**  Ledger Sections 10.114--10.131 and
   `artifacts/profile_renormalization_semigroup.md` derive exact fibre profile
   composition.  Equal scalar cap data do not determine the parent cap; the
   exact profile is associative but can encode the changing-scale Boolean
   action.  Scope: scalar or bounded-profile closure.  It does not rule out a
   new algebraically closed growing hierarchy.

2. **Scalar atom range obstruction.**  In
   `artifacts/finite_fibre_cut_decomposition_no_go.md`, every signed cut-cone
   microkernel certificate that pays atoms separately has
   `L(R)>1.05 k^(3/2)` for every `k>=2`.  Symmetric left/right box atoms paid
   through polarization obey the same all-order loss.  Scope: certificate
   interface, not the actual Boolean cap of a tensor lift.

3. **Joint polynomial-size SDP floor.**  In
   `artifacts/joint_finite_fibre_action_audit.md`, the complete inter-fibre
   action satisfies `cap(B) <= k||R||_op Gamma(A)+...` with channels retained
   jointly, but `Gamma(A)>=n sqrt(n-1)/2`, equality on conference matrices.
   The skew-Clifford analogue has the same floor.  Scope: two stated joint norm
   interfaces, not every seed-dependent or nonlocal lift.

4. **Bounded-local inheritance.**  In
   `artifacts/nonlocal_lift_joint_sos_inheritance.md`, quotient-compatible
   fixed-degree polynomial/SDP states pull back from the child and retain the
   conference floor; bounded local Boolean cuts do too.  Scope: bounded level
   and quotient-compatible states.  Growing nonlocal algebraic closure is not
   covered.

## B. Grothendieck, polarization, and same-spin conversion

5. **Exact normalization.**  For symmetric hollow `A`, with
   `Q=max_z |sum_(i<j)a_ij z_i z_j|` and
   `B=max_(x,y)|x^T A y|`,
   `Q <= B <= 2Q`; converting matrix normalization carelessly creates an
   apparent extra factor two.  See
   `artifacts/symmetric_grothendieck_krivine_obstruction_audit.md`.

6. **Canonical same-map ceiling.**  The same artifact proves that any
   `n`-dependent common Gaussian response formed from odd self-kernels has
   nonnegative odd Hermite coefficients and linear coefficient at most
   `2/pi`.  On the natural conference Gram input this gives a scalable ceiling
   below the required lower-bound constant.  Scope: canonical same-map input;
   genuinely asymmetric responses with a new same-spin recoupling theorem are
   not covered.

7. **Polarization can be sharp.**  The same artifact and
   `artifacts/asymmetric_same_spin_recoupling.md` exhibit the exact recoupling
   obligations.  A large bilinear objective alone does not control a favorable
   one-spin quadratic energy.  Verify whether a proposal keeps a joint signed
   energy until the final choice or silently pays both channels.

## C. Coding, moments, and moving representations

8. **Packing is coset-blind.**  Ledger 10.138 and
   `artifacts/moving_projection_cut_code_coset_audit.md` show that translating
   the augmented cut code preserves internal pair distances.  A packing or
   minimum-distance theorem alone cannot control its covering radius.  A valid
   import needs a rooted/all-coset statistic.

9. **Moving-Gram root obligation.**  The same sources prove
   `(lambda-mu(a))_+ T_a <= J` for scalar kernels.  The operator extension
   `(lambda-mu(a))_+T_a <=_Loewner J` is in
   `artifacts/high_rank_moving_fiber_matching_obstruction.md`.  Correct scale
   requires hidden support `exp(Omega(n log n))`; a constant root scalarizes
   without loss.  This is an exact interface, but its arbitrary-signing root
   estimate is not known simpler than the original tail.

10. **Direct matching classes are closed.**  In the high-rank matching
    artifact, radial matching squares give only `O(1/n)`, any operator kernel
    whose Fourier support lies directly on matchings has
    `lambda<=1/(n-1)`, and pure add/delete matching representations have
    coefficient at most `4/(3sqrt(3))<1`.  Scope: direct/pure matching
    architectures; broader noncommuting graph-orbit differences are not
    formally covered.

11. **Fixed degree misses extreme cosets.**  Ledger 10.135--10.140 and
    `artifacts/eulerian_free_energy_identity.md` show exact Eulerian-dual
    expansions, vertex deletion opening exponentially many boundary sectors,
    and an overlap hierarchy.  Fixed cycle/replica depth misses planted
    zero-entropy resonances; degree `Theta(n)` can restore the complete signed
    coset histogram.  A proposed growing hierarchy must prove algebraic closure
    rather than list its moments.

## D. Pressure, rare bridges, and limits

12. **High-temperature boundary.**  Theorems in
    `artifacts/finite_temperature_spectral_regularization_dichotomy.md`,
    `artifacts/symmetrized_r_transform_bernoulli_extremizer.md`, and
    `artifacts/high_temperature_frobenius_pressure_stability.md` control a
    strict high-temperature regime.  They do not establish minimized pressure
    limits for all fixed inverse temperatures and cannot by themselves reach
    the ground state.

13. **Exact entropy tilt and its wall.**  In
    `artifacts/two_temperature_bridge_audit.md`, the bridge soft minimum has an
    exact Rényi identity and probability-weighted reveal recursion.  Fixed
    small disorder temperature has a positive linear deficit on conference
    children; a known algebraic bridge orbit costs `Theta(n^2)` entropy.  A
    surviving proposal needs a diffuse `O(n)`-entropy law with linear pressure
    gain and a state-closing theorem, not full backward dynamic programming.

14. **Bounded dual cycles do not close.**  Ledger 10.140 classifies fixed
    Eulerian cumulants as an obstructed finite-temperature compression.  The
    growing-degree version has not been shown weaker than the full coset tail.

## E. Graph limits, restrictions, and arithmetic subsequences

15. **Boolean spikes are invisible to ordinary action convergence.**  In
    `artifacts/action_convergence_boolean_spikes.md`, operator sequences can be
    action-convergent while the normalized Boolean extremum fails to be
    continuous.  A graphon/action proposal must strengthen the topology and
    prove all-order realizability under the hollow full-sign constraint.

16. **Sublinear padding is continuous, not decisive.**  Earlier ledger
    restriction/padding results show `o(n)` order changes alter the normalized
    value by `o(1)`.  This transfers within dense sets of orders but does not
    bridge multiplicatively separated epochs or prove convergence.

17. **Conference upper propagation is all-order.**  Dense Paley conference
    restrictions give `limsup M_n/n^(3/2)<=1/2` on every order.  Any proposed
    nonconvergence mechanism requiring a subsequence uniformly above `1/2` is
    impossible.  Genuine separation must use two constants at or below
    `1/2` and requires an all-signings lower theorem on the high subsequence.

18. **Selected algebraic families are not universal lower bounds.**  Bent,
    symplectic, conference, and Hadamard signings often have explicit cap at
    the `1/2` scale.  Their spectra, parity, or Witt class constrain the chosen
    family but not arbitrary edge signings.  Check quantifier reversal in every
    arithmetic nonconvergence proposal.
