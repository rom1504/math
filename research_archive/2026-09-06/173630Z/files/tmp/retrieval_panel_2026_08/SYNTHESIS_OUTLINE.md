# Retrieval-grounded panel synthesis outline

## Retrieval outcome by domain

- Discrepancy: exact `Theta(n^(3/2))` order and modern linear balancing, but
  no adversarial same-spin thermodynamic comparison.
- Spin glass: interpolation, cavity, and zero-temperature limits are powerful
  for sampled disorder; the deterministic outer minimum reverses the key
  comparison.
- Coding: augmented cut-code mapping is exact; all-coset theorems are too
  coarse, while moving representations currently prove packing statements.
- Banach/tensor: correct scale from Boolean Littlewood/BH theory; bilinear
  tensor multiplicativity retains fixed polarization/prefactor/all-order gaps.
- Extremal: absorption and near-subadditivity give conclusion engines only
  after a summable interface theorem is proved.
- Graph limits: fluctuation-scale action topology plus a spectral moment bound
  makes the Boolean objective continuous; exact all-order sign recovery is
  absent.

## Three bottleneck classes

Provisional only:

1. **All-order fluctuation realization.** Compact subsequential objects do not
   automatically have exact sign realizers at every large order.
2. **Joint bridge/Gibbs response.** Scalar pressures or child caps do not close
   a binary bridge without retaining the full state-dependent response.
3. **All-coset extreme tail.** Fixed-degree/root-blind representation data miss
   the least likely coset; growing degree has no proved algebraic compression.
