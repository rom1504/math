# Wave 59 idea ranking

Read against README.md, the Wave 58 Updated frontier, and STEERING.md.  These
are main-agent research hypotheses, not user directives.

1. **Entropy-weighted global block orbit.**  For a bare favourable fibre
   `F_d`, sample `S in F_d` and then a `k=Theta(H)` vertex block
   `U subset S^c`.  The flip `d^U` preserves the induced local spin exactly
   and costs only `exp(-O(H))` in double counting.  Derive the exact row and
   deficit averages under this tilted law and prove that a high-row column
   has a retained low-row block flip, or exhibit the precise fibre-weighted
   PSD obstruction.

2. **Row-adaptive fibre-transfer curve.**  Use (10.1377) before substituting
   the target cap: a column of surprise `h` can transfer across relative row
   `B` whenever `h B=O(T_n^2)` (up to the operator ceiling).  Seek a
   multistage orbit in which surprise growth pays for row decrease, rather
   than demanding target row at every intermediate state.

3. **Many-selector restriction cocycle.**  For one fixed parent cut and
   canonical child grounds, encode relative cut/uncut labels on overlapping
   selectors.  Derive exact triangle or Johnson-cycle identities involving
   full signed cancellation, not merely scalar deficit Lipschitzness, and
   test whether they force a saved same-parent cluster.

4. **Global-glue obstruction search.**  Attempt to realize the Wave 58 local
   child-sector templates simultaneously as restrictions of one global
   signing and one parent state.  Either obtain a scalable obstruction or
   isolate an inconsistent overlap equation that can seed a positive
   Johnson-reuse theorem.

5. **Row-regularized active-face minimax.**  Add a row or relative-degree
   penalty to the Wave 56 fractional minimax before rounding.  Determine
   whether exact signing minimality bounds the new game value at the project
   scale, or whether convexity controls only the mean field and not captured
   row.

6. **Matrix-valued edge attenuation.**  Replace the scalar positive block by
   an attenuation whose coefficients are chosen from the high-field vector.
   Seek a simultaneous-flip certificate controlling the full masked degree
   `b`, so the migrated state satisfies the exact transfer premise
   `||b||^2=O(R_*)`.

7. **Low-row ground-face law.**  Treat parent ground sign patterns as exposed
   subgradients of the signing norm.  Ask whether coordinatewise exact
   minimality supplies a distribution on the ground face with
   `E R=O(R_*)`, perhaps through wedge correlations; construct a finite dual
   certificate if false.

8. **Bare-fibre hub dichotomy.**  Quantify how a family of density
   `e^{-O(H)}` can shield all row-descending coordinates.  Prove that either
   a random outside block sees enough row excess or the family is controlled
   by `O(H)` hubs, then use exact child/parent energy on those hubs rather
   than treating the junta case abstractly.

9. **Full-histogram consistency energy.**  Express the weighted Johnson
   histogram as a sum of squared global edge-restriction correlations of the
   child-ground map.  Search for a nonnegative term forced by one global
   signing and exact minimality that is absent from the local random-label
   model.

10. **Global block Harnack at the correct scalar.**  Test an orbit-average
    inequality for `e^h+gamma R`, not `h+lambda R`, over `k`-vertex switches.
    Use exact double counting rather than monotone one-spin paths, and audit
    it on stored minimizers before proposing an asymptotic form.

Selected independent assignments: 1 together with 2, 8, and 10; 3 together
with 4 and 9; and 5 together with 6 and 7.  The main agent will independently
derive the fibre-weighted outside-block row identity and audit whether its
PSD remainder has a usable sign.
