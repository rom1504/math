# Wave 44 idea ranking

Evidence basis: README, ledger Updated frontier through Wave 43, and the
Wave-44-boundary STEERING refresh including its blank-slate audit. This is
scratch planning, not a ledger claim.

1. **Direct complement incidence scalarization.** Study
   min_d{-log U_m(I_d)+lambda R_2(d)} from (10.1149). Derive exact
   vertex/block-flip Euler inequalities and use global signing minimality to
   prove a project-scale upper bound or produce a scalable obstruction.
   This is now the shortest honest implementation of the leader.
2. **Incidence-versus-row bipartite expansion.** Regard selectors and full
   cuts as a bipartite graph. Combine the exact all-selector cover with the
   Hanson--Wright rarity of high-row cuts and a signing-specific switching or
   compression inequality preventing every incidence neighborhood from being
   trapped in that rare set. This is testable independently of consensus.
3. **High-replica common-center pressure.** Expand
   E_z q(z)^r at r=Theta(n/L_0) as an exact multi-selector/common-center
   partition and seek a minimizer-specific overlap theorem that yields
   (10.1152). This preserves rare exceptional centers and avoids the failed
   first center moment.
4. **Replica center Euler/mismatch compression.** Add an affordable row price
   to the high-replica center objective, derive all block-flip inequalities,
   and bound the number or weighted mass of simultaneous mismatch patterns.
   This is a concrete successor to (10.995)--(10.1000).
5. **Intrinsic harmonic load self-bounding.** Derive deletion/flip increments
   of L_s=sum_i k_i(s) and seek a signing-specific variance inequality
   directly implying J_L^2=O(n^(1/2-2c)). It is baseline-free and exactly
   targets the Wave 43 bootstrap.
6. **Losing-context variation theorem.** Analyze the positive variation of
   context masses in J_Phi: use the log-sum-exp/tropical line envelope to
   charge repeated backtracking to endpoint entropy or energy gaps, while
   retaining the Wave-36 transient wall as a mandatory test.
7. **Approximate complement incidence with slack.** Enlarge I_d by a
   project-scale response slack and prove that low-row approximate columns
   have affordable mass; quantify exactly how the slack enters favorability.
   This may evade threshold congestion but risks reverting to the bare tail.
8. **Rare-coverage contrapositive plus optimality.** Assume every low-row cut
   has too little favorable mass, use the sharp upper-tail converse to build
   a selector law, and turn its separating functional into an edge/block
   modification lowering the parent norm.
9. **Favorable-distance min-plus composition.** Seek a genuine triangle or
   exchange inequality for distances from a fixed cut to selector favorable
   fibers, with a project-scale average bound. It remains independent but has
   no current estimate.
10. **Growing-order SoS diagnostic.** Compute whether increasing hierarchy
    levels distinguish finite minimizers from planted zero-entropy extrema.
    This can falsify the blank-slate hierarchy mechanism but presently has no
    asymptotic bridge, so it is not assigned.

Selected assignments: items 1--2 as one complement-column project; items
3--4 as one high-replica pressure project; and items 5--6 as one integrated
harmonic project. Items 7--9 are ordered replacements if a selected route
closes.
