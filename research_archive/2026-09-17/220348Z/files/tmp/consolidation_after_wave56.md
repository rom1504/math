### 10.112 Consolidation checkpoint after Wave 56

This is a retrospective classification of the major work in Waves 40--56,
written after the later Wave 57--58 refinements but before any further
ordinary wave.  Those refinements do not change the central accounting: the
rigorous interval is unchanged, no restriction recurrence has been completed
uniformly for exact minimizers, and no genuine nonconvergence mechanism is
known.  The just-started Wave 59 was stopped before completion and contributes
no ledger result.

The classification below applies the strict rule that a cleaner interface is
not mathematical progress unless a verified implication removes an obligation
without replacing it by one of equal strength.  Route falsification is useful
evidence but is not a convergence or nonconvergence result.

#### 10.112.1 Category 1: verified reductions of actual proof obligations

The following results genuinely remove a previously stated sub-obligation.
None by itself improves the rigorous interval or completes a recurrence step.

1. **Conflict and decoding are redundant once information and row are
   controlled (Wave 43).**  The posterior-support extraction specialized in
   (10.1148) shows that a low-information, low-row favourable joint law
   already produces one good column.  Colored conflict, plurality decoding,
   Gram transport, and shared-priority consistency were removed rather than
   replaced.  The surviving incidence-and-row input was later reformulated as
   the selected-prior lemma.

2. **Non-strict coarea and then saved second-eigenvalue excess suffice
   (Waves 48 and 51).**  The slice FKN consequence (10.1222) removed the need
   for a strict first-eigenvalue margin in the non-strict coarea regime.
   More sharply, (10.1261) proves that saved
   `P_ell-lambda_2(ell)` already extracts a saved-degree centre.  This removes
   the stronger conditions `P>=lambda_1`, constant FKN curvature, and
   independent-resampling repair.  It does **not** provide either project-row
   box mass or the required overlap excess.

3. **Exact minimality supplies migration against a hidden child-field spike
   (Wave 55).**  The attenuated replacement theorem (10.1321)--(10.1323) and
   its child-field application (10.1326) replace the former purely generic
   spike obstruction by an actual subproject-deficit parent response.  The
   remaining obligation is to couple those responses to favourable child
   fibres and project row; no such coupling is proved.

4. **One common active-face law replaces one witness per block (Wave 56).**
   The minimax and rounding theorem (10.1340)--(10.1343) constructs a single
   selector-independent law on an `o(T_n)` parent face which escapes a
   constant fraction of every canonical child excess block.  This is the
   strongest positive exact-minimality theorem in the period: it removes the
   separate-witness/multiplicity obligation.  It controls neither signed bare
   favourability nor captured row, so it is not a completed component of the
   restriction recurrence.

These are strict reductions of route obligations, but their conjunction has
not been shown to make the original bare-tail problem easier.  In particular,
the two unproved properties after item 4 are exactly the two quantitative
clauses of the selected-prior target.

#### 10.112.2 Category 2: route falsifiers and scalable obstructions

The following results close implementations or generic implications.  They do
not show that `M_n/n^(3/2)` fails to converge.

1. **Canonical complement and local harmonic shortcuts (Waves 40--44).**
   Cyclic monotonicity and quantized Johnson gaps have no usable Hamming
   modulus; summing four-cycles adds no curvature.  Generic endpoint cost,
   componentwise convexity, unweighted centre moments, pointwise
   fixed-density reveal bounds, local Poincare/Efron--Stein, and monotone
   deletion all fail in the recorded scopes.  The strict-pressure inverse
   theorem (10.1162)--(10.1163) shows that a fixed positive pressure premise
   already contains a stronger restriction edge, retiring strict pressure and
   high replicas as independent mechanisms.

2. **Complement abundance and Harnack implementations (Waves 45--48).**
   Average complement incidence has the unsaved exponent; the central
   half-cube/geometric Harnack proposal is impossible; fixed-strength slack is
   impossible near the diagonal; and, decisively, (10.1216) proves that every
   fixed-density project-row complement column has mass
   `e^{-Omega(n^(3/4))}`.  This retires the entire fixed-density row-good
   complement-column implementation, not the bare arbitrary-cut tail.

3. **Generic block pressure and rounding (Waves 47--50).**  The exact
   antipodal block-game dual defeats one-block proofs of tight principal
   decomposition; the full-slack fractional point has an integral gap; an
   optimal same-cap box need not carry a pure positive-core coarea scale; and
   canonical Bellman pressure is exactly a convex mixture of norm-preserving
   switchings.  Thus fractional replacement, one-block anti-migration, and
   switching-orbit pressure cannot furnish the missing recurrence.

4. **Moment and far-completion implementations (Waves 51--55).**  The
   two-moment and cubic envelopes are confined to a recurrence-strength local
   band.  Uniform arbitrary-cut completion localizes by
   (10.1317)--(10.1318), and the proposed far K-profile population is
   `e^{-omega(H)}` by (10.1320).  These results retire the uniform-completion,
   low-moment, clipped-head, and far-K abundance detours; they leave the fixed
   selected-cut tail untouched.

5. **Generic box and cluster implementations (Waves 52--55).**  A
   sublinear-radius exchange ball supplies too few labels; generic cap,
   operator, trace, and child-ground data allow hidden `A^2` spikes; a
   scalable competitive non-minimizer hides all proposed cancellation; and a
   one-threshold linear-core cluster violates Johnson-ball capacity except on
   a tuned surface, where the sharp square-root shortfall still defeats it.
   Nonnegative kernel mixtures cannot beat their best component.  These are
   scoped implementation walls, not exact-minimizer nonconvergence examples.

6. **Marginal common-witness logic (Wave 56, sharpened later).**  A matching
   can give every selector an active witness while every supported joint law
   has information cost `Theta(n)`, as in (10.1357).  Common marginal escape
   therefore cannot substitute for a saved low-information collision.

#### 10.112.3 Category 3: equivalent or conditional sufficient reformulations

These results are exact and often useful for diagnosis, but they do not by
themselves reduce the difficulty of the original problem.

1. **Soft terminal and pressure languages (Waves 40--44).**  Favourable
   distance is an exact flip-compatible terminal potential, but its dynamic
   program is the old favourable forest/scaffold problem.  Mixed pressure,
   colored consensus, common-core pressure, hard-column scalarization, and
   phase-aware harmonic endpoint domination are sufficient interfaces whose
   missing minimizer-specific alignment or transport is at least as strong as
   the obligation they replace.

2. **Arithmetic, coarea, and decomposition packages (Waves 45--50).**  The
   arithmetic switching theorem, normalized common-core boundary, port-regret
   decomposition, Bellman profile composition, tight principal decomposition,
   partial-completion box discrepancy, triple retention, and all-state block
   duality are exact.  Each still requires an unproved mass, correlation,
   overlap, or cross-block consistency theorem.  None supplies a recurrence
   edge on its own.

3. **Annealed bare incidence (Waves 49--55).**  The identity
   (10.1228)--(10.1230) shows that saved total uniform incidence makes row
   automatic.  This initially looks like a scalar reduction, but
   (10.1317)--(10.1318) proves that any such saved uniform-completion mass
   already contains recurrence-strength local-band mass.  It is therefore a
   logically valid but not demonstrably easier formulation.

4. **K-functional and orientation packages (Waves 52--55).**  The
   dependence-safe K theorem, exact clipped K dual, orientation pairing, and
   canonical-head formulas correctly characterize sufficient far-tail
   events.  Their required saved population is either recurrence-local or
   uniformly too small, so they are diagnostics rather than a reduced proof
   problem.

5. **Box plus spectral excess (Waves 48--56).**  Project-row box mass together
   with saved full-histogram excess implies a selected cut by (10.1261).  The
   core-load calculus, direct-versus-amplified scale boundary, and exact
   threshold-retention identities specify what overlap would suffice.  They
   do not prove the box or signing-specific overlap and hence split one hard
   tail into two unproved bridges.

6. **Mesoscopic phase diagram (Wave 56).**  The count-feasible scale
   `ell~sqrt(nH)` and the one-threshold window
   `sqrt(nH) lesssim ell lesssim n^(5/6)` for `c>1/12` correct earlier scale
   choices.  Capacity is necessary, not a construction.  No Johnson-correlated
   reuse theorem follows.

7. **Selected-prior extraction (Wave 56).**  Equations
   (10.1353)--(10.1356) and the KL collision identity give the cleanest exact
   statement of the goal: saved favourable incidence, captured row, and
   captured deficit under one prior imply one deterministic bare-tail cut.
   This removes bookkeeping, but obtaining that joint law is not known to be
   easier than proving the bare tail directly.

#### 10.112.4 Category 4: finite or numerical evidence

Finite evidence is retained for mechanism testing but makes no asymptotic
claim.

1. Stored exact minimizers `A_5,A_6,A_8,A_9,A_10` supply the recorded flat
   normal cones, empty or nonretained switching neighbours, harmonic phase
   bottlenecks, nearest-core failures, box cancellation examples, migration
   witnesses, and pointwise common-witness incompatibilities.  They falsify
   universal local implications only in their stated scopes.

2. Tight principal decomposition and related ground-profile composition have
   exhaustive support only through small orders (with additional order-ten
   samples).  This is evidence for a conjecture, not a uniform theorem, and
   even that conjecture would leave several harmonic bridges.

3. The order-thirteen affine puncture example and several scalable algebraic
   constructions are not exact minimizers at competitive asymptotic scale.
   They refute proofs using only their listed generic hypotheses, not the
   desired exact-minimizer statements.

4. Finite common-core, K-profile, threshold, and completion-CDF tables are
   useful falsification diagnostics.  Self-loops, fixed constants, and small
   codimension dominate several of them, so none is evidence for saved
   asymptotic mass or for separated subsequences of normalized `M_n`.

#### 10.112.5 Complete implication chains for the live lemmas

Put

~~~math
H=\lceil n^{3/4-c}\rceil,\qquad
T_n=n^{3/2-c},\qquad R_*=n^{9/4-c}.
~~~

The already verified final step, common to every convergence route below, is

~~~text
uniform fixed-density recurrence
q_m <= p^(3/2) q_n + O(T_n)
    -> geometric-window summability and exact landing
    -> convergence of q_n/n^(3/2)
    -> convergence of M_n/n^(3/2).
~~~

The live antecedents are as follows.

1. **Selected-prior lemma (cleanest target).**

   ~~~text
   one selector-independent prior with
     favourable mass >= e^{-O(H)},
     favourable-captured row O(R_*),
     and captured deficit O(T_n)
       -> selected-prior extraction (10.1353)--(10.1356)
       -> one cut with saved bare fibre, project row, and near-ground deficit
       -> bare-tail theorem (10.795)
       -> uniform recurrence
       -> convergence.
   ~~~

   Additional bridges: prove all three captured properties on the same event.
   The common active face supplies only the deficit and block escape.  The
   saved bare-favourability and project-row clauses remain wholly open.
   **Assessment:** this is the cleanest formulation but is not demonstrably
   simpler than the original bare-tail problem; it is essentially that tail
   written as a joint-law collision.

2. **Common-active-face route.**

   ~~~text
   exact minimality
       -> common near-ground law and block escape (10.1340)--(10.1343)
       -> [missing] saved signed cancellation giving bare favourability
       -> [missing] project-row control on the same incidences
       -> selected-prior lemma
       -> bare tail -> recurrence -> convergence.
   ~~~

   Additional bridges: a joint sector-cancellation theorem, captured row, and
   compatibility of both with the common law.  Marginal block balance cannot
   prove them.  **Assessment:** the proved common-law step is real, but its
   successor package is not shown weaker than the bare tail and may simply
   restate it.

3. **Project-row box plus full-histogram spectral excess.**

   ~~~text
   [missing] nonzero favourable box mass at row O(R_*)
       + [missing] some nontrivial core with
         P_ell-lambda_2(ell) >= e^{-O(H)}
       -> spectral-excess extraction (10.1261)
       -> one saved-degree low-row centre
       -> bare tail -> recurrence -> convergence.
   ~~~

   Additional bridges: construct the box, prove signing-specific overlap at
   a controlled cap, and preserve deficit/tolerance.  At one mesoscopic
   threshold the overlap bridge is exactly the Johnson-retention target
   (10.1364); the full histogram still needs the analogous same-witness
   excess.  **Assessment:** capacity calculations show only non-impossibility.
   Two major unproved clauses replace one tail, so this is not demonstrably
   simpler.

4. **Integrated harmonic route (dormant).**

   ~~~text
   [missing] phase-aware endpoint domination (10.1167)
       + [missing] restoring and orientation control
       + [missing] adjacent-selector transport and endpoint mass
       -> harmonic entropy criterion
       -> favourable low-information incidence
       -> bare tail -> recurrence -> convergence.
   ~~~

   Tight principal decomposition, if proved, addresses only part of endpoint
   geometry.  Weighted crossing fan-in and zero-slope phases remain.
   **Assessment:** the number and strength of independent bridges give no
   evidence that this is simpler than the bare tail.

5. **Genuine nonconvergence alternative.**

   ~~~text
   fixed epsilon > 0 and two infinite subsequences with normalized values
   separated by epsilon
       -> liminf M_n/n^(3/2) < limsup M_n/n^(3/2)
       -> genuine nonconvergence.
   ~~~

   Additional bridge: an asymptotic construction or theorem controlling the
   actual optima `M_n` on two infinite order classes.  Residue-class finite
   data and failures of complement, coarea, pressure, or spectral lemmas do
   not enter this chain.  **Assessment:** no current ledger result supplies
   leverage toward the required separated subsequences.

#### 10.112.6 Consolidation decision before diagnostics

Continuous wave generation is paused.  The selected-prior lemma remains the
cleanest convergence target, but present evidence does not establish it as a
strict reduction of the original bare-tail problem.  The project will run one
bounded diagnostic campaign: an adversarial equivalence audit, a blank-slate
convergence attack, and a genuine nonconvergence attack.  Each is limited to
90 minutes with 15-minute checks and no replacement or extension.  Their
reports will be synthesized into one committed recommendation before any
further research.  If ordinary waves later resume, Wave 61 remains the next
scheduled blank-slate steering boundary.
