## Ten candidate attacks, ranked

1. **Internal Gram from certificate slack.**  Starting with the exact
   completion identity (10.1078) and cap persistence (10.1079), seek a
   deterministic or averaged inequality charging
   `||A[S]y^S||_2^2` to certificate deficit `Delta`, favorable conflict, or
   an exact parent cut row.  This attacks precisely the missing half of
   (10.1080) and inherits the verified external bound.
2. **Integrated harmonic approximate order.**  Expand the total local KL
   load `L_t` in conditional log-likelihood increments and try to control the
   integrated negative covariance with `g` directly, possibly by a Bregman or
   mutual-information identity, rather than proving pointwise monotonicity.
   This targets (10.1083)--(10.1085) with all known cancellations retained.
3. **Terminal-min-cut compression from exact minimality.**  Use the path
   packing dual (10.923)--(10.924) and exchange/cut minimality to charge
   coordinate-tagged opposite-terminal paths collectively, allowing
   different coordinates to reuse tree edges.  The target is total separator
   cost `O(k_0)` on an event of mass `exp{-O(rL_0)}` without Johnson entry
   costs.  This is genuinely independent of Gram and Gibbs interpolation.
4. **Joint Lagrangian selection for Gram.**  Optimize favorable child lifts
   simultaneously under `Delta + lambda ||A[S]y||^2` and derive a minimax or
   averaging theorem that supplies affordability, conflict, cap persistence,
   and internal row in one law.  Test whether the surviving `q_n-q_m` term
   cancels only after summing the joint family.
5. **Harmonic time integration by parts.**  Rewrite
   `int Cov_{mu_t}(g,L_t) dt` as endpoint mutual information plus a signed
   curvature integral.  Seek convexity only after summing coordinates and
   time, avoiding the finite failure of coordinatewise signs.
6. **Correlated complement congestion.**  Form the fractional-cover LP for
   favorable complement flips and use the all-subset slack corridor (10.1074)
   in its dual.  Aim directly for `log tau_flip=O(n^(3/4-c))`; do not use a
   uniform first moment or independent product noise.
7. **Cap-threshold layer cake.**  Average near-parent certificates over their
   exact surplus `Gamma_S(d)` and search for a threshold where both cap
   persistence and internal Gram hold.  This explicitly keeps the surplus
   that the finite A8 zero-slack wall shows cannot be discarded.
8. **Direct nonlinear rare tail over low-row cuts.**  Exponentially tilt the
   low-row cut ensemble by favorable deficit, retain the full nonlinear
   log-partition function, and seek a minimizer-specific lower-tail bound for
   (10.795).  Any proof must beat the rare-coverage converse and generic
   quadratic-concentration wall.
9. **Laminar slack charging for terminal paths.**  Laminarize minimum
   separators within each coordinate, sum the induced shores, and charge
   their boundary multiplicities to the exact flipped-ground slack corridor.
   This is a more structured successor if direct path charging fails.
10. **Cavity susceptibility cutset.**  Revisit (10.817)--(10.819) only at the
    path level: look for a deletion choice forced by an averaged variance
    trace, or construct an unavoidable low-susceptibility cutset.  It is
    exact and falsifiable, but ranked last because no square-root mechanism
    has appeared.
