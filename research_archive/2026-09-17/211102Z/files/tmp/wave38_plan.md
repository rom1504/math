# Wave 38 ranked ideas

Evidence basis: README, STEERING through Wave 34, and the Wave 37 Updated
frontier in ledger §10.90.5.  The three selected subagent attacks are marked
S1--S3.  The main agent independently pursues M.

1. **Dual weighted complement-flip compression (S1).**  Start from the exact
   dual of `tau_flip`.  Sum the complement-flip minimality inequalities
   against a putative large dual law and derive a row-sensitive contradiction
   or an exact obstruction.  The target is a project-scale bound on
   `log tau_flip`, not another pointwise witness statement.
2. **Favorable-label full-column Gram energy (S2).**  Use the parent completion
   polynomial, its two orientations, Parseval/level-one energy, and affordable
   thinning to prove or falsify (10.1057).  Keep favorable support explicit;
   unrestricted deletion witnesses are invalid after the A9 wall.
3. **Matched harmonic combined covariance (S3).**  Work with the exact split
   (10.1066), but seek a theorem for the combined time-integrated movement or
   flux (10.1065).  Test boundary/telescoping, entropy-production, and
   minimizer-specific external-partition structure; do not bound the two
   large pieces separately.
4. **Nonlinear row-penalized flip game (M).**  Respect the linear circularity
   wall (10.821)--(10.824): regularize the hard incidence
   `1_{2c_S(d)-E_d>=q_n}` or a one-sided hinge, rather than its signed mean,
   against `R_2(d)`.  Derive the exact minimax/dual form and test whether
   simultaneous minimality controls the nonlinear price, or prove a new
   quantitative wall.
5. **Near-one-ratio star expansion.**  Write complement flips for
   `m=n-r` as flips supported on the omitted vertices and their boundary;
   combine single-edge local minimality inequalities to control the row of a
   certificate when `r` is at the landing scale.
6. **Fractional-cover Gibbs regularization.**  Put a Gibbs law on states with
   energy `lambda 1_(S in I_d)-theta R_2(d)` and seek a PAC-Bayes or entropy
   inequality converting average selector coverage into low-row congestion.
7. **Gram geometry of complement certificates.**  Translate (10.1044) into
   inner products of signed columns and apply restricted-invertibility or
   discrepancy geometry to a selector-weighted family; audit all exponent
   losses before importing any theorem.
8. **Multiblock replacement cancellation.**  Replace two complementary or
   overlapping blocks so the two copies of `q_n-q_m` telescope before width
   is charged.  Require favorable labels and an exact migration accounting.
9. **Dynamic flux transport inequality.**  Try to control (10.1064) by the
   dynamic lifted KL in (10.1062), perhaps through a one-dimensional
   log-Sobolev/transport inequality integrated over contexts; orientation and
   restoring remain separate.
10. **Exact finite dual-pattern discovery.**  Rationally certify the small
    `tau_flip` LPs and inspect optimal primal/dual supports versus row square,
    energy defect, and selector intersections to conjecture the missing
    asymptotic inequality.  Treat this as mechanism discovery, not evidence
    for an exponent.

Selection rationale: ideas 1--3 directly attack the three clean open
interfaces from Wave 37 and are mutually independent.  The main attack 4
shares the complement-flip object with idea 1 but uses a different variational
form, allowing independent falsification.  Ideas 5--10 are successors if a
selected route hits a precise wall.
